This market will resolve to "Yes" if the total number of pentakills recorded across all official games of the 2026 League of Legends World Championship (Worlds 2026), currently scheduled for October 15 to November 14, 2026, is greater than or equal to the number specified in the market title. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

A pentakill is a single player killing all five players on the opposing team in quick succession, as announced by the game client ("PENTAKILL") and recorded in the official game statistics. Every completed official game of the tournament counts (Play-In, Swiss Stage, and Knockout Stage). Pentakills in games that are remade, abandoned, or not completed do not count.

If Worlds 2026 is canceled or postponed such that it has not concluded by December 14, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve according to the number of pentakills recorded in completed official games as of that time.

The primary resolution source for this market will be the tournament statistics on Leaguepedia (https://lol.fandom.com/wiki/2026_Season_World_Championship); however, official information from Riot Games (https://lolesports.com) and a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
