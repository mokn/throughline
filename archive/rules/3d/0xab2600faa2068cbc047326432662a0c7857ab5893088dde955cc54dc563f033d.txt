This market will resolve to “Yes” if a qualifying military action impacts the Crimean Bridge between market creation and the specified date, 11:59 PM Moscow Time (MSK). Otherwise this market will resolve to “No.”

The Crimean Bridge (Kerch Bridge) refers to the bridge across the Kerch Strait connecting the Taman Peninsula with the Kerch Peninsula, including its road and rail sections, spans, arches, and supporting piers.

A qualifying military action refers to an air strike, a surface-to-surface missile strike, or a naval drone strike that impacts the Crimean Bridge. An air strike includes bombs, air-to-surface missiles, and air-launched drones. A surface-to-surface missile strike includes one-way attack drones and surface-to-surface missiles such as cruise or ballistic missiles. A naval drone strike includes uncrewed surface or underwater vessels carrying explosives.

The following actions do not constitute a qualifying military action: 
Munitions destroyed or intercepted before impact;
Surface-to-air missile strikes;
Small-arms fire;
Ground incursions;
Cyber operations;
Naval gunfire and artillery fire;
Howitzers, artillery pieces, mortars, and rocket artillery (e.g. MLRS systems);
Minor surface-to-surface strikes, including short range loitering munitions, FPV drones, and ATGM strikes;
Any threat, authorization, or announcement of force that has not been executed.

Any munition that is intercepted or destroyed before impact does not constitute a qualifying military action. Debris, fragments, or any wreckage from intercepted munitions that land on the Crimean Bridge do not constitute a military action regardless of any damage incurred.

A military action will be considered to impact the Crimean Bridge if it directly impacts any part of the bridge structure, including its road and rail sections, spans, arches, or supporting piers. Impacts on land-based approaches, nearby ferry crossings, or air defense positions around the bridge do not qualify.

The occurrence and timing of a qualifying military action will be primarily determined based on a consensus of information available from the resolution sources. No single statement, denial, or presentation of evidence will govern where it is contradicted by the totality of information.

Unless a qualifying military action has already been established by a consensus of information available from the resolution sources, this market will remain open until 11:59 PM MSK on the third calendar date following the specified date. If multiple sources conflict regarding the occurrence or timing of a potentially-qualifying incident at 11:59 PM MSK on the third calendar date following the specified date, the incident will be adjudicated based on the totality of information available from the resolution sources at that time.

The resolution sources for this market will be official information from the governments and militaries of Ukraine and Russia and a consensus of credible reporting.
