This market will resolve to “Yes” if the United States Department of Justice formally charges, files a criminal complaint against, or otherwise announces a criminal indictment of Megaspeed International Pte. Ltd. (“Megaspeed”) or any of its subsidiaries or successors between market creation and the specified date, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to “No”.

A federal criminal complaint, information, or indictment naming Megaspeed or any of its subsidiaries or successors as a defendant will qualify. Charges filed under seal will qualify only once they are unsealed or publicly announced by the US federal government within this market's timeframe, regardless of when they were originally filed.

Charges under any federal criminal statute will qualify (for example, export-control or sanctions violations, smuggling, or fraud). Civil enforcement actions will not qualify.

A qualifying charge need not result in a conviction. This market will resolve to “Yes” even if the charge is later dismissed, reduced, or resolved through a deferred prosecution agreement, so long as it was publicly filed, unsealed, or announced by the specified date. Charges solely against individuals, including Alice Huang or other current or former Megaspeed executives, charges against any other entity, investigations, subpoenas, sanctions designations, Entity List additions, and administrative export-control penalties will not qualify.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from US governmental sources, however a wide consensus of credible reporting will also be used.
