This market will resolve to “Yes” if the United States Social Security Administration is unable to fully pay benefits due to lack of solvency by the specified date at 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to “No”.

For the purposes of this market, Social Security will be considered to have a lack of solvency if its Trust Funds' combined asset reserves are exhausted and benefit payments must be reduced or eliminated. A temporary lack of payments related to the debt ceiling limit will not qualify. 

A projected depletion date alone will not qualify; actual depletion must occur. 

The primary resolution source will be official publications from the Social Security Administration or the White House, however a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
