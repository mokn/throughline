This market will resolve according to the number of Triple Crowns won by Ben Johns during the 2026-27 Carvana PPA Tour season, including the 2027 PPA Tour Finals.

A "Triple Crown" is achieved when a player wins the singles, gender doubles, and mixed doubles titles at the same PPA Tour event. Each Triple Crown counts once. Titles won at events that are not part of the 2026-27 PPA Tour season do not count toward this market.

Each option covers exactly one total, and the highest option covers that total or more, such that every possible number of Triple Crowns, including zero, maps to exactly one option.

If the 2026-27 PPA Tour season is cancelled, or the number of Triple Crowns won by Ben Johns cannot be determined by May 16, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve according to the number of Triple Crowns won by Ben Johns up to that point.

The primary resolution source will be official information from the Professional Pickleball Association (https://ppatour.com/); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
