This market will resolve according to the division of the team that wins the 2027 Stanley Cup Final.

If at any point it becomes impossible for a listed division to produce the 2027 Stanley Cup champion (e.g., all of its teams have been eliminated from the 2027 Stanley Cup Playoffs), the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

If the 2027 Stanley Cup Final is cancelled, postponed after June 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or a champion has not been declared within that timeframe, each listed division's market will resolve to "No" and the "Other" market will resolve to "Yes".

The primary resolution source will be official information from the NHL (https://www.nhl.com/); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
