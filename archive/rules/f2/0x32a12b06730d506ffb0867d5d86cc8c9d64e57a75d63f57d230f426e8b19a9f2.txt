This market will resolve to "Yes" if Team Spirit officially announces the signing of a competitive Deadlock roster between market creation and December 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

For the purposes of this market, a "Deadlock roster" is a team of at least six players that is announced to compete in Deadlock under the Team Spirit name or a Team Spirit-branded name (e.g., "Spirit Academy"). The signing of individual content creators or streamers, a roster made of fewer than six players, or a partnership with a team that does not compete under a Team Spirit-branded name will not qualify.

An official announcement of the signing within this market's timeframe will resolve this market to "Yes", regardless of when the roster first competes.

For the purposes of this market, "Team Spirit" refers to the esports organization that fields Team Spirit's Dota 2 and Counter-Strike rosters (https://liquipedia.net/dota2/Team_Spirit).

The primary resolution source for this market will be official announcements from Team Spirit; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
