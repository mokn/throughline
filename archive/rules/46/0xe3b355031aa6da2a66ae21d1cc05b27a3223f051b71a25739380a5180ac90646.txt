This market will resolve according to whether the first individual who is officially announced as the successor to Jensen Huang as CEO of NVIDIA Corporation by December 31, 2028, 11:59 PM ET is an internal or external candidate.

A candidate is internal if, at the time of the announcement, they are an employee of NVIDIA Corporation or any of its subsidiaries, or a member of its Board of Directors. Any other candidate is external.

If co-CEOs are announced, this market will resolve to "External" if any of them is an external candidate.

An announcement of a successor will be sufficient for resolution regardless of whether the announced replacement actually becomes the next CEO of NVIDIA.

Interim CEOs will not qualify.

If, before any successor is announced, NVIDIA officially announces that Jensen Huang will return as CEO on a non-interim basis, or if no successor is announced by December 31, 2028, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "No new permanent CEO".

The resolution source will be public statements from NVIDIA Corporation, however a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
