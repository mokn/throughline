This market will resolve according to the length of the national anthem performance at Super Bowl LXI. 

The length will be measured from the start of when the primary singer begins audibly singing until the moment they finish singing the word “brave,” for the last time no matter how long they hold that note.

Music and background vocals will not be considered.

If the reported value falls exactly between two brackets, then this market will resolve to the higher range bracket.

If Super Bowl LXI is cancelled, postponed after February 28, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there is no national anthem performance within that timeframe, this market will resolve to the lowest bracket.

The primary resolution source for this market will be the official broadcast of Super Bowl LXI; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
