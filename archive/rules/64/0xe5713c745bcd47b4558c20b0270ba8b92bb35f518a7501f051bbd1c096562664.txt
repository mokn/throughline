This market will resolve to the athlete who wins the 2026 BBC Sports Personality of the Year award.

If the listed player is not announced as a finalist for the BBC Sports Personality of the Year, this market will resolve to "No".

In case of a tie for the winner, this market will resolve in favor of the winner whose listed last name comes first in alphabetical order.

If the 2026 BBC Sports Personality of the Year award is cancelled, postponed after January 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there is otherwise no winner declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the BBC; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
