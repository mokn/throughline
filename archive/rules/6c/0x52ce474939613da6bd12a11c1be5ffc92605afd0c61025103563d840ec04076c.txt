This market will resolve to "Yes" if any biological man is drafted in the 2027 WNBA Draft. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

For the purpose of this market, 'biological man' refers to an athlete assigned male at birth who identifies as female. This definition excludes intersex athletes (including those with differences in sex development) whose variations in sex characteristics do not involve a gender identity different from their assigned sex at birth, unless the athlete publicly identifies as transgender and this is widely and credibly confirmed.

If the 2027 WNBA Draft is cancelled, postponed after May 1, 2027, or the results of the draft cannot be confirmed in this timeframe, this market will resolve to "50-50".

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from the WNBA, or announcements from specific WNBA teams, however a consensus of credible reporting may be used.
