This market will resolve to "Yes" if UEFA imposes a sanction on Manchester City FC concerning the club's compliance with financial rules by May 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

A sanction includes a fine, exclusion from UEFA competitions, a restriction on registering players for UEFA competitions, or a points deduction in a UEFA competition, imposed by any UEFA body or agreed under a settlement agreement with the Club Financial Control Body. Sanctions arising from the Premier League's proceedings against Manchester City FC announced on February 6, 2023 (commonly referred to as the "115 charges" case) will count. A sanction will count once it is officially announced, regardless of whether it is later appealed, reduced, or overturned. A sanction that is entirely suspended or conditional, warnings or reprimands alone, and sanctions for unrelated matters will not count.

The resolution source for this market will be official information from UEFA or Manchester City FC; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
