This market will resolve according to the total number of tokens processed on OpenRouter for the four weeks of October 26 to November 22, 2026.

The total number of tokens is the sum of the weekly totals displayed in the Top Models chart on OpenRouter's AI Model Rankings page (https://openrouter.ai/rankings#top-models) for the weeks of October 26, November 2, November 9 and November 16, 2026. OpenRouter's weeks run from 12:00 AM UTC on Monday to 11:59 PM UTC on Sunday, and a week's figures continue to update until the following week's figures are first displayed.

This market will resolve according to the totals displayed for those four weeks once the figures for the week of November 23, 2026 are first displayed. If the figures for the week of November 23, 2026 have not been displayed by 11:59 PM ET on November 27, 2026, this market will resolve according to the totals displayed for those four weeks at that time.

This market will resolve based on the totals as displayed on the chart, including any rounding applied by OpenRouter. If the total falls exactly on the boundary between two brackets, this market will resolve to the higher bracket.

The resolution source for this market is https://openrouter.ai/rankings.
