This market resolves to the next individual officially announced as a member of the Politburo Standing Committee (“中央政治局常委”) of the Communist Party of China after market creation. Individuals who are members of the Politburo Standing Committee at the time of this market's creation will not count.

If more than one new member is announced on the same calendar date (Beijing time), for example in the communiqué of the First Plenary Session of the 21st Central Committee, the market will resolve to the new member ranked highest in the official order of the Politburo Standing Committee, as listed by Xinhua or another official Chinese state source. If no official order is given, the market will resolve to the individual announced first by an official source; if that cannot be determined, the market will resolve to the individual whose surname comes first alphabetically in pinyin.

If no new member of the Politburo Standing Committee (“中央政治局常委”) of the Communist Party of China is announced by December 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to “None by December 31, 2027”.

The primary resolution sources will be official Chinese state announcements (such as Xinhua bulletins, People’s Daily readouts, etc.) However a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
