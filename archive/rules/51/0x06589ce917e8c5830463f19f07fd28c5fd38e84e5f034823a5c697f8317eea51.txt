On November 17, 2026, NVIDIA Corporation announced that its Board of Directors established the National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee (the "Committee"), a standing committee responsible for board-level oversight of the company's national security policies, export control compliance and global trade risk management. NVIDIA stated that the Committee will retain independent, outside experts.

This market will resolve to "Yes" if, between market creation and March 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, the listed individual is officially announced as either:
(a) appointed or elected to NVIDIA's Board of Directors and named a member of the Committee, or
(b) retained, appointed, or engaged by NVIDIA, its Board of Directors, or the Committee as an outside expert or advisor to the Committee.

Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Appointment or election to NVIDIA's Board of Directors without being named a member of the Committee will not qualify.

An individual nominated to NVIDIA's Board of Directors will qualify if NVIDIA announces within this market's timeframe that the individual will serve on the Committee, even if the individual has not yet been elected by shareholders.

Announcements of an individual's appointment or engagement that will take effect after March 31, 2027 will qualify, so long as the announcement is made within this market's timeframe.

If the Committee is renamed, or its responsibilities are transferred to a successor committee or advisory body, membership in or engagement by that successor will qualify.

Engagement of a firm, law firm, or consultancy that employs or is affiliated with the listed individual will not qualify unless NVIDIA or a consensus of credible reporting specifically identifies the listed individual as providing the services.

The primary resolution sources for this market will be official information from NVIDIA, including its press releases and filings with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
