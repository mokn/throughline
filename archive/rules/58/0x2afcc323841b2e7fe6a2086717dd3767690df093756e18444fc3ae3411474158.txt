This market will resolve to "Yes" if Helvijs "broky" Saukants is officially announced as a member of the active Counter-Strike 2 roster of any professional team by December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

For the purposes of this market, joining a team means an official announcement by the team, by broky, or by his representatives that he has been signed to, loaned to, or has otherwise become part of, that team's active playing roster. A return to the active roster of FaZe, where broky is currently benched, will also qualify. Stand-in or temporary substitute appearances for a single event or match, benched, inactive, or reserve listings, and coaching, analyst, management, or content-creator roles will not qualify. Joining a team whose Counter-Strike 2 roster is not covered by HLTV will not qualify. Reports, rumors, or leaks that are not confirmed by an official announcement will not qualify on their own.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official announcements from the acquiring team and/or broky; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used. HLTV's listing of broky's team status (https://www.hltv.org/player/18053/broky) may be used as a supporting reference.
