Each market in this group will resolve to "Yes" if the Grand Final of the 2026 League of Legends World Championship (Worlds 2026), currently scheduled for November 14, 2026, in New York City, ends with the listed series score, regardless of which team wins. Otherwise, it will resolve to "No". The Grand Final is a best-of-five series.

"3-0" means the winning team wins three games without losing a game, "3-1" means the winning team wins three games and loses one game, and "3-2" means the winning team wins three games and loses two games.

Only completed, officially recorded games count. A game that is remade or restarted before completion counts once, as the completed game. If any game of the Grand Final is awarded by forfeit or administrative decision without being played to completion, every market in this group will resolve to "No".

If the Grand Final has not been completed by December 14, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or the tournament is canceled or postponed past that date, every market in this group will resolve to "No".

The resolution source for this market group will be official information from the tournament organizer, Riot Games, via the LoL Esports website (https://lolesports.com). However, a consensus of credible reporting (e.g., Liquipedia at https://liquipedia.net/leagueoflegends/World_Championship/2026) may also be used.
