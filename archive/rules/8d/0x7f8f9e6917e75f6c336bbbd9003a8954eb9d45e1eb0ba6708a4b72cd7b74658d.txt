This market will resolve to "Yes" if Logan Paul is a recognized holder of any WWE championship at any point from market creation to December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

For the purposes of this market, "holding a championship" means Logan Paul is officially recognized by WWE as a champion or co-champion of any championship sanctioned by WWE, including but not limited to world championships, midcard championships (e.g., United States or Intercontinental), tag team championships (whether held with a partner or under any shared-holder rule), and any specialty or newly created championships officially recognized by WWE. Holding a championship for any duration from market creation through December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET.

The following do not count: unsanctioned, fictional, or unofficial titles not recognized by WWE's official championship history; the Money in the Bank contract or briefcase; tournament wins, trophies, or accolades that are not championships; championships from non-WWE promotions; and merely competing in or being announced for a championship match without being recognized as champion. If WWE retroactively vacates, strips, or voids a reign, resolution will be based on whether WWE recognized Paul as champion at any point during the window, as reflected in its official championship history.

The primary resolution source for this market will be WWE's official championship history (https://www.wwe.com). A consensus of credible reporting may also be used where the primary source is incomplete or in conflict.
