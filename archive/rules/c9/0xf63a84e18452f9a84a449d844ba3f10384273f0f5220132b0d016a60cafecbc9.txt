This market will resolve to "Yes" if, in any single game of the BLAST Slam IX Playoffs (the online double-elimination Playoffs stage), currently scheduled for November 26 to November 29, 2026, the same team is credited with killing a Tormentor two or more times. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

A Tormentor kill is credited to the team whose hero lands the killing blow on the Tormentor, as recorded in the game's objectives log. Each Tormentor death counts as a separate kill, regardless of which side of the map that Tormentor spawned on. Both kills must be made by the same team within the same game. Only completed official games of the Playoffs stage count (Upper Bracket, Lower Bracket, and Grand Final). Games from the Group Stage, Seeding Matches, and Last Chance stage do not count. Games that are remade or not completed, and games awarded by forfeit without being played, do not count.

This market may resolve to "Yes" as soon as a qualifying game is confirmed.

If the BLAST Slam IX Playoffs are canceled, or are postponed such that they have not concluded by December 29, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "No", unless a qualifying game has already occurred.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official match data from OpenDota (https://www.opendota.com) for the games of the BLAST Slam IX Playoffs; however, Dotabuff (https://www.dotabuff.com), Datdota (https://www.datdota.com), official broadcast footage, and a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
