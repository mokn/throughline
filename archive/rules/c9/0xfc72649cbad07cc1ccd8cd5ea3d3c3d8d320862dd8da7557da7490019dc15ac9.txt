The PGL Major Singapore 2026 is scheduled to take place from November 24 to December 13, 2026.

This market will resolve according to which teams qualify for the PGL Major Singapore 2026. If the listed team officially qualifies as one of the teams invited to the PGL Major Singapore 2026, the market will resolve to "Yes". Otherwise, it will resolve to "No".

Qualification to any stage of the Major (Stage 1, Stage 2, or Stage 3) will count. Invitations are expected to be issued based on the Valve Regional Standings (VRS); a team's projected or current VRS ranking alone will not count until the official invited team list is published. Ties in standings will be broken according to the official PGL / Valve rules.

If the PGL Major Singapore 2026 is canceled, postponed indefinitely, or if the official list of PGL Major Singapore 2026 participants is not published by January 12, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official information from PGL (https://www.pglesports.com/cs2/pgl-major-singapore-2026/). However, a consensus of credible reporting (e.g., Liquipedia at https://liquipedia.net/counterstrike/PGL/2026/Singapore) may also be used.
