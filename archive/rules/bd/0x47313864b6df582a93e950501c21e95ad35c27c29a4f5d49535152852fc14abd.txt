This market will resolve to "Yes" if, in any game of the BLAST Slam IX Playoffs (the online double-elimination Playoffs stage), currently scheduled for November 26 to November 29, 2026, a team wins the game after trailing the opposing team by 10,000 or more net worth (gold) at any point during that game. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

A team's deficit is measured using the per-minute team net worth advantage data for the game (the net worth advantage graph). A game qualifies if the eventual winner's net worth deficit is 10,000 or greater at any recorded minute. Only values at recorded minute marks count; a deficit occurring between minute marks does not count. If the listed sources report different values, the OpenDota per-minute data will be used. Only completed official games of the Playoffs stage count (Upper Bracket, Lower Bracket, and Grand Final). Games from the Group Stage, Seeding Matches, and Last Chance stage do not count. Games that are remade or not completed, and games awarded by forfeit without being played, do not count.

This market may resolve to "Yes" as soon as a qualifying game is confirmed.

If the BLAST Slam IX Playoffs are canceled, or are postponed such that they have not concluded by December 29, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "No", unless a qualifying game has already occurred.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official match data from OpenDota (https://www.opendota.com) for the games of the BLAST Slam IX Playoffs; however, Dotabuff (https://www.dotabuff.com), Datdota (https://www.datdota.com), official broadcast footage, and a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
