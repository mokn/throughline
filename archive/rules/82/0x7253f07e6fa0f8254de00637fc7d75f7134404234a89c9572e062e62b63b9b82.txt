This market will resolve in favor of the team that finishes the 2026-27 NHL regular season with the fewest points.

In the event of a tie, this market will resolve in favor of the team with fewer regulation wins during the 2026-27 NHL regular season. If a tie still persists, this market will resolve in favor of the team with fewer regulation plus overtime wins. If a tie still persists, this market will resolve to "Other".

If the 2026-27 NHL season concludes early, is shortened, or is truncated for any reason, the outcome shall be determined using available NHL statistics for completed games.

If the 2026-27 NHL regular season is cancelled, postponed after April 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or it is unclear which team finished with the fewest points within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the NHL; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
