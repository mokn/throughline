This market will resolve to the player who scores the most goals through all rounds of the 2026-27 UEFA Nations League competition.

Goals scored in any 2026-27 UEFA Nations League match count, across Leagues A, B, C and D, including the league phase, the promotion/relegation play-offs, the quarter-finals and the Nations League Finals. Goals scored in any other competition will not count.

In the event of a tie, this market will resolve according to the official leader as determined by UEFA rules. If multiple leaders are announced then this market will resolve to the player who scored fewer goals from penalty kicks. If a tie still persists, this market will resolve to the player whose listed last name comes first alphabetically.

If the 2026-27 UEFA Nations League is cancelled, postponed after June 27, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there is no leader declared within that timeframe, the corresponding market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from UEFA; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
