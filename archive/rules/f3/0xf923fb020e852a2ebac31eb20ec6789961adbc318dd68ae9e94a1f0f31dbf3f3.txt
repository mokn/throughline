Dancing with the Stars is a reality dance competition in which celebrities are paired with professional dancers to perform dance routines. This market will resolve to the celebrity participant of the winning team of Dancing with the Stars: Season 35.

If multiple winners are announced, this market will resolve in favor of the listed contestant whose name comes first in alphabetical order.

If Dancing with the Stars: Season 35 concludes without a winner being declared, or if Dancing with the Stars: Season 35 has otherwise not concluded by March 31, 2027, 11:59PM ET this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source for this market will be the official broadcast of the final episode of Dancing with the Stars: Season 35.
