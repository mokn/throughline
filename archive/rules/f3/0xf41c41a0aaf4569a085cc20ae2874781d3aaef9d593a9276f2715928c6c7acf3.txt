This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed individual attends the 2027 Pro Football Championship, currently scheduled for February 14, 2027. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

If the event is canceled or postponed beyond March 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "No".

For the purpose of this market, attending the Pro Football Championship is defined as being in physical attendance during any part of the event.

The resolution source will be a consensus of credible reporting.
