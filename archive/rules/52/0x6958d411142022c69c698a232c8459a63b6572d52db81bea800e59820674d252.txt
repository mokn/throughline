The 2027 Australian Open is scheduled to take place from January 10 - 31, 2027.

This market will resolve to the player that wins the 2027 Australian Open Women’s Singles Tournament.

If at any point it becomes impossible for a listed player to win the 2027 Australian Open Women’s Singles Tournament per the rules of the tournament, the corresponding market will resolve to “No”.

If the 2027 Australian Open Women’s Singles Tournament is cancelled, postponed after February 28, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there is otherwise no winner declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to “Other”.

The primary resolution source will be official information from the Australian Open (https://ausopen.com/); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
