This market will resolve according to the person appointed as the next permanent head coach of the United States Men's National Soccer Team (USMNT).

If no permanent head coach is appointed by March 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, the market will resolve to "Other."

Appointments of 'interim,' 'caretaker,' or other non-permanent head coaches will not impact this market's resolution.

An announcement of a new permanent head coach's appointment before this market's close date will immediately resolve this market to the corresponding option, regardless of when the announced appointment goes into effect.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from U.S. Soccer (the United States Soccer Federation); however, a consensus of credible reporting will also be used.
