This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team achieves promotion from the Primera Nacional (Argentina) to the Liga Profesional (Argentina) for the 2027 season at the conclusion of the current league season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Official promotion is defined as a team clinching a place in the Liga Profesional (Argentina) for the 2027 season through their final standing in the Primera Nacional (Argentina), via playoff victory, or by any other official league decision recognized by the Primera Nacional (Argentina) and Liga Profesional (Argentina).

If the 2026 Primera Nacional (Argentina) season is cancelled, postponed after December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or there are no teams confirmed for promotion within that timeframe, the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from the Primera Nacional (Argentina) and Liga Profesional (Argentina). A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
