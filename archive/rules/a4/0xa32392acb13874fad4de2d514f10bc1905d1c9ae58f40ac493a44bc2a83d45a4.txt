This market will resolve "Yes" if at least one map ends with a final score of 13–0 in any official match at the PGL Major Singapore 2026, scheduled for November 24 to December 13, 2026. Otherwise, this market will resolve "No."

Every completed map in every official match of the event counts, across Stage 1, Stage 2, Stage 3, and the Playoffs. A 13–0 result on any single map by either team resolves this market "Yes," regardless of which stage, match, or team it occurs in. Maps played in qualifiers held before Stage 1 do not count. Only maps that start after this market was created count.

Only completed maps count. A map that is not completed (e.g., a technical remake, or a forfeit before a 13–0 is reached) does not count toward a "Yes." A map awarded by forfeit or administrative decision does not count, even if it is recorded as 13–0.

If a qualifying 13–0 map is completed before the PGL Major Singapore 2026 is cancelled or abandoned, this market will resolve "Yes." If the event is cancelled, or is not completed such that its results are not determined by January 12, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, and no qualifying 13–0 map has been completed, this market will resolve "No."

The resolution source for this market will be official information from HLTV (https://www.hltv.org); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
