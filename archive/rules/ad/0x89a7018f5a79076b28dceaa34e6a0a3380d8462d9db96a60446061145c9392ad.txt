On November 12, 2026, NVIDIA announced that Jensen Huang had resigned as its President, Chief Executive Officer, and a member of its Board of Directors, effective immediately.

This market will resolve to "Yes" if Donald Trump publicly calls for Jensen Huang's return as CEO of NVIDIA between market creation and the specified date, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

A qualifying call is a statement by Trump that clearly says Jensen Huang should be reinstated, brought back, or returned as CEO of NVIDIA, or that Trump wants Huang back as CEO (e.g., "NVIDIA should bring Jensen back" or "I want Jensen back as CEO"). Exact wording is not required, and a call directed at NVIDIA, its board of directors, or Huang himself will qualify. Huang does not need to actually return as CEO for this market to resolve to "Yes".

Conditional, hypothetical, or speculative remarks (e.g., "I'd like to see him back," "maybe they'll bring him back," or a statement that Huang should return only if some condition is met) will not qualify. Praise of Huang, or criticism of his departure or of NVIDIA's board, will not qualify on its own.

Any written, verbal, or recorded public statement made by Trump himself qualifies, including posts on Trump's official Truth Social or X accounts, interviews, speeches, and remarks to reporters. Statements attributed to Trump by others, such as White House officials or anonymous sources, will not qualify. Reposts or shares without additional commentary from Trump will not qualify.

The primary resolution source for this market will be video or official transcripts of Trump's statements and his official social media accounts; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
