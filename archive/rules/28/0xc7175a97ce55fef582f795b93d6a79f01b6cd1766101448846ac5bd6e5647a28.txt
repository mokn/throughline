This market will resolve to "Yes" if Jordan Spieth wins the 2027 PGA Championship, scheduled for May 20-23, 2027, completing the career Grand Slam. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

If the 2027 PGA Championship is cancelled, or is not completed by December 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "No".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the PGA of America (https://www.pgachampionship.com/); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
