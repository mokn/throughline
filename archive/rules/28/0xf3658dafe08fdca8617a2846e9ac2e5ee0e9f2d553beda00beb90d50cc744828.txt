This market will resolve to "Yes" if the Miami Heat reach the Knockout Rounds of the 2026 NBA Cup. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Eight teams reach the Knockout Rounds: the six group winners and the second-place team from each conference with the best group-play record, as determined by the NBA under the 2026 NBA Cup rules and tiebreakers.

If at any point it becomes impossible for the Miami Heat to reach the Knockout Rounds of the 2026 NBA Cup (e.g. they are mathematically eliminated), this market will resolve to "No".

If the 2026 NBA Cup is cancelled, postponed after December 11, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or the teams reaching the Knockout Rounds have not been determined within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "No".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the NBA; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
