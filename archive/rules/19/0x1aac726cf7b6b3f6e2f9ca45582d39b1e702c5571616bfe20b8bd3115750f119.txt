This market will resolve to "Yes" if an Iranian diplomat is expelled from any country by the government of that country between market creation and the listed date, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

For the purposes of this market, “expel” refers to a formal action by a country requiring the Iranian diplomat to leave that country, including a declaration of persona non grata. Revocation of the diplomat’s visa or denial of entry to the relevant country will not alone suffice.

An announcement of a qualifying expulsion will suffice for a “Yes” resolution, regardless of whether the diplomat actually leaves the country.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from the governments of the expelling country and Iran; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
