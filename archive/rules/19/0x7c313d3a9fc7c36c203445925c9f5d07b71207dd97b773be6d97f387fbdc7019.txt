This market will resolve to the player who scores the most goals during the regular season of the 2027 Liga MX Clausura competition.

Only goals scored in Liga MX Clausura regular season matches will count. Goals scored in other competitions (e.g., Campeon de Campeones, CONCACAF Champions Cup, Leagues Cup, international matches) will not count for this market.

If multiple players tie for the top goal scorer, this market will resolve to the player whose last name comes first alphabetically.

If the 2027 Liga MX Clausura regular season is canceled or not completed by June 27, 2027, the market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source will be official statistics from Liga MX. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
