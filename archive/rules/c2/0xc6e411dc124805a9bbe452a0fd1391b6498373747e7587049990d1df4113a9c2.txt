This market will resolve to "Yes" if, between market creation and December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, a qualifying organization officially announces that it is disbanding its Dota 2 team or leaving Dota 2. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

For the purposes of this market, a "qualifying organization" is an esports organization that fields an active Dota 2 roster at market creation and whose Dota 2 team has total prize money earnings above $1,000,000, as listed on its Liquipedia team page (https://liquipedia.net/dota2) at market creation.

An official announcement that all players have been released from a team's active roster also qualifies, unless the same announcement states that the organization will field a new Dota 2 roster. A roster that only moves to inactive status, the release of some but not all players, a rebrand, or the sale of a roster to another organization that keeps playing will not qualify.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official announcements from the organizations; however, a consensus of credible reporting, including Liquipedia, may also be used.
