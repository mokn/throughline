This market will resolve according to the player who records the most AWP kills across all maps played at the PGL Major Singapore 2026 (Stage 1, Stage 2, Stage 3, and the Playoffs), currently scheduled for November 24 to December 13, 2026.

AWP kills will be counted from HLTV's official statistics for every map played at the tournament, as shown on HLTV's statistics page for this event at 11:59 PM ET on the day after the Grand Final is played. Statistics revisions made after that time will not be considered. Maps awarded by forfeit or administrative decision do not count.

If two or more players, listed or unlisted, are tied for the most AWP kills, this market will resolve in favor of the tied player whose name comes first in case-insensitive alphabetical order, with digits ordered before letters. If that player is unlisted, this market will resolve to "Other".

If an unlisted player records the most AWP kills, this market will resolve to "Other".

If the PGL Major Singapore 2026 is canceled, postponed after January 12, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or the AWP kill totals cannot be determined within this timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source for this market will be HLTV (https://www.hltv.org/events/8302/pgl-major-singapore-2026); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
