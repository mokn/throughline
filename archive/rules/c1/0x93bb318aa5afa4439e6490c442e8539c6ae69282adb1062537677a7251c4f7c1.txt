This market will resolve according to the winner of the 2026 American Conference Football Championship, determining the champion of the American Conference for the 2026 college football season, currently scheduled for December 5, 2026.

If this championship is postponed after December 19, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, canceled, or a champion has not been declared within this timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other."

If multiple teams are declared co-champions, this market will resolve in favor of the team whose listed name comes first alphabetically.

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the American Conference (https://theamerican.org); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
