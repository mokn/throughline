This market will resolve according to the team that wins the 2027 AFC Asian Cup.

If at any point it becomes impossible for a listed team to win the 2027 AFC Asian Cup per the rules of the AFC (e.g., they are eliminated), the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

If the 2027 AFC Asian Cup is cancelled, postponed after February 19, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there is otherwise no winner declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source will be official information from the AFC; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
