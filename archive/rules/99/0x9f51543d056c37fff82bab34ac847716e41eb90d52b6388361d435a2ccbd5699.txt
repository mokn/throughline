This market will resolve according to the team that wins the 2026 MLS Eastern Conference (the Eastern Conference club that advances to MLS Cup 2026 by winning the Eastern Conference Final).

If at any point it becomes impossible for a listed team to win the 2026 MLS Eastern Conference per the rules of MLS (e.g., they are eliminated from the Eastern Conference playoffs), the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

If the 2026 MLS season is cancelled, postponed after December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or there is otherwise no Eastern Conference champion declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source will be official information from Major League Soccer; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
