This market will resolve to "Yes" if a team with Hunter "inspire" Schline listed on its official roster as a player clinches a spot in VCT 2027: Americas Kickoff through the VCT 2027 Americas open qualification path (the Open Qualifiers, the qualifier Playoffs, or the Last Chance Qualifier) per Riot Games rules. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Only teams that qualify through the open qualification path count; invited partner teams do not. inspire must be on the qualifying team's official roster at the time the team clinches its spot. If inspire changes teams, only the team inspire is rostered on at the time qualification is decided will be considered.

If at any point it becomes impossible for a team with inspire on its roster to clinch a spot in VCT 2027: Americas Kickoff (e.g. the team is eliminated from the qualification path or does not enter it), this market will resolve to "No".

If the VCT 2027 Americas open qualification path is canceled, postponed after January 17, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or the teams that qualified for VCT 2027: Americas Kickoff have not been officially confirmed by Riot Games within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "No".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from Riot Games (via valorantesports.com); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
