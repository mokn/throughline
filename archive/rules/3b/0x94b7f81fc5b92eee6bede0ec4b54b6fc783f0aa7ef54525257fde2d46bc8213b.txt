This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team finishes Stage 1 of the PGL Major Singapore 2026 with a 3-0 match record, meaning it wins each of its first three Stage 1 matches. The PGL Major Singapore 2026 is currently scheduled for November 24 to December 13, 2026, with Stage 1 scheduled for November 24 to November 27, 2026. Match records count series results, not individual maps: each Stage 1 match counts once, whether it is played as a Bo1 or a Bo3. Forfeits and other administrative results count as officially recorded by the tournament organizer.

Teams are identified by organization. A team competing at this tournament under an alternative name required by local regulations (e.g. GOSH for BC.Game, THE ONE for 1win, BB Team for BetBoom, PVISION for PARIVISION) counts as the listed team.

If the PGL Major Singapore 2026 is canceled, postponed after January 12, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or the Stage 1 results have not been officially confirmed within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "No".

The resolution source for this market will be HLTV (https://www.hltv.org/events/8302/pgl-major-singapore-2026); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
