This market will resolve according to Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez's path in the 2028 U.S. presidential election. Exactly one of the following outcomes will resolve to "Yes"; all others will resolve to "No":

- "Doesn't announce Dem run before 2028": Ocasio-Cortez does not make a qualifying announcement that she is running for the Democratic nomination for President of the United States by December 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET. If this occurs, this market will resolve to "Doesn't announce Dem run before 2028" regardless of any later events.
- "Runs and loses Dem nomination": Ocasio-Cortez makes a qualifying announcement by that deadline but does not win the 2028 Democratic presidential nomination. If this occurs, this market will resolve to "Runs and loses Dem nomination" regardless of any later events.
- "Wins Dem nomination and loses general": Ocasio-Cortez makes a qualifying announcement by that deadline, wins the 2028 Democratic presidential nomination, and does not win the 2028 presidential election.
- "Wins presidency": Ocasio-Cortez makes a qualifying announcement by that deadline, wins the 2028 Democratic presidential nomination, and wins the 2028 presidential election.

A qualifying announcement is an official announcement by Ocasio-Cortez that she is running for the Democratic nomination for President of the United States in the 2028 presidential election. An announcement will qualify regardless of whether Ocasio-Cortez has filed, or later files, any paperwork to run. The announcement must be explicit and unambiguous; conditional statements, or statements that are clearly sarcastic, made in jest, or immediately clarified as not being genuine, will not count. Announcements that Ocasio-Cortez is exploring or considering a run, including the formation of an exploratory committee, will not count. An announcement of a run as an independent or as the candidate of any party other than the Democratic Party will not count.

Ocasio-Cortez will be considered to have won the 2028 Democratic presidential nomination if she wins and accepts the 2028 nomination of the Democratic Party for President of the United States. Any replacement of the Democratic nominee after Ocasio-Cortez has won and accepted the nomination will not change this. If another individual wins and accepts the 2028 Democratic nomination, Ocasio-Cortez will be considered not to have won it, even if she later replaces that individual as the nominee.

Ocasio-Cortez will be considered to have won the 2028 presidential election once the Associated Press, Fox News, and NBC have all called the race for her. If all three sources have not called the race for the same candidate by the inauguration date (January 20, 2029), the winner will be determined by who is inaugurated.

The resolution source for whether Ocasio-Cortez makes a qualifying announcement will be official statements by Ocasio-Cortez (e.g., via speech or social media); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used. The resolution source for the Democratic nomination will be a consensus of official Democratic Party sources. The resolution source for the presidential election will be the Associated Press, Fox News, and NBC, as described above.
