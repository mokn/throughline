This market will resolve in favor of the team that finishes the 2026-27 NFL regular season with the most special teams and defensive touchdowns (kickoff return, punt return, blocked kick, interception return, and fumble return touchdowns).

In the event of a tie, this market will resolve in favor of the tied team with more total takeaways (interceptions plus fumble recoveries) during the 2026-27 NFL regular season. If a tie still persists, this market will resolve in favor of the team with more combined kickoff and punt return yards during the 2026-27 NFL regular season. If a tie still persists, this market will resolve to “Other”.

If the 2026-27 NFL season concludes early, is shortened, or is truncated for any reason, the outcome shall be determined using available NFL statistics for completed games.

If the 2026-27 NFL regular season is cancelled, postponed after January 26, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or it is unclear which team led the NFL in special teams and defensive touchdowns (kickoff return, punt return, blocked kick, interception return, and fumble return touchdowns) within that timeframe, this market will resolve to “Other”.

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the NFL; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
