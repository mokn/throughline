This market will resolve to "Yes" if the named country finishes Eurovision 2027 as one of the top 5 highest ranked candidates. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Countries will be ranked according to their official competition ranking in the Grand Final. If two or more countries share the same ranking, ties will be broken by the total number of points received. If still tied, ties will be broken by the total number of points received from the national juries. If still tied, ties will be broken by the total number of points received from the public vote. If still tied, ties will be broken alphabetically by English country name. This market will resolve according to the countries ranked in the top 5 under this ranking.

If at any point it is impossible for the listed candidate to finish as one of the top 5 highest ranked based on the rules of the competition (i.e. they are eliminated), this market may immediately resolve to "No".

If the results of the Grand Final are not announced by July 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from Eurovision (https://www.eurovision.com/), including live footage of Eurovision 2027, however a consensus of credible reporting will suffice.
