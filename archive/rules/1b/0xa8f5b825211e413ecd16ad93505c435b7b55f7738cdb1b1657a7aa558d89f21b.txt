This market will resolve to “Yes” if the team that wins the opening coin toss at Super Bowl LXI also wins Super Bowl LXI. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

This market will resolve to “No” if the team that wins the opening coin toss does not win Super Bowl LXI.

The winner of the opening coin toss at Super Bowl LXI will be the team that gets to choose whether to receive, defer, or choose a goal to defend as a result of the opening coin toss.

If Super Bowl LXI is cancelled, postponed after February 28, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, there is no coin toss outcome determined within that timeframe, or no winner declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to 50-50.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from the NFL (including the official broadcast of Super Bowl LXI); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
