This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team records the listed number of points or more during the 2026-27 Bundesliga season. Otherwise, the associated market will resolve to "No".

The listed team's points total will be its total in the official 2026-27 Bundesliga standings once all of the season's matches have been completed, including any points deductions reflected in those standings at that time, whether or not they are under appeal. Points deductions or adjustments made after that point will not be considered. Only Bundesliga league matches count toward the total; the relegation play-off will not be considered.

If at any point it becomes impossible for the listed team to record the listed number of points or more during the 2026-27 Bundesliga season (e.g. they are mathematically eliminated), the associated market will resolve to "No".

If the 2026-27 Bundesliga season is cancelled, postponed after June 5, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or it cannot be determined whether the listed team has recorded the listed number of points or more within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official information from the Bundesliga; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
