This market will resolve “Yes” if the listed team reaches the NFC Championship Game of the 2027 NFL Playoffs. 

If at any point it becomes impossible for the listed team to advance to the NFC Championship Game of the 2027 NFL Playoffs (e.g. they are mathematically eliminated), the associated market will resolve to "No".

If the 2027 NFL Playoffs are cancelled, postponed after February 28, 2027, 11:59 PM ET or the NFC Championship Game matchups have not been declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to “No”.

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the NFL (https://www.nfl.com/); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
