This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team is selected as a number 1 seed in the 2027 NCAA Division I Men's Basketball Tournament. Otherwise, it will resolve to "No".

The tournament field and seeding are scheduled to be announced on Selection Sunday, March 14, 2027.

If the 2027 NCAA Division I Men's Basketball Tournament is cancelled, or the number 1 seeds have not been announced by March 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from the NCAA (https://www.ncaa.com); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
