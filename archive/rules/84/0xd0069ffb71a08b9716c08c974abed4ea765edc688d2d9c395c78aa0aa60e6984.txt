This market will resolve to "Yes" if, in any round of any map of the PGL Major Singapore 2026 Playoffs, currently scheduled for December 10-13, 2026, a player becomes the last surviving member of their team while all five opponents are alive, personally kills all five opponents, and their team wins the round. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Only rounds played during the Playoffs count. Rounds played during Stage 1, Stage 2, or Stage 3 do not count.

If the PGL Major Singapore 2026 is canceled, or postponed after January 12, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "No".

The resolution source for this market will be HLTV (https://www.hltv.org/events/8302/pgl-major-singapore-2026) and the official PGL broadcast; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
