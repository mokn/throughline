This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team achieves promotion from the Brazil Série B to the Brazil Série A for the 2027 season at the conclusion of the current league season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Official promotion is defined as a team clinching a place in the Brazil Série A for the 2027 season through their final standing in the Brazil Série B, via playoff victory, or by any other official league decision recognized by the Brazil Série B and Brazil Série A.

If the 2026 Brazil Série B season is cancelled, postponed after December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or there are no teams confirmed for promotion within that timeframe, the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from the Brazil Série B and Brazil Série A. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
