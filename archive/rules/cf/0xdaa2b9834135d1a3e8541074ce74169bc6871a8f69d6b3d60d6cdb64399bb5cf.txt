This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team is relegated from Veikkausliiga (Finland) at the conclusion of the 2026 Veikkausliiga (Finland) season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Official relegation is defined as a team losing its place in Veikkausliiga (Finland) for the following season through their final standing in the 2026 Veikkausliiga (Finland), via relegation playoff defeat, or by any other official league decision recognized by Veikkausliiga (Finland).

If the 2026 Veikkausliiga (Finland) season is cancelled, postponed after December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or there are no teams confirmed for relegation within that timeframe, the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from Veikkausliiga (Finland). A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
