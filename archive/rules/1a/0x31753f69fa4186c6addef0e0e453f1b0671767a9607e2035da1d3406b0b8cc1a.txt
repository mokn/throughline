This market will resolve to “Yes” if a member of the Politburo of the Chinese Communist Party (CCP) is purged between market creation and the listed date, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to “No”.

For the purposes of this market, a Politburo member will be considered purged if any of the following conditions are met:

1) The individual is removed or resigns from their primary political post or from their position on the Chinese Politburo or its standing committee, with a consensus of credible reporting describing the resignation/removal as a “purge”, “ousting”, or similar language, or to have presumably or definitively occurred as a result of corruption, bribery, other criminal wrongdoing, or a lack of political favor.

2) The individual is expelled from the Chinese Communist Party (CCP).

3) The individual is officially announced to be under investigation, detained, or otherwise subject to disciplinary or criminal proceedings by any Chinese Communist Party, Chinese state, or Chinese military body, including but not limited to the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection (CCDI), the National Supervisory Commission (NSC), the Central Military Commission (CMC) Commission for Discipline Inspection, the Supreme People’s Procuratorate, or the Ministry of Public Security.

Only individuals who are members of the Chinese Politburo at the time of the qualifying purge will count. Any individual officially announced to be under investigation, detained, or removed before this market's creation, including Chen Jining (announced November 24, 2026), will not count toward the resolution of this market, nor will any subsequent removal, resignation, or expulsion of that individual.

The qualifying announcement and, where required, the qualifying consensus of credible reporting must both occur by the listed date, 11:59 PM ET. Once they have, this market will immediately resolve to "Yes", regardless of when the announced resignation/removal/expulsion/investigation goes into effect.

A Politburo member leaving office at the end of a regularly scheduled term, or being removed/resigning from office without a consensus of credible reporting describing the resignation/removal as a “purge”, “ousting”, or similar language, or to have presumably or definitively occurred as a result of corruption, bribery, other criminal wrongdoing, or a lack of political favor, will not qualify.

The resolution sources for this market will be information from the Chinese Government and a consensus of credible reporting.
