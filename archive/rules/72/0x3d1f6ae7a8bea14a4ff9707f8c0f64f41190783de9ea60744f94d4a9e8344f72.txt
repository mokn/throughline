This market will resolve according to the U.S. exchange on which OpenAI's shares are listed in its initial public offering (IPO).

The "Pricing Prospectus" is the first final prospectus for the IPO filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) on EDGAR under Rule 424(b) under the Securities Act of 1933. Later prospectuses or supplements do not replace it.

OpenAI's IPO counts only if all of the following have occurred by December 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET: (i) the SEC has declared OpenAI's registration statement for the offering effective; (ii) a U.S. national securities exchange registered with the SEC has approved OpenAI's common equity securities for listing; (iii) the offering has been priced, as shown by the Pricing Prospectus; and (iv) the shares sold in the IPO have first traded on a U.S. national securities exchange registered with the SEC. Steps (ii) and (iv) only establish that the IPO occurred; the listing exchange for this market is determined solely as set out below. A registration statement declared effective or a listing approval without pricing, a preliminary prospectus, or an offering priced without a first trade by that time does not count.

Only a traditional IPO qualifies: a priced, underwritten offering of newly issued common equity securities of OpenAI, or American depositary shares representing them, sold to the public for cash under a registration statement on Form S-1 or Form F-1, as the first public offering and listing of OpenAI's common equity on any exchange. Selling stockholders may also sell shares in the offering. An IPO by a parent or holding company formed to hold all or substantially all of OpenAI's business counts; an IPO by a subsidiary or separated business does not. Direct listings, SPAC mergers, reverse mergers, private placements and offerings limited to institutional or accredited investors do not count.

The listing exchange is the exchange named as the listing exchange on the cover of the Pricing Prospectus. If the Pricing Prospectus and the exchange's official listing notice disagree, the Pricing Prospectus governs. If the cover names more than one exchange, the exchange whose official name comes first in alphabetical order governs. If the cover names no exchange, the exchange's official listing notice governs. Trading on other exchanges under unlisted trading privileges, statements of intention to list, and any transfer of the listing after the first trade are not considered.

"Nasdaq" is the Nasdaq Stock Market, including the Nasdaq Global Select Market, Nasdaq Global Market and Nasdaq Capital Market. "NYSE" is the New York Stock Exchange only. "Another U.S. exchange" is any other U.S. national securities exchange registered with the SEC, such as NYSE American, NYSE Texas, NYSE Arca, Cboe BZX Exchange, the Texas Stock Exchange or the Long-Term Stock Exchange. A listing only on an exchange outside the United States is not a qualifying IPO.

If OpenAI's shares first trade by December 31, 2027 but the Pricing Prospectus has not been published on EDGAR by then, this market will wait for it until 11:59 PM ET on the second business day after December 31, 2027.

If no qualifying IPO occurs by December 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or if before then OpenAI is acquired by or merged into another entity (other than a holding company formed for its IPO), its shares become publicly traded other than through a qualifying IPO, or it otherwise permanently ceases to be able to complete an IPO, this market will resolve to "No IPO before 2028".

The primary resolution source will be the Pricing Prospectus and related SEC filings on EDGAR, together with the listing exchange's official notice of the listing and record of the first trade. A consensus of credible reporting may be used only if those sources have not published the relevant information within two business days after the event is otherwise public.
