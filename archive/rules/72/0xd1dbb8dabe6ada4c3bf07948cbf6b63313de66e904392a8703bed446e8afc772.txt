This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team is relegated from Chance Liga (Czechia) at the conclusion of the 2026-27 Chance Liga (Czechia) season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Official relegation is defined as a team losing its place in Chance Liga (Czechia) for the following season through their final standing in the 2026-27 Chance Liga (Czechia), via relegation playoff defeat, or by any other official league decision recognized by Chance Liga (Czechia).

If the 2026-27 Chance Liga (Czechia) season is cancelled, postponed after June 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there are no teams confirmed for relegation within that timeframe, the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from Chance Liga (Czechia). A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
