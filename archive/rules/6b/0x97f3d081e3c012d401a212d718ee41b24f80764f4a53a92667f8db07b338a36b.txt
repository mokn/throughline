This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team finishes 14th in the 2026 Chile Primera. Otherwise, the associated market will resolve to "No".

If at any point it becomes impossible for the listed team to finish 14th in the 2026 Chile Primera, the associated market will resolve to "No".

If the 2026 Chile Primera season is cancelled, postponed after December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or there is otherwise no 14th place team declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from Chile Primera; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
