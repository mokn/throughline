This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed club finishes in the top 4 (positions 1 through 4) of the final standings of the 2026-27 Ligue 1 season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

If at any point it becomes impossible for the listed club to finish in the top 4 of the 2026-27 Ligue 1 season (e.g., they are mathematically unable to achieve enough points), this market will resolve to "No".

In the event of a tie for the final top-4 position, this market will resolve based on the clubs officially recognized by the Ligue de Football Professionnel (LFP) as finishing in the top 4 of the final standings.

If the 2026-27 Ligue 1 season is cancelled, postponed after June 12, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there are otherwise no final standings declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official information from the Ligue de Football Professionnel (LFP); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
