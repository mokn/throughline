This market will resolve according to the division of the team that wins the 2027 NFL league championship game, which concludes the 2026 NFL season.

If at any point it becomes impossible for a listed division to produce the 2027 NFL league champion (e.g., all of its teams have been eliminated from the 2026 NFL playoffs), the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

If the 2027 NFL league championship game is cancelled, postponed after March 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or a champion has not been declared within that timeframe, each listed division's market will resolve to "No" and the "Other" market will resolve to "Yes".

The primary resolution source will be official information from the NFL (https://www.nfl.com/); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
