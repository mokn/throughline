This market will resolve according to the team that wins the 2027 FIFA Women's World Cup.

If at any point it becomes impossible for a listed team to win the 2027 FIFA Women's World Cup per the rules of FIFA (e.g., they are eliminated), the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

If the 2027 FIFA Women's World Cup is cancelled, postponed after August 8, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there is otherwise no winner declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source will be official information from FIFA; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
