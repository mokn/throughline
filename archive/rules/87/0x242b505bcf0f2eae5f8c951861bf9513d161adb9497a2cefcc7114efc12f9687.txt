This market will resolve to "Yes" if Jalen Green is traded to another NBA team between October 9, 2026 and the 2027 NBA trade deadline. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

For the purpose of this market, the 2027 NBA trade deadline is February 11, 2027, 3:00 PM ET, or any revised date and time the NBA officially announces, but no later than February 25, 2027, 11:59 PM ET. A trade counts once it has been officially processed and approved by the NBA. A trade that is later rescinded or voided for any reason after being officially processed will still count. A player who is waived, released, bought out, retires, or otherwise leaves his team by any means other than a trade will not count as traded.

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the NBA and the teams involved; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
