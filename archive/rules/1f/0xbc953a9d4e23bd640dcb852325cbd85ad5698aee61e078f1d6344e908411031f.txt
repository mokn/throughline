This market will resolve “Yes” if the listed team reaches the 2027 UEFA Europa League semifinal. 

If at any point it becomes impossible for the listed club to advance to the UEFA Europa League semifinal (e.g. they are mathematically eliminated), the associated market will resolve to "No".

If the 2027 UEFA Europa League is cancelled, postponed after May 20, 2027, 11:59 PM ET or the 2027 UEFA Europa League semifinal matchup has not been declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to “No”.

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the UEFA; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
