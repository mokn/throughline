This market will resolve according to which map is played the most times across all official matches of the PGL Major Singapore 2026, currently scheduled for November 24 to December 13, 2026.

The Active Duty map pool for the PGL Major Singapore 2026 consists of Ancient, Anubis, Cache, Dust II (also known as Dust2), Inferno, Mirage, and Nuke. Every completed map in every official match of the event (Stage 1, Stage 2, Stage 3, and the Playoffs) counts once, regardless of its result or whether it went to overtime. Maps played in qualifiers held before Stage 1 do not count. Maps that are forfeited or awarded without being played do not count. Maps that are started but not completed (e.g., abandoned and not replayed) do not count. A map that is restarted or remade counts only once.

If two or more maps are tied for the most plays, this market will resolve to the map whose name comes first alphabetically. If the map played the most times is not listed in this market (e.g., due to a map pool change), this market will resolve to "Other".

If the PGL Major Singapore 2026 is canceled or postponed such that it has not concluded by January 12, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source for this market will be the official match pages, map veto records, and map statistics on HLTV (https://www.hltv.org); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
