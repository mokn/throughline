This market will resolve to the goalkeeper who records the most clean sheets during the 2026-27 La Liga season.

Only clean sheets recorded in La Liga matches will count. Clean sheets in other competitions (e.g., domestic cups, UEFA Champions League, UEFA Europa League, UEFA Conference League, international matches) will not count for this market.

In the event of a tie, this market will resolve according to the official leader as determined by La Liga. If multiple leaders are announced then this market will resolve to the goalkeeper who conceded fewer goals during the 2026-27 La Liga season. If a tie still persists, this market will resolve to the goalkeeper whose listed last name comes first alphabetically.

If the 2026-27 La Liga season is canceled or not completed by June 14, 2027, the market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source will be official statistics from La Liga. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
