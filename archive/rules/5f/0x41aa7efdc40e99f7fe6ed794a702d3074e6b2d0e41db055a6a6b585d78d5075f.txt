This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed player qualifies for the 2027 PPA Tour Finals, the season-ending championship of the 2026-27 Carvana PPA Tour season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

A player is considered to have "qualified" if they are named to the women's field for the 2027 PPA Tour Finals according to the official Professional Pickleball Association source.

If a player qualifies but withdraws before competing, they will still be considered to have "qualified" and this market will resolve to "Yes".

If the 2027 PPA Tour Finals is cancelled, postponed after May 10, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or the women's field has not been officially determined within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "No".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the Professional Pickleball Association (https://ppatour.com/); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
