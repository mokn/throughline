This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed player is the first wide receiver to reach 1,000 receiving yards in the 2026-27 NFL regular season. Otherwise, the associated market will resolve to "No".

A player reaches 1,000 receiving yards at the moment his cumulative 2026-27 NFL regular season receiving yards total equals or exceeds 1,000. Only regular season games count. Playoff games, including Wild Card, Divisional, Conference Championship, and Pro Football Championship games, do not count.

If two or more wide receivers reach 1,000 receiving yards within the same game, or in separate games that have the same scheduled kickoff time, the market for each such player who reaches the mark will resolve to "Yes".

If the listed player does not play in the 2026-27 NFL regular season for any reason, the associated market will resolve to "No".

If the 2026-27 NFL regular season is shortened, truncated, or ends early for any reason, the outcome shall be determined using available official data for completed games.

If the 2026-27 NFL regular season is cancelled, postponed after January 26, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or it cannot be determined which wide receiver first reached 1,000 receiving yards within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "50-50".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the NFL; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
