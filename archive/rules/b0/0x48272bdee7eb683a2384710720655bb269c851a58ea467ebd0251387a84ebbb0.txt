This market will resolve to “Yes” if, between market creation and the specified date, 11:59 PM ET, the US Department of Commerce’s Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS) or the Department of Commerce publicly announces or publishes a penalty imposed by BIS on NVIDIA Corporation or any of its subsidiaries or successors for violations of US export-control laws or regulations. Otherwise, this market will resolve to “No”.

A qualifying penalty is a final administrative order imposing a civil monetary penalty, a denial or suspension of export privileges, or another administrative sanction, or a settlement agreement between BIS and NVIDIA that includes a civil monetary penalty or a denial or suspension of export privileges. A temporary denial order against NVIDIA will qualify.

The date of the public announcement or publication will be used to determine whether a penalty falls within this market’s timeframe, regardless of the date on the underlying order or agreement.

Charging letters or proposed charges that have not resulted in a final order or settlement, warning letters, “is-informed” letters, license denials or revocations, new export-control rules or license requirements that apply to NVIDIA, Entity List additions, criminal charges, and penalties imposed by any other agency will not qualify.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from BIS and the Department of Commerce, however a consensus of credible reporting will also be used.
