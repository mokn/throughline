This market will resolve to “Yes” if the listed company/organization runs one or more advertisements during the national broadcast of Super Bowl LXI on ABC, scheduled for February 14, 2027, at 6:30 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to “No.”

If an advertisement is co-funded or co-branded by multiple companies, all listed companies involved will count as having advertised.

Any brand which is at least 50% owned by a listed company will count for the parent company, such as Beats for Apple.

If this game is delayed or postponed past February 28, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to “No”.

The advertisement must air between kickoff and the final whistle to qualify.

The resolution source for this market is the official broadcast of Super Bowl LXI, however a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
