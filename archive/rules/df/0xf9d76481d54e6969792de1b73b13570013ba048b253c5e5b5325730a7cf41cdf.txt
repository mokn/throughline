This market will resolve according to the home nation of the 2027 UEFA Champions League winner.

If at any point it becomes impossible for any club from the listed country to win the 2027 UEFA Champions League (e.g. they are all eliminated), the associated market will resolve to "No".

If the 2027 UEFA Champions League is cancelled, postponed after June 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or the 2027 UEFA Champions League champion has not been declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from UEFA; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
