This market will resolve to "Yes" if all five of England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland and the Republic of Ireland are among the teams that take part in the UEFA Euro 2028 final tournament. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

A team counts as taking part regardless of the route by which it reaches the final tournament, including qualifying as a group winner, qualifying as a best runner-up, winning a play-off, or taking one of the two places reserved for host nations.

If at any point it becomes impossible for all five listed teams to take part in UEFA Euro 2028, this market will resolve immediately to "No".

If UEFA Euro 2028 is cancelled, postponed after June 1, 2028, 11:59 PM ET, or the final tournament participants have not been officially confirmed by UEFA within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "No".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from UEFA; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
