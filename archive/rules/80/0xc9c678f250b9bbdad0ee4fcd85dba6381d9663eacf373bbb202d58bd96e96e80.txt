This market will resolve according to the highest number of units of Grand Theft Auto VI sold that Take-Two Interactive (including Rockstar Games) officially reports between market creation and December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET.

"Units sold" refers to units sold-in across all platforms, editions, and formats. Figures reported as units sold-in, or as units sold without specifying a different measure, will count. Figures for sell-through, players, pre-orders, revenue, net bookings, or the Grand Theft Auto franchise as a whole will not count.

If Take-Two Interactive (including Rockstar Games) does not publish a units sold figure for Grand Theft Auto VI by December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to the lowest bracket.

If the reported value falls exactly between two brackets, this market will resolve to the higher range bracket.

Figures stated as a lower bound (e.g., "over", "more than", "in excess of" or "surpassing" a number) will be treated as that number. If no exact figure is available, figures stated as approximations (e.g., "about", "approximately", "roughly" or "around") will be taken at their stated value, while figures described as "nearly", "almost", "close to" or "just under" a value will be treated as just below that value.

The resolution source will be official reports from Take-Two Interactive (https://www.take2games.com/ir) or Rockstar Games (https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire); however, other official company sources or a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
