This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team achieves promotion from the China League One to the Chinese Super League for the 2027 season at the conclusion of the current league season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Official promotion is defined as a team clinching a place in the Chinese Super League for the 2027 season through their final standing in the China League One, via playoff victory, or by any other official league decision recognized by the China League One and Chinese Super League.

If the 2026 China League One season is cancelled, postponed after December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or there are no teams confirmed for promotion within that timeframe, the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from the China League One and Chinese Super League. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
