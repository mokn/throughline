The FIFA presidential election for the 2027–2031 term is scheduled to be held on March 18, 2027, at the 77th FIFA Congress.

This market will resolve to the individual who is elected President of FIFA at the 2027 FIFA presidential election.

To count for resolution, the individual must be elected President of FIFA by the FIFA Congress at the 2027 FIFA presidential election. Any interim, acting, or caretaker President of FIFA will not count toward the resolution of this market.

If no such President of FIFA is elected by December 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from FIFA; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
