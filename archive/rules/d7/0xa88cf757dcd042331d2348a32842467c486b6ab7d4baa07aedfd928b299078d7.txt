This market will resolve to the player who wins the PGA TOUR Player of the Year award (Jack Nicklaus Award) for the 2027 PGA TOUR season.

If the PGA TOUR names more than one winner, this market will resolve to "Other".

If no winner is announced by June 30, 2028, 11:59 PM ET, for any reason, this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the PGA TOUR (https://www.pgatour.com/); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
