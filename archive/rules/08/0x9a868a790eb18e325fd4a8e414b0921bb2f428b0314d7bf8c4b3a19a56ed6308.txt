Parliamentary elections to elect the 26th Knesset took place on October 27, 2026.

This market will resolve to "Yes" if an Israeli government that includes an Alternate Prime Minister, or that is formed under a coalition agreement providing for a rotation of the office of Prime Minister, is in office at any point by February 28, 2027, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

An Alternate Prime Minister refers to the office of Alternate Prime Minister as established under Israel's Basic Law: The Government. A rotation of the office of Prime Minister refers to an agreement under which the office of Prime Minister is scheduled to pass from one individual to another during the government's term.

The government is considered formed when the newly elected Knesset approves the government in a vote of confidence.

This market will resolve based on a consensus of credible reporting. In case of ambiguity, this market will resolve based on official information from the Government of Israel and the Knesset.
