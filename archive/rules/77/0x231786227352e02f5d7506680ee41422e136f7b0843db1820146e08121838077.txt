Regional elections to elect the Regional Council of Region Gävleborg, Sweden are scheduled to take place on September 13, 2026.

This market will resolve to the next individual who officially assumes the office of Chair of the Regional Board of Region Gävleborg following the 2026 Swedish regional elections.

To count for resolution, the individual must be formally elected by the relevant regional council as Chair of the Regional Board. Any interim or caretaker Chair will not count toward the resolution of this market.

If no such Chair takes office by March 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to “Other”.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from the government of Sweden and Region Gävleborg; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
