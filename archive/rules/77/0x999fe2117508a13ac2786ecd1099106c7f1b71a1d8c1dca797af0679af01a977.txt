This market will resolve to “Yes” if Anthropic, or an authorized representative of Anthropic, publicly and officially announces that Anthropic has created an artificial general intelligence or that Anthropic’s AI models constitute artificial general intelligence between market creation and December 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to “No”.

A qualifying announcement must be made through official channels, by an individual acting in an official capacity. Statements made incidentally or informally in a context not intended for official communication do not qualify.

A qualifying announcement must clearly communicate that Anthropic has attained artificial general intelligence or that its models constitute artificial general intelligence. Statements that merely allude to, reference, or describe artificial general intelligence, without communicating that it has been achieved, do not qualify.

The following do not qualify as official announcements:
Anonymous, unattributed, or leaked statements not confirmed as official;
Statements by persons not authorized to speak for Anthropic;
Third-party speculation, analysis, or predictions that Anthropic will announce AGI;
Satirical, fabricated, hacked, or impersonated communications;

The resolution source for this market will be official information from Anthropic and the official representatives of Anthropic.
