This market will resolve according to the coach who wins the 2026 AP Coach of the Year Award, currently scheduled to be announced on January 27, 2027.

If the award is not presented, is canceled, or a winner has not been announced by February 10, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other."

If multiple coaches are named co-winners, this market will resolve in favor of the coach whose listed name comes first alphabetically.

The resolution source for this market will be official information from AP; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
