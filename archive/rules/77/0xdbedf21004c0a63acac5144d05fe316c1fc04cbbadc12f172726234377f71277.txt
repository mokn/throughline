This market will resolve according to the country officially announced by UEFA as the host of the 2027 UEFA Nations League Finals, currently scheduled for June 9-13, 2027.

A qualifying announcement means an official confirmation by UEFA of the country selected to host the Finals. Bid proposals, recommendations by member associations, and media speculation about UEFA's decision will not qualify on their own.

Under UEFA regulations, the host is selected by the UEFA Executive Committee, preferably from among the four teams that qualify for the Finals.

A qualifying announcement before this market's close date will immediately resolve this market to the corresponding option, regardless of any subsequent change to the host or to the scheduled dates of the Finals.

If no host is officially announced by June 9, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from UEFA.
