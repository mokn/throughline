This market will resolve according to which player records the most total kills across all official games played during the 2026 League of Legends World Championship (Worlds 2026), currently scheduled for October 15 to November 14, 2026.

Every completed official game of the tournament counts (Play-In, Swiss Stage, and Knockout Stage). Games that are remade, abandoned, or not completed do not count unless their kills appear in the official tournament statistics. Games awarded by forfeit without being played do not count.

If two or more players are tied for the most kills, this market will resolve to the tied player with the fewer total deaths across the tournament. If a tie persists, this market will resolve to the tied player with the more total assists. If a tie still persists, this market will resolve to the player whose listed in-game name comes first alphabetically.

If a player not listed in this market records the most kills, this market will resolve to "Other".

If Worlds 2026 is canceled, or is postponed such that it has not concluded by December 14, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source for this market will be the official tournament player statistics on Leaguepedia (https://lol.fandom.com/wiki/2026_Season_World_Championship); however, official information from Riot Games (https://lolesports.com) and a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
