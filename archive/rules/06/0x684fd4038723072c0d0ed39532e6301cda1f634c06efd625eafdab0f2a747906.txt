This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team is relegated from Liga Nacional (Guatemala) at the conclusion of the 2026-27 Liga Nacional (Guatemala) season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Official relegation is defined as a team losing its place in Liga Nacional (Guatemala) for the following season through their final standing in the 2026-27 Liga Nacional (Guatemala), via relegation playoff defeat, or by any other official league decision recognized by Liga Nacional (Guatemala).

If the 2026-27 Liga Nacional (Guatemala) season is cancelled, postponed after June 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there are no teams confirmed for relegation within that timeframe, the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from Liga Nacional (Guatemala). A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
