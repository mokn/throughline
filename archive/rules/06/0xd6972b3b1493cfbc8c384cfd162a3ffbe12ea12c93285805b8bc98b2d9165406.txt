This market will resolve to the contestant who comes in third-place on The Voice season 30.

If The Voice season 30 concludes without a third-place finisher, or if The Voice season 30 has otherwise not concluded by March 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET this market will resolve to "Other". 

The named individuals will be primarily ranked by order of elimination. If two or more individuals are eliminated exactly simultaneously, ties will be broken by alphabetical order of the individuals' listed first names. This market will resolve to the individual that occupies the third-highest finishing position after applying this ranking.

This market will remain open until the conclusion of the season.

The resolution source for this market will be the official broadcast of the final episode of The Voice season 30.
