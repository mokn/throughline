This market will resolve to the player who wins the 2026-27 Serie A Player of the Season award, as awarded by Lega Serie A.

In the event of a tie, this market will resolve according to the official winner as determined by Lega Serie A. If multiple winners are announced, this market will resolve to the player whose listed last name comes first alphabetically.

If the 2026-27 Serie A season is cancelled, or there is otherwise no such award announced by June 13, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from Lega Serie A (https://www.legaseriea.it); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
