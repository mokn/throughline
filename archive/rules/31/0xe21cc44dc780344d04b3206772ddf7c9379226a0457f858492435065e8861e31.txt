This market will resolve according to Anthropic's (Nasdaq: ANT) market capitalization at the close of regular trading on the last trading day of December 2026 (December 31, 2026).

Anthropic's Class A shares began trading on the Nasdaq Stock Market under the ticker "ANT" on November 19, 2026.

Market capitalization will be calculated as Nasdaq's official closing price (NOCP) for ANT on December 31, 2026, as shown on Nasdaq's ANT historical NOCP page (https://www.nasdaq.com/market-activity/stocks/ant/historical-nocp), multiplied by Anthropic's total number of issued and outstanding shares. If Nasdaq is not, or is no longer, the primary listing exchange for Anthropic's Class A shares on December 31, 2026, the official closing price published by its primary listing exchange will be used instead, and the trading-halt provisions below will apply to that exchange. To capture the company's total market capitalization rather than a class-specific one, the calculation will include all share classes that have economic rights (i.e., participate in dividends and in distributions on liquidation) or are convertible into the publicly traded class, applying any stated conversion ratios to the publicly traded class. Shares that have no economic rights and are not convertible into the publicly traded class will not be counted. Options, warrants, restricted stock units, unvested awards and other rights to acquire shares are not counted.

Share counts will be taken from the most recent official company filing, including filings with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (e.g., the final prospectus filed under Rule 424(b), or a Form 10-Q, 10-K or 8-K), dated on or before December 31, 2026 that states the number of shares outstanding. If a filing states the number of shares outstanding both with and without exercise of the underwriters' option to purchase additional shares, the number assuming no exercise will be used, unless the exercise of that option has been disclosed in an official company filing dated on or before December 31, 2026. Media reports and market data websites will not be used for share counts.

Each bracket includes its lower bound and excludes its upper bound.

If trading in ANT on Nasdaq is halted or ends early on December 31, 2026 and no official closing price is published for that day, the last regular-session trade price on Nasdaq for December 31, 2026 will be used. If ANT does not trade on Nasdaq at all on December 31, 2026, the most recent official closing price published before that date will be used. If Anthropic is acquired by or merges into another company and ANT ceases trading before December 31, 2026, this market will resolve according to the market capitalization, calculated as above, on the last trading day before ANT ceased trading.
