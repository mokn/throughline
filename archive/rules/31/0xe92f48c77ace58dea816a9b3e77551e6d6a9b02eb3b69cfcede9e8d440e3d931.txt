The 2026 Esports Awards honor achievement across the esports industry. The awards ceremony is scheduled for November 17, 2026 in Miami, Florida.

This market will resolve according to the nominee who wins the 2026 Esports Awards award for "Esports Commercial Partner of the Year".

In case of a tie for the winner, this market will resolve in favor of the nominated company whose listed name comes first in alphabetical order.

If for any reason no winner is declared by December 14, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or in case of overall cancellation, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from the Esports Awards, including data from their website (e.g. https://esportsawards.com) and the live broadcast of the award ceremony; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
