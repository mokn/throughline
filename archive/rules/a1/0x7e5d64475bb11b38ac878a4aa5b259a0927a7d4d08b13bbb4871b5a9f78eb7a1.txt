This market will resolve to "Yes" if Novak Djokovic plays in at least one singles match at the 2026 Nitto ATP Finals, currently scheduled for November 15 to November 22, 2026, in Turin, Italy. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Any singles match in the round-robin stage, the semifinals, or the final will qualify, including a match played as an alternate. A match counts once its first point has been played, even if Djokovic later retires from it. Walkovers, exhibition matches, practice sessions, and doubles matches will not count.

If Djokovic has not played a qualifying match by the end of the tournament, or if the 2026 Nitto ATP Finals are canceled or postponed after December 6, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "No".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the ATP Tour (https://www.atptour.com); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
