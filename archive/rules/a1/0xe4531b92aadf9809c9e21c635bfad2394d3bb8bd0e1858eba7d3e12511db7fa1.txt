This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team finishes 10th in the 2026-27 Scottish Premiership. Otherwise, the associated market will resolve to "No".

If at any point it becomes impossible for the listed team to finish 10th in the 2026-27 Scottish Premiership, the associated market will resolve to "No".

If the 2026-27 Scottish Premiership season is cancelled, postponed after June 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there is otherwise no 10th place team declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from Scottish Premiership; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
