This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team is relegated from K-League at the conclusion of the 2026 K-League season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Official relegation is defined as a team losing its place in K-League for the following season through their final standing in the 2026 K-League, via relegation playoff defeat, or by any other official league decision recognized by K-League.

If the 2026 K-League season is cancelled, postponed after December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or there are no teams confirmed for relegation within that timeframe, the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from K-League. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
