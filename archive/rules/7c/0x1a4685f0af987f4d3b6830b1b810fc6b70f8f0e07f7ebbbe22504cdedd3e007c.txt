This market will resolve according to the highest daily Arctic sea ice extent published by the National Snow and Ice Data Center (NSIDC) for any day from December 1, 2026 through April 1, 2027. Exactly one outcome in this market will resolve to "Yes". If the reported value falls exactly between two brackets, then this market will resolve to the higher range bracket. Each bracket includes its lower bound and excludes its upper bound, so a value exactly equal to a bound shared by two brackets belongs to the higher bracket.

The qualifying value is the largest daily extent, in millions of square kilometers, shown in the "NH-Daily-Extent" tab of NSIDC's Sea Ice Index Daily Extent spreadsheet (G02135, version 4 as of this market's creation) for the days in that range, read from the row for each day and the column for that day's year (2026 for December 2026, 2027 for January to April 2027). Days with no published value will not be considered for resolution. The 5-day average values, including the "NH-5-Day-Extent" tab and the annual maximum NSIDC announces in Arctic Sea Ice News & Analysis, will not be considered for resolution. Values will be used exactly as published by NSIDC, without rounding. NSIDC reports to 3 decimal places.

A daily datapoint must be finalized before it is considered for this market (namely, once the next day's datapoint is published, the previous one is finalized).

This market will resolve once NSIDC has published the daily extent for April 2, 2027, which finalizes the datapoint for April 1, 2027. If any data remains unpublished or unfinalized, this market will remain open for up to 2 calendar days (ET) after April 2, 2027; it will then resolve based on the finalized data published up to that point. If NSIDC has published no daily extent for any day in the range by then, or has discontinued the series, this market will resolve 50-50 across the outcomes. No substitute data provider will be used.

Revisions published before NSIDC publishes the daily extent for April 2, 2027 will be considered; revisions published after it will not be considered for resolution.

In the case that the Resolution Source publishes data, sufficient to settle a Market, that is clearly erroneous and the result of technical or operational error, or of unauthorized interference with the Resolution Source or its data, the Market may remain open for a period of up to seven (7) calendar days (ET) from the time of the original release, in anticipation of a correction by the Resolution Source. If the Resolution Source publishes a corrected value within that period, that value will be eligible for resolution. If no corrected value is published within that period, Polymarket will issue a Clarification to determine whether the originally published value may be eligible for resolution, or whether other action is required to preserve the orderly functioning of the Market. This seven-day period is separate from the 2-day period for unpublished data above: the 2-day period applies when data has not been published, and the seven-day period applies only to a published value that is clearly erroneous.

Revisions remain subject to the cutoff above. The only exception is a correction of a clearly erroneous print under the preceding paragraph, which will be used even if published after the cutoff.

The primary resolution source for this market will be NSIDC, specifically the "NH-Daily-Extent" tab of the Sea Ice Index Daily Extent spreadsheet, linked from https://nsidc.org/sea-ice-today/sea-ice-tools (direct file: https://noaadata.apps.nsidc.org/NOAA/G02135/seaice_analysis/Sea_Ice_Index_Daily_Extent_G02135_v4.0.xlsx). Please note that this market is about the daily Arctic sea ice extent according to NSIDC's Sea Ice Index, not according to any other datasets or providers.
