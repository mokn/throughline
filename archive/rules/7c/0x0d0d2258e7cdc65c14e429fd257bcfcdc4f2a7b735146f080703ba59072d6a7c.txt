This market will resolve according to the CNN Fear & Greed Index closing value for January 8, 2027.

The closing value is the value shown for January 8, 2027 in the "Timeline" tab (the historical chart) of the Fear & Greed Index at https://www.cnn.com/markets/fear-and-greed at the time CNN first shows a value for the next trading day after January 8, 2027. Changes CNN makes to the value for January 8, 2027 after that time are not considered, unless the value shown at that time was clearly erroneous, in which case the correction paragraph below applies. Values shown for January 8, 2027 before then, while the index is still updating, and the separate previous-close and other summary figures CNN displays, are not used. If the value is shown with decimals, the decimals are dropped (e.g., 24.9 counts as 24).

The brackets are whole-number ranges that include both numbers shown (e.g., "25–34" includes 25 and 34). They follow CNN's own rating zones: 0–24 Extreme Fear; 25–44 Fear; 45–55 Neutral; 56–75 Greed; 76–100 Extreme Greed.

If CNN has not shown a value for the next trading day within seven (7) calendar days after January 8, 2027, the value shown for January 8, 2027 at the end of that period will be used. If CNN shows no value for January 8, 2027 within that period, this market will resolve according to the value for January 8, 2027 published by a successor index from CNN under the same or a substantially similar name, if one exists. Otherwise, this market will resolve to the "45–55" bracket.

The resolution source for this market is CNN's Fear & Greed Index, available at https://www.cnn.com/markets/fear-and-greed.

In the case that the Resolution Source publishes data, sufficient to settle a Market, that is clearly erroneous and the result of technical or operational error, or of unauthorized interference with the Resolution Source or its data, the Market may remain open for a period of up to seven (7) calendar days (ET) from the time of the original release, in anticipation of a correction by the Resolution Source. If the Resolution Source publishes a corrected value within that period, that value will be eligible for resolution. If no corrected value is published within that period, Polymarket will issue a Clarification to determine whether the originally published value may be eligible for resolution, or whether other action is required to preserve the orderly functioning of the Market.
