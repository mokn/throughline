This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed player reaches the Final of the 2027 PPA Tour Finals in any discipline in which they compete (women's singles, women's doubles, or mixed doubles). Otherwise, the associated market will resolve to "No".

The 2027 PPA Tour Finals is an invitational, season-ending event: players and teams qualify by their Race to the Finals ranking and are drawn into pools, with the top finishers in each pool advancing to the semifinals. A player who does not qualify for the 2027 PPA Tour Finals will resolve to "No".

If at any point it becomes impossible for the listed player to reach the Final of the 2027 PPA Tour Finals (e.g. they fail to qualify, are eliminated, withdraw, or are disqualified), the associated market will resolve to "No".

If the 2027 PPA Tour Finals is cancelled, postponed after May 10, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or the players that reached the Final have not been officially confirmed by the Professional Pickleball Association within that timeframe, this market will resolve 50-50.

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the Professional Pickleball Association (https://ppatour.com/); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
