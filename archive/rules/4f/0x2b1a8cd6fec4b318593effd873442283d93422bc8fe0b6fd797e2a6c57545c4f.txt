This market will resolve to "Yes" if Anthropic, PBC becomes a component of the Nasdaq-100 Index (NDX) effective prior to market open on a trading day that is on or before the listed date. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

The effective time of an addition is the time stated by Nasdaq, Inc. in its announcement (for example, "prior to market open on" a given date). "Prior to market open" refers to the start of the regular trading session of the Nasdaq Stock Market (9:30 AM ET) on a day on which the Nasdaq Stock Market is open for trading, as stated by Nasdaq, Inc. in its announcement. An addition stated as effective after the close of trading on a given date will be treated as effective prior to market open on the next trading day. An announcement published by the listed date does not qualify if the addition becomes effective after that date. If Nasdaq postpones or cancels an announced addition before it becomes effective, the revised effective date, if any, will be used. If Anthropic publicly announces a definitive agreement to be acquired by or merged into another company before its addition becomes effective, this market will resolve to "No", regardless of whether that transaction is later completed or terminated.

References to Anthropic, PBC include any holding company formed to hold all or substantially all of its business for the purpose of its initial public offering, and any successor by name change.

Anthropic's Class A shares began trading on the Nasdaq Stock Market under the ticker "ANT" on November 19, 2026. An addition through any Nasdaq-100 index action qualifies, including a fast entry addition for a newly listed company, the annual reconstitution, or a replacement of an existing component.

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from Nasdaq, Inc. (https://ir.nasdaq.com/news-and-events/press-releases) and the published Nasdaq-100 constituent list; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
