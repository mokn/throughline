Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

The per-game average is the player's total regular-season rebounds divided by regular-season games played, as displayed to one decimal place on NBA.com/stats. Only 2026-27 NBA regular-season games count. NBA Cup group-play and knockout games other than the NBA Cup Championship game are regular-season games and count. Statistics from the preseason, the NBA Cup Championship game, the Play-In Tournament, the playoffs and the All-Star Game are excluded. If a player plays for more than one team during the season, his statistics from all teams are combined.

If the 2026-27 NBA regular season concludes early, is shortened, or is truncated for any reason, this market will resolve using available NBA statistics for completed games, with leaderboard qualification applied as the NBA applies it to the shortened season.

If the 2026-27 NBA regular season is cancelled, or postponed after April 25, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve according to the player's average and qualification status in regular-season games completed by that time.

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the NBA, including NBA.com/stats; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
