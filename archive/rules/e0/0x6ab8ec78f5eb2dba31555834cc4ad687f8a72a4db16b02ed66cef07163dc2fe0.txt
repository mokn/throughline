This market will resolve according to the club in first place in the official 2026-27 LaLiga standings as of December 23, 2026, 11:59 PM ET.

LaLiga is scheduled to begin its winter break after Matchday 17, the last matchday scheduled in 2026. Only matches completed by December 23, 2026, 11:59 PM ET will be considered. Matches postponed to a date after December 23, 2026 will not count, and matches rescheduled to take place on or before that date will count.

If two or more clubs are level on points in first place, the leader will be determined by applying, in order, the tie-breaking criteria used to rank clubs in the final end-of-season standings under the official LaLiga regulations, applied to all matches completed by December 23, 2026, 11:59 PM ET. This applies regardless of how the official standings published at that time order those clubs. Any criterion that requires a play-off match or a drawing of lots will not be used. If two or more clubs remain level after all other end-of-season tie-breaking criteria have been applied, this market will resolve in favor of the club whose listed name comes first alphabetically.

Point deductions or result changes officially applied to the standings by December 23, 2026, 11:59 PM ET will be considered. Changes applied after this time will not be considered.

If the 2026-27 LaLiga season is cancelled before December 23, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source for this market will be the official LaLiga standings, available here: https://www.laliga.com/en-GB/laliga-easports/standing; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
