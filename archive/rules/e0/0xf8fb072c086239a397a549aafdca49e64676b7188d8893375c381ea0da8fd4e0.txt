This market will resolve to the player who wins the 2026-27 Premier League Player of the Season award.

In the event of a tie, this market will resolve according to the official winner as determined by the Premier League. If multiple winners are announced, this market will resolve to the player whose listed last name comes first alphabetically.

If the 2026-27 Premier League season is cancelled, or there is otherwise no such award announced by June 13, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the Premier League (https://www.premierleague.com); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
