Following the Brazilian general election held on October 4, 2026, the Federal Senate of Brazil is set to hold an internal election for the position of President of the Brazilian Senate. This market will resolve according to the name of the candidate that becomes President of the Brazilian Senate as a result of this election. 

Temporary, interim, or placeholder Presidents appointed before the election will not count.

If this election does not take place or the results of this election are not known by February 28, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "No president by Feb 28".

This market will resolve based on official information from the Federal Senate of Brazil or a consensus of credible reporting.
