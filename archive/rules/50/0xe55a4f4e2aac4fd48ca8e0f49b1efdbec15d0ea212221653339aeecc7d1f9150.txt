This market will resolve in favor of the quarterback who finishes the 2026 NFL regular season with the highest pass completion percentage, among quarterbacks meeting the NFL's qualification threshold for the completion percentage title.

In the event of a tie, this market will resolve to the tied quarterback with more total pass completions in the 2026 NFL regular season. If a tie still persists, this market will resolve to the tied quarterback whose listed last name comes first alphabetically.

If at any point it becomes impossible for a listed quarterback to finish with the highest completion percentage, the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

If the 2026 NFL regular season is cancelled, shortened, or no leader is determined by January 24, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source will be official information from the NFL (https://www.nfl.com); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
