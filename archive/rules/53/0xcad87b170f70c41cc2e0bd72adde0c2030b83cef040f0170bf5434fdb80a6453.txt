On November 12, 2026, NVIDIA announced that Jensen Huang had resigned as its President, Chief Executive Officer, and a member of its Board of Directors, effective immediately.

This market will resolve to “Yes” if, after market creation, Jensen Huang is officially announced as accepting or beginning a formal role within the corporate or governance structure of NVIDIA before Jensen or Lori Huang file for divorce or announce their definitive intention to divorce. Otherwise, this market will resolve to “No”.

If neither a qualifying announcement of Jensen Huang rejoining NVIDIA nor a qualifying divorce filing or announcement occurs by December 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to “No”.

Qualifying roles include employment, executive, officer, director, formal advisory, or governance positions at NVIDIA, including on an interim or acting basis and membership in a body formally responsible for governing or overseeing the company. A role within a subsidiary or controlled entity of NVIDIA will also qualify.

Merely holding or trading NVIDIA shares, voting as a shareholder, entering into a commercial or research partnership with NVIDIA, informally advising, appearing at NVIDIA events, or receiving an honorary or emeritus title will not qualify unless Jensen Huang also accepts or begins a formal role within NVIDIA’s corporate or governance structure.

An official announcement that Jensen Huang has accepted a qualifying role will be sufficient, regardless of when he formally begins serving in that role.

Once a qualifying announcement of Jensen Huang rejoining NVIDIA is made before a qualifying divorce filing or announcement, this market will resolve to “Yes” regardless of whether he later leaves the role. If a qualifying divorce filing or announcement occurs before a qualifying announcement of Jensen Huang rejoining NVIDIA is made, this market will resolve to “No”.

The primary resolution source for whether Jensen Huang has rejoined NVIDIA will be official information from NVIDIA (including its filings with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission) or Jensen Huang; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.

A qualifying divorce filing or announcement occurs if Jensen or Lori Huang file for divorce or announce their definitive intention to divorce after market creation. An announcement from either Lori or Jensen Huang, or their official authorized representatives, of their definitive intention to divorce will be sufficient, regardless of when or whether the divorce ultimately occurs. Announcements that they are considering or open to a divorce will not qualify.

The resolution sources for whether a qualifying divorce filing or announcement has occurred will be statements from Jensen Huang and/or Lori Huang, and their representatives, as well as relevant court records; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
