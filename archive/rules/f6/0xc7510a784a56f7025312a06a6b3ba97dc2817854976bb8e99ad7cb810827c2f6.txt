This market will resolve based on the total number of times Roshan is killed in Game 1 of the BLAST Slam IX Grand Final (the best-of-five final of the Playoffs), currently scheduled for November 29, 2026.

Each market in this event will resolve to "Over" if the total number of Roshan kills in Game 1 is greater than the listed line, and to "Under" if it is less than the listed line.

Every Roshan kill in the game counts toward the total, regardless of which team lands the killing blow, including repeat kills after Roshan respawns. Only the first game of the Grand Final series counts. If Game 1 is remade before it is completed, only the completed replay counts.

If Game 1 of the Grand Final is not played to completion (including a game awarded by forfeit), or if it is canceled or postponed such that it has not been completed by December 29, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve 50-50.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official match data from OpenDota (https://www.opendota.com), using the Roshan kills recorded in the match's objectives log; however, Dotabuff (https://www.dotabuff.com), Datdota (https://www.datdota.com), official broadcast footage, and a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
