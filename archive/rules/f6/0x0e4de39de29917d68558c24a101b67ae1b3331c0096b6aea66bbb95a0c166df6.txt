This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team records 80 or more points during the 2026-27 NHL regular season. Otherwise, the associated market will resolve to "No".

If at any point it becomes impossible for the listed team to record 80 or more points during the 2026-27 NHL regular season (e.g. they are mathematically eliminated), the associated market will resolve to "No". Any tiebreaker will be considered part of the NHL regular season.

If the 2026-27 NHL regular season is cancelled, postponed after April 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or it cannot be determined whether the listed team has recorded 80 or more points within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official information from the NHL; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
