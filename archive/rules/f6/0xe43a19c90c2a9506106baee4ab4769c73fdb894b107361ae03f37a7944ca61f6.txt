This market will resolve to "AFC" if the AFC champion wins the 2027 NFL league championship game, which concludes the 2026 NFL season. This market will resolve to "NFC" if the NFC champion wins the 2027 NFL league championship game.

If the 2027 NFL league championship game is cancelled, postponed after March 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there is otherwise no winner declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to 50-50.

The primary resolution source will be official information from the NFL (https://www.nfl.com/); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
