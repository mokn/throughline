This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team is relegated from Bolivia LFPB at the conclusion of the 2026 Bolivia LFPB season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Official relegation is defined as a team losing its place in Bolivia LFPB for the following season through their final standing in the 2026 Bolivia LFPB, via relegation playoff defeat, or by any other official league decision recognized by Bolivia LFPB.

If the 2026 Bolivia LFPB season is cancelled, postponed after December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or there are no teams confirmed for relegation within that timeframe, the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from Bolivia LFPB. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
