This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed artist is confirmed as a featured or credited artist on "Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album", scheduled for release on November 19, 2026. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

To qualify as "featured", the listed artist must be credited on at least one track on the album according to Rockstar Games' official tracklist or artist announcements (https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/music). If this information is unavailable from Rockstar's official sources, official album credits listed on major streaming platforms (e.g., Spotify, Apple Music) may be used instead.

If the album fails to release by November 26, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "No".

The resolution source of this market will be Rockstar Games' official GTA VI: The Album page (https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/music) and associated official Rockstar announcements, or official album credits listed on major streaming platforms (e.g., Spotify, Apple Music).
