This market will resolve to "Yes" if Team Falcons wins a Tier 1 Counter-Strike event between market creation and November 24, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, the day before the PGL Major Singapore 2026 is currently scheduled to begin. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

A Tier 1 event means a Counter-Strike tournament classified as S-Tier by the Liquipedia Counter-Strike wiki (https://liquipedia.net/counterstrike/S-Tier_Tournaments) at the time the tournament concludes. A later change to a tournament's Liquipedia tier classification will not affect this market. Events won before market creation, including the IEM Cologne Major 2026, do not count.

Winning means Team Falcons finishing in first place at the event. The match or standing that decides the event title must conclude within the window above; an event that is still in progress at the deadline will not count. A change to the start date of the PGL Major Singapore 2026 will not change the deadline above.

If it cannot be determined whether Team Falcons won a qualifying event by December 8, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from the relevant tournament organizers and HLTV.org, with tier classification per Liquipedia; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
