This market will resolve to "Yes" if Cristiano Ronaldo appears in at least one match for Portugal at the UEFA Euro 2028 final tournament. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

An appearance means taking the field, whether as a starter or as a substitute, for any length of time. Being named in the squad or on the bench without taking the field does not count.

If Portugal does not qualify for UEFA Euro 2028, this market will resolve to "No".

If UEFA Euro 2028 is cancelled, postponed after August 1, 2028, 11:59 PM ET, or Ronaldo has not appeared in a match at the final tournament within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "No".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from UEFA; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
