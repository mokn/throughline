This market will resolve to "Yes" if the specified team reaches the Grand Final of the 2026 League of Legends World Championship (Worlds 2026), currently scheduled for October 15 to November 14, 2026, with the Grand Final scheduled for November 14, 2026. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

A team is considered to have made the Grand Final if it is one of the two teams that competes in the Grand Final match, regardless of the Grand Final's outcome.

If this tournament is postponed after November 28, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, canceled, or the Grand Final participants have not been determined within this timeframe, this market will resolve to "No".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the tournament organizer, Riot Games, via the LoL Esports website (https://lolesports.com). However, a consensus of credible reporting (e.g., Liquipedia at https://liquipedia.net/leagueoflegends/World_Championship/2026) may also be used.
