This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team achieves promotion from the J2 League to the Japan J. League for the 2027-28 season at the conclusion of the current league season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Official promotion is defined as a team clinching a place in the Japan J. League for the 2027-28 season through their final standing in the J2 League, via playoff victory, or by any other official league decision recognized by the J2 League and Japan J. League.

If the 2026-27 J2 League season is cancelled, postponed after June 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there are no teams confirmed for promotion within that timeframe, the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from the J2 League and Japan J. League. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
