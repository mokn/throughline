This market will resolve to "Yes" if Lando Norris is officially classified as the winner of three or more Grands Prix during the 2026 FIA Formula One World Championship season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

A win means being listed in first place in the first final race classification published by the FIA following a Grand Prix, including any time penalties and official adjustments applied before publication. Disqualifications or changes made after the publication of the first final classification will not affect this market. Sprint race wins will not count.

This market will resolve to "Yes" as soon as Norris records his third win as defined above. If at any point it becomes impossible for Norris to win three or more Grands Prix during the 2026 season (e.g. fewer races remain than wins required), this market will immediately resolve to "No".

If the remainder of the 2026 Formula One season is cancelled, postponed after December 20, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or three wins as defined above have not been recorded within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official information from the FIA and Formula1.com; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
