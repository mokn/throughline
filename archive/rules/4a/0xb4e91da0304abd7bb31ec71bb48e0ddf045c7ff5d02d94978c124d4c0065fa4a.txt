This market will resolve according to the primary vocal performer of the national anthem at Super Bowl LXI, currently scheduled for February 14, 2027.

If multiple individuals perform the national anthem, this market will resolve according to the performer billed as the primary national anthem vocal performer by the NFL.

If this event is cancelled or otherwise delayed beyond February 28, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there is no vocal national anthem performance within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source for this market will be the official broadcast of Super Bowl LXI; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
