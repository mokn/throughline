This market will resolve in favor of the team that finishes the 2026-27 NFL regular season with the most total offensive yards per game (total yards gained divided by games played).

In the event of a tie, this market will resolve in favor of the tied team with more points scored during the 2026-27 NFL regular season. If a tie still persists, this market will resolve in favor of the team with a higher yards per play average during the 2026-27 NFL regular season. If a tie still persists, this market will resolve to “Other”.

If the 2026-27 NFL season concludes early, is shortened, or is truncated for any reason, the outcome shall be determined using available NFL statistics for completed games.

If the 2026-27 NFL regular season is cancelled, postponed after January 26, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or it is unclear which team led the NFL in total offensive yards per game (total yards gained divided by games played) within that timeframe, this market will resolve to “Other”.

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the NFL; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
