This market will resolve to "Cerrone" if Donald Cerrone is officially declared the winner of the match against Anthony Pettis at the NOW Arena in Hoffman Estates, Illinois, scheduled for November 28, 2026. It will resolve to "Pettis" if Anthony Pettis is officially declared the winner.

If the match ends level on points or in any form of draw, this market will resolve according to the official winner as determined by the rules of Real American Freestyle, including any tiebreaker criteria. If no official winner is designated under those rules, or if the match is ruled a No Contest, canceled, or postponed beyond December 12, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve "50-50." If either wrestler is replaced, this market will resolve "50-50."

This market will resolve based on the official result announced at the conclusion of the event. Any later change to that result, including an overturn to a No Contest, will not be considered.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from Real American Freestyle (https://www.realamericanfreestyle.com/); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
