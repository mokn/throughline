This market will resolve according to the team that wins the 2026 Copa Sudamericana.

If at any point it becomes impossible for a listed team to win the 2026 Copa Sudamericana per the rules of CONMEBOL (e.g., they are eliminated), the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

If the 2026 Copa Sudamericana is cancelled, postponed after December 5, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or there is otherwise no winner declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source will be official information from CONMEBOL; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
