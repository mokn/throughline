This market will resolve to the contestant who wins the Ultimate Self-Defense Championship (USDC) Season 4.

Contestants will be ranked primarily by their final standing in USDC Season 4, as announced in the show. If any two contestants jointly receive the same result, ties will be broken by alphabetical order of the contestants' listed names. This market will resolve to the contestant that occupies the highest finishing position after applying this ranking.

If the final episode of USDC Season 4 has not been publicly released on the official USDC by Rokas Leo YouTube channel by March 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or if USDC Season 4 concludes without a winning contestant being declared, this market will resolve to "Other".

Only episodes publicly released on the official USDC by Rokas Leo YouTube channel will be considered. Early-access releases (e.g. on Patreon) will not count toward this market's resolution.

The resolution source for this market will be footage from USDC Season 4, as published on the official USDC by Rokas Leo YouTube channel (https://www.youtube.com/@usdc.rokasleo).
