This market will resolve according to the first individual who is officially announced as the successor to Jensen Huang as CEO of NVIDIA Corporation by December 31, 2028, 11:59 PM ET.

An announcement of a successor will be sufficient for a "Yes" resolution regardless of whether the announced replacement actually becomes the next CEO of NVIDIA.

Interim CEOs will not qualify.

If no successor is announced by December 31, 2028, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to “Other”.

The resolution source will be public statements from NVIDIA Corporation, however a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
