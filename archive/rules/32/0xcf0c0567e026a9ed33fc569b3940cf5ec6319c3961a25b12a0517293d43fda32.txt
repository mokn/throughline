This market will resolve to "Yes" if any single game played at the 2026 League of Legends World Championship (Worlds 2026), currently scheduled for October 15 to November 14, 2026, has an official game duration of 50 minutes or more (50:00 or longer). Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

All completed official games of the tournament count, including the Play-In, the Swiss Stage, and the Knockout Stage. Game duration is the in-game time from the start of the game to the end of the game, as recorded in the official post-game results. Time spent in pauses does not count. A game that is remade or restarted before completion does not count; only the completed, officially recorded game counts. Games awarded by forfeit without being played do not count.

If Worlds 2026 is canceled, or is postponed such that it has not concluded by December 14, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve based on the games completed by that time.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official match data from Riot Games via the LoL Esports website (https://lolesports.com), with per-game durations as logged on Leaguepedia (https://lol.fandom.com/wiki/2026_Season_World_Championship). However, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
