This market will resolve to "Yes" if GTE officially launches a token by 11:59 PM ET on the date specified in the title. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Only an official token launched by GTE will qualify. Stablecoins, memecoins, LSTs and synthetic tokens will not count.

The token must be actively and publicly tradable. Announcements alone do not qualify.

The primary resolution source for this market will be information from GTE (https://x.com/gte_xyz), however a consensus of credible reporting will also be used.
