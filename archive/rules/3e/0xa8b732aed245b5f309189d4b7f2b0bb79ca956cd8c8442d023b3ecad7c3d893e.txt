This market will resolve to “Yes” if the departure of any member of NVIDIA’s Board of Directors, other than Jensen Huang, through resignation or removal from the Board of Directors is officially announced between market creation and the specified date, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

For the purposes of this market, the specified person refers to any individual, other than Jensen Huang, who is a member of NVIDIA’s Board of Directors at any time between market creation and the specified date, including directors appointed after market creation. Announcements made before this market’s creation, including the announcement of Jensen Huang’s resignation from the Board of Directors, will not qualify.

The departure will be considered to be announced when the specified person, NVIDIA, or their authorized representatives publicly and definitively announce that the specified person has resigned or been removed, or will resign or be removed, as a member of NVIDIA’s Board of Directors. Such an announcement will qualify regardless of when the specified person formally leaves the position; resignation includes retiring or stepping down from the Board of Directors, and removal includes removal by NVIDIA’s stockholders. Departures from executive roles at NVIDIA (e.g., CEO) will not qualify if the specified person remains a member of the Board of Directors.

Only announcements which are framed as announcing a departure through resignation or removal intended to be effective within 18 months of the date of the announcement will qualify (e.g., neither “I will leave office in 2 years,” nor “I will not be in office forever,” would qualify).

Announcements of temporary leaves of absence, dated suspensions, indefinite suspensions, administrative leaves, or other temporary changes to the specified person’s duties in the specified position do not qualify.

The following do not qualify as public and definitive announcements: statements that the specified person is considering or open to departing; conditional statements that the specified person will depart only if certain conditions are met; the announcement of an offer of resignation that requires acceptance and remains pending or has been refused; media reports that the specified person plans to depart; statements made in sarcasm or jest; and statements by persons other than the specified person, the relevant authority governing the specified position, or their respective authorized representatives.

If the specified person holds the specified position on a predefined term with a scheduled end date, only announcements that they will depart the position prior to the scheduled end of their term qualify.

If the specified person formally ceases to hold the specified position before the end of their scheduled term through such a departure without a qualifying announcement, the market will resolve to “Yes” if a qualifying resignation or removal occurs by the specified date, 11:59 PM ET. If the specified person ceases to hold the specified position by any other means, this will not count toward the resolution of this market.

The primary resolution sources for this market will be official information from NVIDIA and its Board of Directors; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
