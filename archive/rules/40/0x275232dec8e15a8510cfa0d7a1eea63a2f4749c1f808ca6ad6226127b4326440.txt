This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team achieves promotion from the Ligue 2 to the Ligue 1 for the 2027-28 season at the conclusion of the current league season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Official promotion is defined as a team clinching a place in the Ligue 1 for the 2027-28 season through their final standing in the Ligue 2, via playoff victory, or by any other official league decision recognized by the Ligue 2 and Ligue 1.

If the 2026-27 Ligue 2 season is cancelled, postponed after June 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there are no teams confirmed for promotion within that timeframe, the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from the Ligue 2 and Ligue 1. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
