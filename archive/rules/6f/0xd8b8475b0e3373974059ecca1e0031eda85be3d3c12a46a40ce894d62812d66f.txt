This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team plays in the Grand Final at the PGL Major Singapore 2026, currently scheduled for November 24 to December 13, 2026 (the Playoffs are scheduled for December 10-13, 2026). Otherwise, the associated market will resolve to "No".

If at any point it becomes impossible for the listed team to reach the Grand Final (e.g. the team is eliminated before the Grand Final), the associated market will resolve to "No".

Teams are identified by organization. A team competing at this tournament under an alternative name required by local regulations (e.g. GOSH for BC.Game, THE ONE for 1win, BB Team for BetBoom, PVISION for PARIVISION) counts as the listed team.

If the PGL Major Singapore 2026 is canceled, postponed after January 12, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or the Grand Final participants have not been officially confirmed within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "No".

The resolution source for this market will be HLTV (https://www.hltv.org/events/8302/pgl-major-singapore-2026); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
