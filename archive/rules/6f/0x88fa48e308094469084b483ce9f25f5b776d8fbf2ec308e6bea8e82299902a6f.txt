This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team achieves promotion from the EFL Championship to the Premier League for the 2027-28 season at the conclusion of the current league season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Official promotion is defined as a team clinching a place in the Premier League for the 2027-28 season through their final standing in the EFL Championship, via playoff victory, or by any other official league decision recognized by the EFL Championship and Premier League.

If the 2026-27 EFL Championship season is cancelled, postponed after June 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there are no teams confirmed for promotion within that timeframe, the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from the EFL Championship and Premier League. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
