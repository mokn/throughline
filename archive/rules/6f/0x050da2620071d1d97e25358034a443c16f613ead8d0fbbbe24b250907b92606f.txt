This market will resolve to "Yes" if any team achieves mega creeps (destroying all six barracks of the opposing team, across all three lanes) in any game of the BLAST Slam IX Playoffs (the online double-elimination Playoffs stage), currently scheduled for November 26 to November 29, 2026. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Only completed official games of the Playoffs stage count (Upper Bracket, Lower Bracket, and Grand Final). Games from the Group Stage, Seeding Matches, and Last Chance stage do not count. Games that are remade or not completed, and games awarded by forfeit without being played, do not count.

This market may resolve to "Yes" as soon as a qualifying game is confirmed.

If the BLAST Slam IX Playoffs are canceled, or are postponed such that they have not concluded by December 29, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "No", unless a qualifying game has already occurred.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official match data from OpenDota (https://www.opendota.com) for the games of the BLAST Slam IX Playoffs; however, Dotabuff (https://www.dotabuff.com), Datdota (https://www.datdota.com), official broadcast footage, and a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
