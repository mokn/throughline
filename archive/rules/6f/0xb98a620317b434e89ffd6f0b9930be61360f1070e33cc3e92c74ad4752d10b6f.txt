This market will resolve to "Yes" if Theo officially launches a token by the specified date, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Only an official token launched by Theo after the creation of this market will qualify. Stablecoins, memecoins, LSTs, synthetic tokens and tokenized real-world assets will not count.

The token must be actively and publicly tradable. Announcements alone do not qualify.

The primary resolution source for this market will be information from Theo (https://x.com/theo_xyz), however a consensus of credible reporting will also be used.
