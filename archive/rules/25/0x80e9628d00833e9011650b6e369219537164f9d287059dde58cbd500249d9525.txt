This market will resolve according to the winner of the Democratic nomination to contest the Mississippi governorship in 2027. The Democratic primary is scheduled to take place on August 3, 2027, with a runoff, if required, scheduled for August 31, 2027. If a runoff is held, this market will resolve according to the winner of the runoff.

If no nominee is announced by November 2, 2027, 11:59PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source for this market will be the first official announcement of the results from the Mississippi Democratic Party; however, an overwhelming consensus of credible reporting may suffice.
