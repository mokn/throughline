This market will resolve to “Yes” if the listed individual rings the bell at a qualifying bell ceremony on the day of Anthropic’s trading debut. Otherwise, this market will resolve to “No.”

Anthropic’s trading debut is the first day Anthropic’s Class A common stock trades on the exchange on which it is listed in the IPO (the “listing exchange”). The trading debut is set by the date of the first trade on the listing exchange, not by the IPO pricing date, and may fall one or more days after the pricing date. The sale of shares to investors at the IPO price, and any trading before the exchange listing (such as on private-share platforms or in pre-IPO or “when-issued” markets) will not be considered.

For this market, an IPO means an underwritten, SEC-registered offering of Anthropic’s Class A common stock, listed on a U.S. national securities exchange and priced as shown by the first final prospectus filed with the SEC under Rule 424(b). Direct listings, SPAC mergers, reverse mergers, private placements and non-U.S. listings will not count. An IPO counts as completed by December 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET if the pricing date on that prospectus is on or before that time. If Anthropic does not complete an IPO by December 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or announces a definitive agreement to be acquired or merged before completing one, this market will resolve to “No”.

If the IPO is completed by December 31, 2027 but Anthropic’s trading debut falls after that date, this market will remain open until the end of the day of Anthropic’s trading debut. If the IPO is priced but the shares never begin trading on the listing exchange, this market will resolve to “No”.

Bell-ringing ceremonies which take place outside the day of Anthropic’s trading debut will not be considered.

Qualifying Requirements:
- The listed individual must be physically present at the ceremony.
- The ceremony must be at the venue of the listing exchange.
- The purpose of the ceremony must be to commemorate the opening or closing of the listing exchange’s regular trading session on the day of Anthropic’s trading debut.
- The listed individual must singularly or jointly participate in ringing or activating the physical or digital bell of the listing exchange through touch or force.

Ringing the bell at any point during a qualifying ceremony will count, regardless of whether another individual rang it first.

Non-qualifying examples:
- The listed individual rings a bell at a private Anthropic ceremony.
- The listed individual tells someone else to ring the bell on their behalf.
- The listed individual issues a voice command to any device that it should play a ringing sound.
- The listed individual presses play on a ringing sound effect from their phone.
- The listed individual appears virtually at the bell ringing ceremony.
- The listed individual rings the bell from a location other than the listing exchange’s physical venue.
- The listed individual rings the bell at any time that is not part of the ceremony commemorating the opening or closing of the regular trading session (e.g., a ceremonial or photo-opportunity ring held before or after that ceremony, or at any other point during the day).

The resolution source will be a consensus of credible reporting including photographic or video evidence of the listed individual ringing the bell during the bell ringing ceremony.
