This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team is relegated from NB I (Hungary) at the conclusion of the 2026-27 NB I (Hungary) season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Official relegation is defined as a team losing its place in NB I (Hungary) for the following season through their final standing in the 2026-27 NB I (Hungary), via relegation playoff defeat, or by any other official league decision recognized by NB I (Hungary).

If the 2026-27 NB I (Hungary) season is cancelled, postponed after June 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there are no teams confirmed for relegation within that timeframe, the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from NB I (Hungary). A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
