This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team is relegated from Brazil Série A at the conclusion of the 2026 Brazil Série A season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Official relegation is defined as a team losing its place in Brazil Série A for the following season through their final standing in the 2026 Brazil Série A, via relegation playoff defeat, or by any other official league decision recognized by Brazil Série A.

If the 2026 Brazil Série A season is cancelled, postponed after December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or there are no teams confirmed for relegation within that timeframe, the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from Brazil Série A. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
