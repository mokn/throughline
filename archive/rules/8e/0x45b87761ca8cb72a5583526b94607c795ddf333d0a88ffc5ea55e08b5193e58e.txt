This market will resolve based on the total number of knife kills recorded across all completed maps of the PGL Major Singapore 2026, currently scheduled for November 24 to December 13, 2026.

A knife kill is any kill credited in-game to a player using the knife (melee) against an opponent. Kills made with any other weapon, including the Zeus x27 taser, do not count. Every knife kill in every completed official map of the event counts individually toward the total.

Only maps of the PGL Major Singapore 2026 main event count (Stage 1, Stage 2, Stage 3, and the Playoffs). Maps played in qualifiers or showmatches, and maps awarded by forfeit or administrative decision, do not count.

Only rounds that count toward a map's official result count toward the total. Kills during knife rounds played to decide starting sides, warmups, and rounds that are restarted or replayed do not count. The total is the sum of the knife kills credited in the official per-map statistics on HLTV, as shown at 11:59 PM ET on the day after the Grand Final is played; statistics revisions made after that time will not be considered.

The market resolves to the single range containing the final total number of knife kills at the conclusion of the event.

If the PGL Major Singapore 2026 is canceled, or is not completed such that its results are not determined by January 12, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other."

The resolution source for this market will be official information from HLTV (https://www.hltv.org/events/8302/pgl-major-singapore-2026); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
