This market will resolve to "Yes" if a committee or subcommittee of the US Senate votes to subpoena the listed individual between market creation and December 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

A vote to subpoena means a committee or subcommittee of the US Senate, including a select committee, approves by recorded vote, voice vote, or unanimous consent a subpoena naming the listed individual, or authorizes its chair to issue a subpoena naming the listed individual. A subpoena for documents, testimony, or both will qualify. The vote will count when it is taken, regardless of whether the subpoena is later signed, served, or complied with. A single vote that covers several individuals will count for each individual it names.

The following will not qualify: a subpoena issued by a chair or other member without a committee or subcommittee vote; a motion to subpoena that fails, is tabled, or is not put to a vote; a vote taken before market creation; a vote by a House committee; a subpoena addressed only to a company, agency, or office that does not name the listed individual; a court or grand jury subpoena; a vote that delegates subpoena authority generally without naming the listed individual; and a letter, request, or threat to subpoena, or a voluntary interview or deposition.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from the US Senate, including committee records and announcements; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
