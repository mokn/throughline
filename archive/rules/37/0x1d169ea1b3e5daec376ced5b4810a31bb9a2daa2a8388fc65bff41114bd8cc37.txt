This market will resolve to the listed nationality of the player who finishes as the top goalscorer of the 2026-27 Serie A season (the Capocannoniere).

A player's nationality will be the nationality listed for that player on the official Lega Serie A website (legaseriea.it). If a player is listed with multiple nationalities, the nationality of the national team the player is affiliated with will be used. If the top goalscorer's nationality is not among the listed options, this market will resolve to "Other".

If multiple players tie for the most goals, this market will resolve to the nationality of the tied player who has more assists, if a tie persists, this market will resolve to the tied player whose listed last name comes first alphabetically.

If the 2026-27 Serie A season is cancelled, postponed after June 13, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or the top goalscorer cannot be determined within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source will be official statistics from Lega Serie A; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
