This market will resolve according to the team that wins Group D at the 2027 AFC Asian Cup.

If multiple teams tie as group winners, this market will resolve according to the official tiebreak procedure of the 2027 AFC Asian Cup.

If the 2027 AFC Asian Cup is cancelled, postponed after February 19, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there is otherwise no winner declared for this group within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from the AFC; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
