This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed club is determined as the champion of the USL Championship for the 2026 season. Otherwise, it will resolve to "No".

If at any point it becomes impossible for a listed participant to be named as the champion of USL Championship for the 2026 season per the rules of USL Championship (e.g., the participant is eliminated), the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

If multiple teams are declared winners, this market will resolve in favor of the team whose listed name comes first alphabetically.

If this event is cancelled, postponed after December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or a champion has not been declared within this timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source will be official information from the USL Championship; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
