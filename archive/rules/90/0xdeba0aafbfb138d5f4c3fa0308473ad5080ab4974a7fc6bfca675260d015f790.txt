This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team is relegated from Chile Primera at the conclusion of the 2026 Chile Primera season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Official relegation is defined as a team losing its place in Chile Primera for the following season through their final standing in the 2026 Chile Primera, via relegation playoff defeat, or by any other official league decision recognized by Chile Primera.

If the 2026 Chile Primera season is cancelled, postponed after December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or there are no teams confirmed for relegation within that timeframe, the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from Chile Primera. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
