This market will resolve to the first player to win his first men's major championship in 2027. The men's major championships are the Masters Tournament, the PGA Championship, the U.S. Open and The Open Championship.

A player wins his first major if he had not won any men's major championship before the 2027 major he wins. If no player wins his first major championship in 2027, this market will resolve to "None".

Any 2027 major championship that is cancelled, or not completed by December 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, will not be considered. If no 2027 major championship is completed by that time, this market will resolve to "None".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from Augusta National Golf Club, the PGA of America, the USGA and The R&A; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
