This market will resolve to “Yes” if the listed player is selected as a 2027 NBA All-Star. Otherwise, this market will resolve to “No”.

This market will resolve based on the first official announcement of the players selected to the 2027 NBA All-Star rosters, including any players selected outside the voting process to complete the rosters. Subsequent changes or substitutions made after the first official announcement of completed rosters will not be considered.

Only selections to an official NBA All-Star roster will count. Selections to participate in the 3-Point Contest, Slam Dunk Contest, or other NBA All-Star weekend events will not affect the resolution of this market.

If the 2027 NBA All-Star rosters are not announced by February 21, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to “No”.

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the NBA; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
