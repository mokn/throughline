The Voice is a reality singing competition in which artists are coached by celebrity musicians. This market will resolve to the winning artist of The Voice: Season 30.

If multiple winners are announced, this market will resolve in favor of the listed contestant whose name comes first in alphabetical order.

If The Voice: Season 30 concludes without a winner being declared, or if The Voice: Season 30 has otherwise not concluded by March 31, 2027, 11:59PM ET this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source for this market will be the official broadcast of the final episode of The Voice: Season 30.
