This market will resolve to "Yes" if Riot Games officially announces the listed player as an inductee into the League of Legends Esports Hall of Legends for 2027 between market creation and December 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Only inductees named as part of the 2027 Hall of Legends class will qualify. Inductees announced for any other year will not count. If more than one player is inducted for 2027, each corresponding market will resolve to "Yes".

Reports, rumors, or leaks of an induction will not qualify; only an official announcement by Riot Games or LoL Esports will count.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from Riot Games or LoL Esports (https://lolesports.com); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
