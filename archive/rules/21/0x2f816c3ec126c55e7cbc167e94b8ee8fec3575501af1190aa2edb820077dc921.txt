This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed club finishes in the top 10 of the final standings of the 2026-27 English Premier League (EPL) season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

If at any point it becomes impossible for the listed club to finish in the top 10 of the 2026-27 English Premier League (EPL) season (e.g., they are mathematically unable to achieve enough points), this market will resolve to "No".

In the event of a tie for the final top-10 position, this market will resolve based on the clubs officially recognized by the Premier League as finishing in the top 10 of the final standings.

If the 2026-27 English Premier League (EPL) season is cancelled, postponed after June 13, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there are otherwise no final standings declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official information from the Premier League; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
