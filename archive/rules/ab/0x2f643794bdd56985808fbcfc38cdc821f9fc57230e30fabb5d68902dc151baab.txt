This market will immediately resolve to “Yes” if any Bybit 1-minute candle for Sui (SUI/USDT) between the creation of this market and December 31, 2026, 23:59 ET has a final “High” price equal to or greater than the price specified in the title. Otherwise, the market will resolve to “No.”

The resolution source for this market is Bybit, specifically the SUI/USDT “High” prices available at:
https://www.bybit.com/en/trade/spot/SUI/USDT
with the chart set to “1m” (one-minute candles) on the top bar.

Only the Bybit SUI/USDT price feed will be used.
Prices from other exchanges, trading pairs, or spot markets will not be considered.
