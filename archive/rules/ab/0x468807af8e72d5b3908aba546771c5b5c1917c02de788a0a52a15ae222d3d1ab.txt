This market will resolve based on the date, in Eastern Time, that Anthropic launches its Initial Public Offering (IPO).

An IPO refers to the first public sale of shares by the listed company on a recognized stock exchange.

If no IPO occurs by November 30, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, the market will resolve to "No IPO by November 30, 2026".

The primary resolution source for this market will be the official listing page of the relevant exchange; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
