This market will resolve according to the next person, other than Amy Klobuchar, to hold the United States Senate seat from Minnesota currently held by Amy Klobuchar (Class I).

A person appointed or elected to the seat will be considered to hold it once they have been sworn in. Interim, acting, or other temporary Senators will count.

If no person other than Amy Klobuchar has held the seat by March 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other."

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from the U.S. Senate and the Government of Minnesota; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
