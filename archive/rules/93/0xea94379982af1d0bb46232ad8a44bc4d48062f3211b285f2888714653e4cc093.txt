This market will resolve to "Yes" if there is a military encounter between Iraqi government forces and Kataib Hezbollah between market creation and the specified date, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

A "military encounter" is defined as any incident involving the use of force, such as an exchange of gunfire, missile, rocket, drone, or artillery fire, airstrikes, or other forms of direct military engagement between Iraqi government forces and Kataib Hezbollah. An attack by either side on the other will qualify. Non-violent actions, such as standoffs, deployments, arrests or raids carried out without the use of force, the firing of warning shots, or cyberattacks, will not qualify.

"Iraqi government forces" refers to Iraqi security forces acting under the authority of the Iraqi government, including the Iraqi Army, the Federal Police, the Energy Police, the Counter-Terrorism Service, other security forces under the Iraqi Ministry of Defense or Ministry of Interior, units of the Popular Mobilization Forces (PMF) other than those affiliated with Kataib Hezbollah, and any joint security command made up of these forces, such as the joint security headquarters established in Jurf al-Sakhar in November 2026. "Kataib Hezbollah" refers to the armed group Kataib Hezbollah and its members, including PMF units affiliated with it (the PMF's 45th, 46th, and 47th Brigades).

The attribution of an incident to Iraqi government forces and to Kataib Hezbollah will be determined by official information from the Iraqi government and a consensus of credible reporting.

The resolution source for this market will be a consensus of credible reporting.
