This market will resolve to "Yes" if Mauricio Pochettino ceases to be head coach of the United States men's national team before the first match of the 2027 Concacaf Gold Cup, scheduled to begin on June 19, 2027. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Pochettino signed a contract extension in August 2026 reported to run through the 2030 FIFA World Cup. Ceasing to be head coach includes being dismissed, resigning, departing by mutual agreement, or leaving for another role.

A temporary absence for illness, compassionate leave or suspension does not count while Pochettino remains under contract as head coach.

If the 2027 Concacaf Gold Cup is cancelled or postponed beyond July 31, 2027, this market will resolve based on whether Pochettino has ceased to be head coach by June 19, 2027, 11:59 PM ET.

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from U.S. Soccer. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
