This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team qualifies for the 2027 season of the League of Legends Championship Pacific (LCP) through the LCP 2027 Promotion, currently scheduled for November 10 to November 13, 2026, in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

The LCP 2027 Promotion has two paths, Regional Merit and Free-for-All, and the winner of each path qualifies for the 2027 LCP season. A team qualifies if it wins its path and is officially confirmed as qualified for LCP 2027 Split 1, per official tournament rules. Qualification through any route other than the LCP 2027 Promotion (e.g. a later in-season qualifier in 2027) does not count.

If at any point it becomes impossible for the listed team to qualify through the LCP 2027 Promotion (e.g. it is eliminated or withdraws), the associated market will resolve to "No".

If the LCP 2027 Promotion is canceled, postponed after December 13, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or the qualified teams have not been determined within this timeframe, this market will resolve to "No".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the tournament organizer, Riot Games, via the LoL Esports website (https://lolesports.com). However, a consensus of credible reporting (e.g., Liquipedia at https://liquipedia.net/leagueoflegends/LCP/2027/Promotion) may also be used.
