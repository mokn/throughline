This market will resolve according to the club that wins the 2026 Korean Series (the KBO League championship series).

If at any point it becomes impossible for a listed club to win the 2026 Korean Series (e.g., they are eliminated), the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

If the 2026 KBO postseason is cancelled, postponed after November 30, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or there is otherwise no Korean Series champion declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source will be official information from the KBO (https://www.koreabaseball.com); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
