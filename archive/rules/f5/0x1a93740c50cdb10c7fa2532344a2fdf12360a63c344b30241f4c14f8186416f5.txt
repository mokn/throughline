This market will resolve “Yes” if the listed team reaches the 2027 UEFA Europa League Round of 16. 

If at any point it becomes impossible for the listed club to advance to the UEFA Europa League Round of 16 (e.g. they are mathematically eliminated), the associated market will resolve to "No".

If the 2027 UEFA Europa League is cancelled, postponed after March 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET or the 2027 UEFA Europa League Round of 16 matchup has not been declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to “No”.

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the UEFA; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
