This market will resolve in favor of the team that finishes the 2026-27 NFL regular season with the most return yards (yards tracked across kickoff and punt returns).

In the event of a tie, this market will resolve in favor of the tied team with more return touchdowns during the 2026-27 NFL regular season. If a tie still persists, this market will resolve in favor of the team with a higher average yards per return during the 2026-27 NFL regular season. If a tie still persists, this market will resolve to team whose listed name comes first alphabetically.

If the 2026-27 NFL season concludes early, is shortened, or is truncated for any reason, the outcome shall be determined using available NFL statistics for completed games.

If the 2026-27 NFL regular season is cancelled, postponed after January 26, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or it is unclear which team led the NFL in return yards within that timeframe, this market will resolve to “Other”.

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the NFL; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
