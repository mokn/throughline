Departure from any other office held by a specified individual will not qualify.

The individual must formally cease to hold the specified office in accordance with the laws, rules, or procedures applicable to that position. Announcements of a future departure do not qualify. The means of departure do not impact settlement; resignation, removal, dismissal, or other means all qualify. 

Temporary leaves of absence, dated suspensions, indefinite suspensions, administrative leaves, or other temporary changes to the specified person’s duties in the specified office do not qualify. If an individual continues in a caretaker or interim role they will not be considered to have ceased occupying the office.

The primary resolution sources for this market will be official information by the relevant individual and the respective country’s government; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
