This market will resolve to the player who wins the 2026-27 UNFP Ligue 1 Player of the Year award (Trophées UNFP).

In the event of a tie, this market will resolve according to the official winner as determined by the UNFP. If multiple winners are announced, this market will resolve to the player whose listed last name comes first alphabetically.

If the 2026-27 Ligue 1 season is cancelled, or there is otherwise no such award announced by June 12, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the UNFP (https://www.ligue1.com); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
