This market will resolve to "Yes" if a UEFA disciplinary body officially announces its decision on whether FC Barcelona breached UEFA's regulations in connection with FC Barcelona's payments to José María Enríquez Negreira, former vice-president of the Technical Committee of Referees of the Royal Spanish Football Federation, or to companies linked to him (commonly referred to as the "Negreira case") by the specified date, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

An official decision of the UEFA Control, Ethics and Disciplinary Body or the UEFA Appeals Body on the merits of the case will count, whether it finds FC Barcelona in breach or clears the club, and regardless of whether it is later appealed. A decision on FC Barcelona's eligibility for UEFA club competitions on the basis of these payments, or a formal decision to close the proceedings, will also count. If decisions are announced in stages, the first qualifying decision will count. The opening, continuation, or suspension of an investigation or proceedings, reports or recommendations by the UEFA Ethics and Disciplinary Inspectors, interim or provisional measures, rulings by Spanish courts or any other body, and reports or leaks of an expected decision will not count.

The resolution source for this market will be official information from UEFA or FC Barcelona; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
