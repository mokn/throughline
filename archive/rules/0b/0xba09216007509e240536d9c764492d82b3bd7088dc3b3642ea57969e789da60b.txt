This market will resolve to “OpenAI” if OpenAI, or an authorized representative of OpenAI, publicly and officially announces that OpenAI has created an artificial general intelligence or that OpenAI’s AI models constitute artificial general intelligence before Anthropic does so. This market will resolve to “Anthropic” if Anthropic, or an authorized representative of Anthropic, publicly and officially announces that Anthropic has created an artificial general intelligence or that Anthropic’s AI models constitute artificial general intelligence before OpenAI does so.

If neither company makes a qualifying announcement between market creation and December 31, 2029, 11:59 PM ET, or if both make qualifying announcements on the same calendar day, this market will resolve 50-50. A qualifying announcement by any other company does not end the race or affect resolution.

A qualifying announcement must be made through official channels, by an individual acting in an official capacity. Statements made incidentally or informally in a context not intended for official communication do not qualify.

A qualifying announcement must clearly communicate that the relevant company has attained artificial general intelligence or that its models constitute artificial general intelligence. Statements that merely allude to, reference, or describe artificial general intelligence, without communicating that it has been achieved, do not qualify.

The following do not qualify as official announcements:
Anonymous, unattributed, or leaked statements not confirmed as official;
Statements by persons not authorized to speak for the relevant company;
Third-party speculation, analysis, or predictions that either company will announce AGI;
Satirical, fabricated, hacked, or impersonated communications;

The resolution source for this market will be official information from OpenAI and Anthropic and the official representatives of OpenAI and Anthropic.
