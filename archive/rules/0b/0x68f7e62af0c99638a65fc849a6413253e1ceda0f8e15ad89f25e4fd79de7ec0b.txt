This market will immediately resolve to “Yes” if any Binance 1-minute candle for Ethena (ENA/USDT) between the creation of this market and December 31, 2026, 23:59 ET has a final "High" or "Low" price equal to or beyond (above for ↑ High Prices, below for ↓ Low Prices) the listed price. Otherwise, the market will resolve to “No”.

The resolution source for this market is Binance, specifically the ENA/USDT “High” and "Low" prices available at:
https://www.binance.com/en/trade/ENA_USDT
with the chart set to “1m” (one-minute candles) on the top bar.

Only the Binance ENA/USDT price feed will be used.
Prices from other exchanges, trading pairs, or spot markets will not be considered.
