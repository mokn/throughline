This market will resolve to "Yes" if former Malaysian Prime Minister Najib Razak is released from house arrest by the date listed in the question, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

For purposes of this market, Najib Razak will be considered to have been released from house arrest only if he is no longer subject to house arrest and is not physically held in any Malaysian detention facility, including any prison, remand facility, or other place of formal custody, for a continuous period of at least 24 hours. 

A temporary release for medical treatment, a release on bail that is contingent on return to custody, or a release from one case while remaining in custody under another will not qualify. A return to prison or another place of formal custody will not qualify. 

Release with travel restrictions or with other non-custodial conditions following release will not prevent this market from resolving to "Yes".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the State of Malaysia or Najib Razak; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
