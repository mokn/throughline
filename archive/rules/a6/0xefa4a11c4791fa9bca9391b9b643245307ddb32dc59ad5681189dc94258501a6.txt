This market will resolve to "Yes" if Lionel Messi signs a contract extension or a new contract with Inter Miami CF that keeps him at the club beyond the expiry of his current contract, and this is officially confirmed by December 31, 2028, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Messi's current contract with Inter Miami is reported to run through 2028. A qualifying extension means an official confirmation by Inter Miami CF, by Messi, or by his official representatives of a contract that keeps him at the club beyond that expiry.

If Messi joins a different club rather than extending with Inter Miami, or retires, this market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source for this market will be official announcements from Inter Miami CF or Lionel Messi; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
