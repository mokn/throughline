This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team achieves promotion from the 2. Bundesliga to the Bundesliga for the 2027-28 season at the conclusion of the current league season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Official promotion is defined as a team clinching a place in the Bundesliga for the 2027-28 season through their final standing in the 2. Bundesliga, via playoff victory, or by any other official league decision recognized by the 2. Bundesliga and Bundesliga.

If the 2026-27 2. Bundesliga season is cancelled, postponed after June 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there are no teams confirmed for promotion within that timeframe, the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from the 2. Bundesliga and Bundesliga. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
