This market will resolve according to which of the listed streamers is the first to finish the main story of Grand Theft Auto VI live on stream between market creation and December 31, 2026 at 11:59 PM ET. Completions by any streamer not listed in this market will not be considered.

In the event that multiple listed streamers finish the main story simultaneously, this market will resolve in favor of the listed streamer, among those who finished simultaneously, whose name is first in alphabetical order.

In the event that none of the listed streamers finishes the main story of Grand Theft Auto VI live on stream by December 31, 2026 at 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Nobody".

A streamer will be considered to have finished the main story of Grand Theft Auto VI once the end credits that follow its conclusion begin to roll. If the game has multiple possible endings, reaching the end credits after any of them will qualify. Skipping the credits once they have begun to roll will not affect this market. Completion of optional missions, side activities, or any other content beyond the main story is not required.

Only gameplay by each listed streamer broadcast live on their official Kick channels will count. Footage of off-stream or previously recorded gameplay will not qualify.

The primary resolution source for this market will be footage from the listed streamers' official broadcasts on Kick, however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
