This market will resolve to "Yes" if a team competing under the G2 Esports organization wins a VALORANT Champions Tour (VCT) international event held in 2027. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

VCT international events are VCT 2027 Masters Seoul (scheduled for February 20 to March 7, 2027), VCT 2027 Masters Valencia (scheduled for June 5 to 20, 2027), and VALORANT Champions 2027 (scheduled for September 16 to October 10, 2027), plus any other Masters or Champions event Riot Games holds in 2027. Regional events (e.g. Kickoff, Cups, or Open Qualifiers) and third-party events (e.g. the Esports World Cup) do not count. A team wins an event if it wins the event's Grand Final, per official results.

The players on the G2 Esports roster do not matter; only the VALORANT team entered by the G2 Esports organization counts. If an event is canceled, it will not count.

If at any point it becomes impossible for a team competing under the G2 Esports organization to win a VCT international event held in 2027 (e.g. the team is eliminated from, or fails to qualify for, every remaining event), this market will resolve to "No".

If VALORANT Champions 2027 is not completed by November 9, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve based on the VCT international events completed by that time.

The resolution source for this market will be official information from Riot Games (via valorantesports.com); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
