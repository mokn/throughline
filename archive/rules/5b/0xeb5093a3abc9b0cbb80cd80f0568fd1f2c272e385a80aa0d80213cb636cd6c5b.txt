This market will resolve according to the winner of the 2026 Conference USA Football Championship Game, determining the champion of Conference USA for the 2026 college football season, currently scheduled for December 4, 2026.

If this championship is postponed after December 18, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, canceled, or a champion has not been declared within this timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other."

If multiple teams are declared co-champions, this market will resolve in favor of the team whose listed name comes first alphabetically.

The resolution source for this market will be official information from Conference USA (https://conferenceusa.com); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
