This market will resolve to "Yes" if SpaceX's total launches for the three months ended December 31, 2026, as reported in SpaceX's official company earnings materials for the fourth fiscal quarter of 2026, is above the listed amount. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

SpaceX typically reports total launches as "Total launches (#)" in the Space segment table of its earnings release.

The specified metric will be considered as reported in the company's official earnings materials. Subsequent revisions will not be considered.

If SpaceX's official earnings materials for the fourth fiscal quarter of 2026 are released, and the specified metric is not included, this market will resolve to "No".

If SpaceX does not release earnings materials for the fourth fiscal quarter of 2026 by April 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "No".

If the specified metric is reported as a range rather than a specific number, the midpoint of the range will be used for resolution of this market.

The resolution source for this market is SpaceX's official company earnings materials for the fourth fiscal quarter of 2026, including press releases, investor presentations, and regulatory filings (including the Annual Report on Form 10-K). If the specified metric is not reported in these materials, recordings or transcripts of the company's earnings webcast may also be used.

Note: This market will resolve based on the most numerically precise version of the specified metric reported in the company's official earnings materials. Total launches refers to the figure SpaceX reports as "Total launches (#)" for the three months ended December 31, 2026, which includes customer and internal launches and Falcon and Starship launches. Customer, internal, Falcon or Starship launches reported on their own, full-year or other-period figures, and figures calculated from other periods will not be considered. Alternate metrics that differ in definition or scope will not be considered.
