This market will resolve to “Yes” if DeepSeek is added to the Entity List (Supplement No. 4 to Part 744 of the Export Administration Regulations, 15 CFR Part 744) by the US Department of Commerce’s Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS) between market creation and the specified date, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to “No”.

A qualifying addition must be made through a rule published in the Federal Register, including a rule made available for public inspection on the Federal Register website, or officially announced by BIS or the Department of Commerce, within this market’s timeframe. The rule’s effective date does not need to fall within this market’s timeframe.

DeepSeek refers to Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Basic Technology Research Co., Ltd. An Entity List entry naming DeepSeek, any of its subsidiaries or parent companies, or any alias of these will qualify.

An addition will qualify regardless of whether it is later modified, retracted, or withdrawn. Additions to any other US government list, including the Department of Defense Section 1260H Chinese Military Companies list, the Treasury SDN List, or the Unverified List, will not qualify.

The primary resolution source for this market will be the Federal Register and official information from BIS; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
