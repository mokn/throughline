This market will resolve in favor of the team that finishes the 2026 NCAA FBS regular season with the most total yards gained per game.

In the event of a tie, this market will resolve in favor of the team with more total points scored during the 2026 NCAA FBS regular season. If a tie still persists, this market will resolve in favor of the team with more total yards gained. If a tie still persists, this market will resolve to "Other".

If the 2026 NCAA FBS season concludes early, is shortened, or is truncated for any reason, the outcome shall be determined using available NCAA FBS statistics for completed games.

If the 2026 NCAA FBS regular season is cancelled, postponed after January 3, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or it is unclear which team gained the most total yards per game within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the NCAA (https://www.ncaa.com/stats/football/fbs); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
