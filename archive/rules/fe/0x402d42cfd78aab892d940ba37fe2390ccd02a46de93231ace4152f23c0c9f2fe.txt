This market will resolve to "Yes" if HLTV records the listed player as winning at least one 1v5 clutch at the PGL Major Singapore 2026 (Stage 1, Stage 2, Stage 3, and the Playoffs), currently scheduled for November 24 to December 13, 2026. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

HLTV lists the PGL Major Singapore 2026 as three event pages: Stage 1 (https://www.hltv.org/events/9303/pgl-major-singapore-2026-stage-1), Stage 2 (https://www.hltv.org/events/9304/pgl-major-singapore-2026-stage-2), and the main event covering Stage 3 and the Playoffs (https://www.hltv.org/events/8302/pgl-major-singapore-2026). A 1v5 clutch counts if it appears in HLTV's clutch statistics for the listed player ("1on5" clutches won) for any of these three events, as shown at 11:59 PM ET on the day after the Grand Final is played. Statistics revisions made after that time will not be considered.

Only rounds that begin after this market was created count. Round timing will be determined from HLTV's match pages.

The team the listed player represents does not matter. A 1v5 clutch counts for any team the listed player plays for at the PGL Major Singapore 2026. If the listed player does not play in any round at the event, this market will resolve to "No".

If the PGL Major Singapore 2026 is canceled, postponed after January 12, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or HLTV's clutch statistics for the event are not available by January 12, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "50-50".

The resolution source for this market will be HLTV (https://www.hltv.org/).
