This market will resolve to "Yes" if at least one of the listed Asia-region teams qualifies for the Playoffs (the 8-team single-elimination bracket) by finishing in the top 8 of Stage 3 at the PGL Major Singapore 2026, currently scheduled for November 24 to December 13, 2026 (Stage 3 is scheduled for December 2-6, 2026). Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

For this market, the Asia-region teams are the tournament participants listed in the Asia region of the Valve Regional Standings (VRS): The MongolZ, GOSH (BC.Game), FlyQuest, Chinggis Warriors, and Lynn Vision. No other team counts.

If at any point it becomes impossible for every listed team to qualify for the Playoffs (e.g. all listed teams are eliminated in Stage 1, Stage 2, or Stage 3), this market will resolve to "No".

If the PGL Major Singapore 2026 is canceled, postponed after January 12, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or the teams that qualified for the Playoffs have not been officially confirmed within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "No".

The resolution source for this market will be HLTV (https://www.hltv.org/events/8302/pgl-major-singapore-2026); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
