This market will resolve to "HG" if the domestic gross for The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping on its opening weekend is greater than the domestic gross for Dune: Part Three on its opening weekend.

This market will resolve to "Dune" if the domestic gross for Dune: Part Three on its opening weekend is greater than the domestic gross for The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping on its opening weekend.

If the figures for both opening weekends are exactly equal, this market will resolve to 50-50. If either film fails to be released by December 31, 2026, 11:59PM ET, this market will resolve to 50-50.

This market will resolve as soon as final (i.e., not studio estimates) opening weekend figures for both movies are available, and no later than January 20, 2027, 11:59 PM ET. For each movie, the primary resolution source is the opening weekend figure in the "Weekend Box Office Performance" table on the "Box Office" tab of that movie's The Numbers page ("The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping": https://www.the-numbers.com/movie/Hunger-Games-The-Sunrise-on-the-Reaping-(2026)#tab=summary; "Dune: Part Three": https://www.the-numbers.com/movie/Dune-Part-Three-(2026)#tab=summary). If The Numbers does not have a final figure for a movie by January 20, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, that movie's "Weekend" figure for its first weekend on the "Domestic Weekend" tab of its Box Office Mojo (https://www.boxofficemojo.com/) release page will be used.

If neither source has a final figure for a movie by January 20, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, that movie's figure will be the one The Numbers reports at that time, or, if The Numbers reports none, the one Box Office Mojo reports at that time. If neither source reports any figure for either movie by January 20, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to 50-50.

This market will resolve according to the figures for the 3-day weekend (which typically includes Thursday's previews), regardless of whether domestic refers to only the USA, or to USA and Canada, etc.
