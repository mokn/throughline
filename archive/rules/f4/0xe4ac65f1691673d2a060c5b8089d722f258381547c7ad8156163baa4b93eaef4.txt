The winner of Eesti Laul 2027, ERR's national selection for the Eurovision Song Contest 2027, is scheduled to be announced on January 30, 2027.

This market will resolve to the artist ranked first in Eesti Laul 2027, according to the ranking below.

Artists will be ranked according to their official ranking in Eesti Laul 2027. If two or more artists share the same ranking, ties will be broken according to the official tie-breaking procedures of Eesti Laul 2027. If still tied, ties will be broken alphabetically by the artist's name as listed in this market. This market will resolve according to the artist ranked first under this ranking.

If the winner of Eesti Laul 2027 is not announced by February 28, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to the artist officially announced as Estonia's representative at the Eurovision Song Contest 2027, if that announcement is made by May 10, 2027, 11:59 PM ET. If no such announcement is made by May 10, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from ERR (https://eestilaul.err.ee/) and Eurovision (https://www.eurovision.com/), however a consensus of credible reporting will suffice.
