This market will resolve according to the team that wins Group A at the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualification.

If multiple teams tie as group winners, this market will resolve according to the official tiebreak procedure of the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualification.

If the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualification is cancelled, postponed after April 13, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there is otherwise no winner declared for this group within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from CAF; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
