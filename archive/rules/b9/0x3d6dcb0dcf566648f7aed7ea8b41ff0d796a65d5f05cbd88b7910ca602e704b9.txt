This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team is relegated from LigaPro Serie A (Ecuador) at the conclusion of the 2026 LigaPro Serie A (Ecuador) season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Official relegation is defined as a team losing its place in LigaPro Serie A (Ecuador) for the following season through their final standing in the 2026 LigaPro Serie A (Ecuador), via relegation playoff defeat, or by any other official league decision recognized by LigaPro Serie A (Ecuador).

If the 2026 LigaPro Serie A (Ecuador) season is cancelled, postponed after December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or there are no teams confirmed for relegation within that timeframe, the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from LigaPro Serie A (Ecuador). A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
