This market will resolve to the club that records the most clean sheets in the league phase of the 2026-27 UEFA Champions League. A clean sheet is defined as a match in which the club concedes zero goals.

Only matches played in the league phase of the 2026-27 UEFA Champions League will be considered. Qualifying rounds, the play-off round, the knockout phase play-offs, and all knockout rounds will not be considered.

In the event of a tie, this market will resolve according to the club that conceded fewer total goals in the league phase. If a tie still persists, this market will resolve to the club whose listed name comes first alphabetically.

If the 2026-27 UEFA Champions League league phase is cancelled, postponed after February 28, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there is otherwise no official leader declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source will be official statistics from UEFA. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
