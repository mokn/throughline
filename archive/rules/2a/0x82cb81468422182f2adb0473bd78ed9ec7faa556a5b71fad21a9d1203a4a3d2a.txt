This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team achieves promotion from UEFA Nations League B to UEFA Nations League A for the 2028-29 UEFA Nations League at the conclusion of the 2026-27 competition. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Official promotion is defined as a team clinching a place in UEFA Nations League A for the 2028-29 UEFA Nations League through their final standing in the 2026-27 UEFA Nations League B league phase, via promotion/relegation play-off victory, or by any other official decision recognized by UEFA.

If the 2026-27 UEFA Nations League is cancelled, postponed after April 13, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there are no teams confirmed for promotion within that timeframe, the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from UEFA. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
