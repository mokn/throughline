This market will resolve according to which map is banned the most times across all official matches of the PGL Major Singapore 2026, currently scheduled for November 24 to December 13, 2026.

The Active Duty map pool for the PGL Major Singapore 2026 consists of Ancient, Anubis, Cache, Dust II (also known as Dust2), Inferno, Mirage, and Nuke. A map is "banned" when either team removes it during the map veto of an official match of the event (Stage 1, Stage 2, Stage 3, and the Playoffs). Each map can count at most once per match. Picks do not count as bans, and the map left over at the end of a veto (the decider) does not count as a ban. Vetoes for qualifiers held before Stage 1 do not count. Bans in matches that are forfeited or awarded without any map being played do not count.

In the case of a tie, this market will resolve to the tied map that is played in more completed maps across all official matches of the event. If a tie persists, this market will resolve to the tied map whose name comes first alphabetically. If the map banned the most times is not listed in this market (e.g., due to a map pool change), this market will resolve to "Other".

If the PGL Major Singapore 2026 is canceled or postponed such that it has not concluded by January 12, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source for this market will be the official match pages, map veto records, and map statistics on HLTV (https://www.hltv.org); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
