This market will resolve to "Yes" if the average Ornn B300 index value in January 2027 is above the specified price. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

The average Ornn B300 index value in January 2027 is the arithmetic mean of the finalized daily settled values of the Ornn OCPI-B300 index dated within January 2027, as published by Ornn, rounded to the nearest cent. A daily settled value is considered finalized once the following day's settled value is published. Revisions made after a value has been finalized will not be considered.

The resolution source for this market is Ornn (data.ornn.com), specifically the Ornn Compute Price Index for the B300 (OCPI-B300), currently displayed at https://data.ornn.com/markets/b300.

In the case that the Resolution Source publishes data, sufficient to settle a Market, that is clearly erroneous and the result of technical or operational error, or of unauthorized interference with the Resolution Source or its data, the Market may remain open for a period of up to seven (7) calendar days (ET) from the time of the expected original release, in anticipation of a correction by the Resolution Source. If the Resolution Source publishes a corrected value within that period, that value will be eligible for resolution.

If any value for January 2027 is not finalized by 11:59 PM ET on the 7th calendar day after January 2027 ends, this market will resolve using the finalized values available at that time.

If Ornn discontinues the OCPI-B300 index, the page becomes unavailable, or Ornn materially changes the index methodology or unit of measure, and as a result no finalized OCPI-B300 values dated within January 2027 are available by 11:59 PM ET on February 7, 2027, another credible source of B300 rental price data will be considered. If no such source is available, this market will resolve 50-50.
