This market will resolve according to the player who finishes Number 1 in the official PPA Men's end of year rankings for the 2026-27 Carvana PPA Tour season, as published by the Professional Pickleball Association at the conclusion of the season, including the 2027 PPA Tour Finals.

If the 2026-27 end of year rankings are not published, are cancelled, or a year-end Number 1 has not been determined by May 16, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

If multiple players are tied and officially recognized as co-Number 1, this market will resolve in favor of the player whose listed name comes first alphabetically.

The resolution source for this market will be the official end of year rankings as published by the Professional Pickleball Association (https://ppatour.com/); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
