This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team is relegated from Venezuelan Primera División at the conclusion of the 2026 Venezuelan Primera División season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Official relegation is defined as a team losing its place in Venezuelan Primera División for the following season through their final standing in the 2026 Venezuelan Primera División, via relegation playoff defeat, or by any other official league decision recognized by Venezuelan Primera División.

If the 2026 Venezuelan Primera División season is cancelled, postponed after December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or there are no teams confirmed for relegation within that timeframe, the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from Venezuelan Primera División. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
