This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team is determined as the champion of the Liga Nacional de Basquet for the 2026-27 season. Otherwise, it will resolve to "No".

If at any point it becomes impossible for a listed team to be named as the champion of the Liga Nacional de Basquet for the 2026-27 season per the rules of the Liga Nacional de Basquet (e.g., the team is eliminated), the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

If multiple teams are declared winners, this market will resolve in favor of the team whose listed name comes first alphabetically.

If this event is cancelled, postponed after July 21, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or a champion has not been declared within this timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source will be official information from the Liga Nacional de Basquet (https://www.laliganacional.com.ar/); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
