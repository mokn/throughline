This market will resolve according to the champion that is played in the most official games during the 2026 League of Legends World Championship (Worlds 2026), currently scheduled for October 15 to November 14, 2026.

A champion is "played" in a game when it is picked and played by either team in a completed official game of the tournament (Play-In, Swiss Stage, and Knockout Stage). Bans do not count as plays. Games that are remade, abandoned, or not completed do not count. Games awarded by forfeit without being played do not count.

In the case of a tie, this market will resolve to the tied champion with the higher win rate across the tournament. If a tie persists, this market will resolve to the tied champion with more bans across the tournament. If a tie still persists, this market will resolve to the champion whose name comes first alphabetically.

If a champion not listed in this market is the most played, this market will resolve to "Other".

If Worlds 2026 is canceled, or is postponed such that it has not concluded by December 14, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source for this market will be the tournament champion statistics on Leaguepedia (https://lol.fandom.com/wiki/2026_Season_World_Championship); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
