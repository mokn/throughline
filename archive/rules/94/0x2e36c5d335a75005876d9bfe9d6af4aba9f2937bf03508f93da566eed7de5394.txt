On November 19, 2026, Bloomberg reported that Apple is developing an autonomous AI agent with computer-use capabilities, including device control, app interactions, web browsing and coding (https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-19/apple-is-working-on-ai-agent-for-device-control-apps-browsing-and-coding).

This market will resolve to "Yes" if Apple releases an autonomous AI agent between market creation and the specified date, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

An autonomous AI agent is an AI product that, given a user's request, can independently plan and carry out multi-step tasks by operating the interface of the user's device, apps or websites, such as navigating between apps, browsing the web, or filling in and submitting forms. A qualifying agent may be released under any name, including as a feature of Siri, Apple Intelligence or another existing Apple product, and does not need to be identified as the agent described in the Bloomberg report.

Features that act only through integrations that apps provide to Apple, such as App Intents, app skills or Shortcuts, will not qualify. Products limited to a single type of task, such as coding, offered only through an API, or sold only to businesses will not qualify.

A qualifying agent must be released and publicly accessible, including via an open public beta, such as Apple's Beta Software Program, or open rolling waitlist signups. An announcement alone, a developer beta limited to Apple Developer Program members, a closed beta or any other form of private access will not suffice. Availability limited to certain devices, countries, languages or paid subscription plans will qualify.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from Apple, with additional verification from a consensus of credible reporting.
