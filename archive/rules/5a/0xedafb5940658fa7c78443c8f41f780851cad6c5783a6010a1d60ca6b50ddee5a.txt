This market will resolve based on the calendar month (ET) in which Rockstar Games officially releases a PC version of Grand Theft Auto VI (available for purchase or download on PC, including via the Rockstar Games Launcher, Steam, or Epic Games Store). The resolving month will be the calendar month (ET) containing the first day on which the PC version is available for purchase or download.

A "PC version" refers to a full commercial release of the game for Windows PC. Beta versions, early access, technical tests, or leaked/unofficial builds will not qualify for resolution.

If the PC version is released before January 1, 2027, 12:00 AM ET, this market will resolve to "Before January 2027".

If no PC version is released by March 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, the market will resolve to "After March 2027".

Announcements or scheduling of a PC release will not suffice; this market will resolve after the PC version has been released.

Resolution will be based on official information from Rockstar Games; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
