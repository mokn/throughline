This market will resolve to the player ranked No. 1 in the final Official World Golf Ranking (OWGR) published on or before December 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET.

If no Official World Golf Ranking is published between December 1, 2027 and December 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source for this market will be the Official World Golf Ranking (https://www.owgr.com/); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
