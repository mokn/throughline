This market will resolve to "Yes" if Zekken leaves MIBR at any point on or before December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

"Leaving MIBR" refers to Zekken no longer being a member of MIBR's active roster, whether by transfer to another organization, release, benching with removal from the active roster, retirement, or any other permanent departure from the team. A temporary stand-in absence or loan from which Zekken returns to MIBR's active roster will not count. A confirmed departure will qualify even if Zekken's next destination has not been announced.

If Zekken is not a member of MIBR's active roster as of this market's launch, the market will resolve to "No" unless he joins and subsequently leaves within the timeframe.

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from MIBR and Zekken, however a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.

Market resolves on December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or earlier upon a qualifying departure.
