This market will resolve according to the team that finishes as runner-up of the 2026-27 UEFA Conference League.

If at any point it becomes impossible for a listed team to finish as runner-up of the 2026-27 UEFA Conference League per the rules of UEFA Conference League (e.g., they are eliminated before the final, or they win the final), the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

If the 2026-27 UEFA Conference League season is cancelled, postponed after June 16, 2027, 11:59 PM ET or the final is otherwise not played within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source will be official information from UEFA Conference League; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
