This market will resolve to the player who wins the 2026-27 Bundesliga Player of the Season award.

In the event of a tie, this market will resolve according to the official winner as determined by the Bundesliga. If multiple winners are announced, this market will resolve to the player whose listed last name comes first alphabetically.

If the 2026-27 Bundesliga season is cancelled, or there is otherwise no such award announced by June 5, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the Bundesliga (https://www.bundesliga.com); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
