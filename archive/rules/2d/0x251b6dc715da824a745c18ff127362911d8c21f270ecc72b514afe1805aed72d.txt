This market will resolve to "Yes" if any map played during the PGL Major Singapore 2026 Playoffs, currently scheduled for December 10-13, 2026, goes to a third overtime period. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Under the tournament format (MR12 regulation with MR3 overtime), a map goes to a third overtime period if the score is tied 18-18 after regulation and two overtime periods, so that a third overtime period begins. A map that reaches a fourth or later overtime period also counts.

Only maps played during the Playoffs count. Maps played during Stage 1, Stage 2, or Stage 3 do not count. Maps awarded by forfeit or another administrative decision do not count.

If the PGL Major Singapore 2026 is canceled, or postponed after January 12, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "No".

The resolution source for this market will be HLTV (https://www.hltv.org/events/8302/pgl-major-singapore-2026) and the official PGL broadcast; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
