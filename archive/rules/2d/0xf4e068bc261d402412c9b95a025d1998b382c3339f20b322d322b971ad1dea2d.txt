This market will resolve in favor of the team that finishes the 2026-27 Liga Nacional (Guatemala) season with the most clean sheets. A clean sheet is defined as a match in which the team concedes zero goals.

In the event of a tie, this market will resolve in favor of the team with the fewest total goals conceded during the 2026-27 Liga Nacional (Guatemala) season. If a tie still persists, this market will resolve in favor of the team with the higher final standing in the 2026-27 Liga Nacional (Guatemala). If a tie still persists, this market will resolve to "Other".

Only regular season clean sheets will be considered for this market. Clean sheets recorded in playoff, relegation, postseason, preseason, or any other stage of competition will not be considered.

If the 2026-27 Liga Nacional (Guatemala) season concludes early, is shortened, or is truncated for any reason, the outcome shall be determined using available Liga Nacional (Guatemala) statistics for completed games.

If the 2026-27 Liga Nacional (Guatemala) season is cancelled, postponed after June 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or it is unclear which team led Liga Nacional (Guatemala) in clean sheets within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from Liga Nacional (Guatemala); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
