This market will resolve according to the team that wins Group C3 at the 2026-27 UEFA Nations League.

If multiple teams tie as group winners, this market will resolve according to the official tiebreak procedure of the 2026-27 UEFA Nations League.

If the 2026-27 UEFA Nations League is cancelled, postponed after December 1, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or there is otherwise no winner declared for this group within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from UEFA; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
