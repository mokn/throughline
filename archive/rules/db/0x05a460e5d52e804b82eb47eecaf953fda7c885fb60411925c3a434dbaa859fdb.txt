This market will resolve to "Yes" if Cristiano Ronaldo appears in at least one match for Portugal in the qualifying competition for UEFA Euro 2028. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

An appearance means taking the field, whether as a starter or as a substitute, for any length of time. Being named in the squad or on the bench without taking the field does not count. Matches at the UEFA Euro 2028 final tournament, matches in the UEFA Nations League, and friendly matches do not count.

If the UEFA Euro 2028 qualifying competition is cancelled, or Ronaldo has not appeared in a qualifying match by March 31, 2028, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "No".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from UEFA; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
