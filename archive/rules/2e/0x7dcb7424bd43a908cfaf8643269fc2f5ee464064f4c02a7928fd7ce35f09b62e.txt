This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team achieves promotion from the OBOS-ligaen (Norway) to the Norway Eliteserien for the 2027 season at the conclusion of the current league season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Official promotion is defined as a team clinching a place in the Norway Eliteserien for the 2027 season through their final standing in the OBOS-ligaen (Norway), via playoff victory, or by any other official league decision recognized by the OBOS-ligaen (Norway) and Norway Eliteserien.

If the 2026 OBOS-ligaen (Norway) season is cancelled, postponed after December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or there are no teams confirmed for promotion within that timeframe, the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from the OBOS-ligaen (Norway) and Norway Eliteserien. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
