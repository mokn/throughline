This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team is relegated from League of Ireland Premier Division at the conclusion of the 2026 League of Ireland Premier Division season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Official relegation is defined as a team losing its place in League of Ireland Premier Division for the following season through their final standing in the 2026 League of Ireland Premier Division, via relegation playoff defeat, or by any other official league decision recognized by League of Ireland Premier Division.

If the 2026 League of Ireland Premier Division season is cancelled, postponed after December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or there are no teams confirmed for relegation within that timeframe, the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from League of Ireland Premier Division. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
