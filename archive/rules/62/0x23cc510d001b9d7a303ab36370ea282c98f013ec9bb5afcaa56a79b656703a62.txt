This market will resolve to "Yes" if the Los Angeles Clippers win 60 or more games during the 2026-27 NBA regular season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Only wins in 2026-27 NBA regular-season games count. NBA Cup group-play and knockout games other than the NBA Cup Championship game are regular-season games and count. Preseason, NBA Cup Championship game, Play-In Tournament and playoff games do not count. The 2026-27 NBA regular season is scheduled to run from October 20, 2026 to April 11, 2027.

If at any point it becomes impossible for the Los Angeles Clippers to win 60 or more games during the 2026-27 NBA regular season (e.g. they are mathematically eliminated), this market will resolve to "No".

If the 2026-27 NBA regular season is cancelled, shortened, or postponed after April 25, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Yes" if the Los Angeles Clippers have won 60 or more regular-season games by that time, and otherwise to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official information from the NBA; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
