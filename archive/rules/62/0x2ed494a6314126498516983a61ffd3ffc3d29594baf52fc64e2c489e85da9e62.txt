This market will resolve to “Yes” if Benjamin Netanyahu’s departure through resignation or removal as Leader of Israel’s Likud party is officially announced between market creation and the specified date, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to “No”.

Announcements made before this market’s creation will not qualify.

The departure will be considered to be announced when Benjamin Netanyahu, Likud, or their authorized representatives publicly and definitively announce that Netanyahu has resigned or been removed, or will resign or be removed, as Leader of Israel’s Likud party. Such an announcement will qualify regardless of when the specified person formally leaves the position; resignation includes retiring or stepping down as Leader, including to take another role, and removal includes removal through a leadership review, the election of another Leader of Likud through a party leadership primary, or other action by Likud.

Only announcements which are framed as announcing a departure through resignation or removal intended to be effective within 18 months of the date of the announcement will qualify (e.g., neither “I will leave office in 2 years,” nor “I will not be in office forever,” would qualify).

Announcements of temporary leaves of absence, dated suspensions, indefinite suspensions, administrative leaves, or other temporary changes to the specified person’s duties in the specified position do not qualify.

The following do not qualify as public and definitive announcements: statements that the specified person is considering or open to departing; conditional statements that the specified person will depart only if certain conditions are met; the announcement of an offer of resignation that requires acceptance and remains pending or has been refused; media reports that the specified person plans to depart; statements made in sarcasm or jest; and statements by persons other than the specified person, the relevant authority governing the specified position, or their respective authorized representatives.

If the specified person holds the specified position on a predefined term with a scheduled end date, only announcements that they will depart the position prior to the scheduled end of their term qualify. The position of Leader of Likud will not be considered a predefined term with a scheduled end date for the purposes of this market.

If the specified person formally ceases to hold the specified position before the end of their scheduled term through such a departure without a qualifying announcement, the market will resolve to “Yes” if a qualifying resignation or removal occurs by the specified date, 11:59 PM ET. If the specified person ceases to hold the specified position by any other means, this market will resolve to “No”.

The primary resolution sources for this market will be official information from Benjamin Netanyahu and Likud; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
