This market will resolve to "Yes" if Anthropic officially announces a commitment to contribute shares of Anthropic to Trump Accounts by November 30, 2026, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Trump Accounts refers to the federal tax-advantaged investment accounts for American children created under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, which are seeded with a $1,000 government contribution for children born from 2025 through 2028. A commitment to contribute any amount of Anthropic shares or other equity in Anthropic to Trump Accounts will qualify, including shares held by the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund. Commitments to contribute only cash will not qualify.

A qualifying announcement must be issued by Anthropic, or by an Anthropic executive in a public statement, and must clearly and unambiguously communicate that Anthropic has committed to make such a contribution. A commitment will qualify even if it is subject to stockholder, regulatory, or other approvals. Statements expressing interest in, consideration of, or openness to such a contribution will not qualify. Statements made solely by third parties, including government officials or other companies, will not qualify unless confirmed by Anthropic.

Once a qualifying announcement is made, this market will resolve to "Yes" regardless of whether the commitment is later modified, withdrawn, or ever fulfilled.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from Anthropic; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
