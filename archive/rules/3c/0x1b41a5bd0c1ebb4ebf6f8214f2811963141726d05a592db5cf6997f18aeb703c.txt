This market will resolve according to the number of official PGA TOUR wins Scottie Scheffler records during the 2027 PGA TOUR season, including FedExCup Fall events.

Every tournament credited as an official PGA TOUR win counts toward the total, per the official win record published by the PGA TOUR. Results not credited as official PGA TOUR wins, including unofficial events and wins on LIV Golf, the DP World Tour or any other tour, do not count. Each bracket includes both listed numbers; for example, exactly 3 wins resolves to "2-3" and 8 or more wins resolves to "8+".

If Scottie Scheffler does not play in any 2027 PGA TOUR event, or all 2027 PGA TOUR events are cancelled, this market will resolve to "0-1".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the PGA TOUR (https://www.pgatour.com/); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
