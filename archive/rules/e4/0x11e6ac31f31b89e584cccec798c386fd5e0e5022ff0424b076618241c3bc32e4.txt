This market will resolve to "Yes" if a Russian diplomat is expelled from any European Union member state by the government of that state between market creation and the listed date, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Expulsions announced before this market's creation, including Denmark's expulsion of the Russian defence attaché announced on November 20, 2026, will not count toward this market's resolution.

For the purposes of this market, “expel” refers to a formal action by an EU member state requiring the Russian diplomat to leave that state, including a declaration of persona non grata. Revocation of the diplomat’s visa or denial of entry to the relevant state will not alone suffice.

An announcement of a qualifying expulsion will suffice for a “Yes” resolution, regardless of whether the diplomat actually leaves the country.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from the governments of the expelling country and Russia; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
