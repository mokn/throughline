This market will resolve to "Yes" if Javier "Dardo" Zafra is officially announced as the next permanent General Manager of Fnatic's League of Legends team by the listed date, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Appointments of "interim," "acting," or other non-permanent General Managers will not count. Appointments to any other role at Fnatic (e.g., Team Director, or a role outside Fnatic's League of Legends team) will not count unless the official announcement names him General Manager of Fnatic's League of Legends team.

An official announcement of the appointment made by the listed date will count toward a "Yes" resolution, regardless of when the appointment goes into effect.

If another person is officially announced as the next permanent General Manager of Fnatic's League of Legends team before Dardo is, this market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from Fnatic; however, a consensus of credible reporting will also be used.
