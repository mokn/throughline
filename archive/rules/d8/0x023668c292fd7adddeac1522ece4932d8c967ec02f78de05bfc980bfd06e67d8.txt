This market will resolve to “Yes” if Rockstar Games officially announces new single-player story content for Grand Theft Auto VI between market creation and the listed date, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to “No”.

“New single-player story content” refers to any new story missions, chapters, episodes, or story campaign for Grand Theft Auto VI's single-player mode (Story Mode) that were not available at the game's initial release, whether free or paid, and whether delivered as a title update, downloadable content, or an expansion. Content for GTA Online or any other multiplayer or online mode will not count.

For the purposes of this market, “announce” refers to an official public statement that identifies new single-player story content for Grand Theft Auto VI as planned, in development, or forthcoming. Rumors, leaks, or unofficial reports will not count as an official announcement.

The resolution source will be official information from Rockstar Games or its parent company, Take-Two Interactive.
