This market will resolve "Yes" if the listed team is crowned champion (wins the Grand Final) of at least one qualifying international VALORANT event concluding in the 2027 calendar year. Otherwise, this market will resolve "No."

For the purposes of this market, "qualifying international VALORANT event" means any of the following, and only the following:
VALORANT Masters Seoul 2027 (February 20 – March 3, 2027)
VALORANT Masters València 2027 (June 5 – June 20, 2027)
Esports World Cup 2027 — VALORANT (dates to be announced)
VALORANT Champions 2027, Austin (September 16 – October 10, 2027)

No other event counts.

Teams are identified by their organization. A change to a team's roster, name, branding, or sponsor does not affect this market.

In the case of a tie, the market will resolve to "Yes" for the team whose name appears first alphabetically.

This market resolves "Yes" as soon as the team wins any one qualifying event; winning more than one is not required. If the team wins none of the listed events, this market resolves "No."

If all qualifying events are cancelled, or are postponed such that no champion is determined for any of them by December 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "No".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from Riot Games via the VALORANT Esports website (https://valorantesports.com) and, for the Esports World Cup, the official EWC website (https://esportsworldcup.com). A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
