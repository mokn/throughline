This market will resolve to the player who records the most assists during the 2026-27 Serie A season.

Only assists recorded in Serie A matches will count. Assists in other competitions (e.g., domestic cups, UEFA Champions League, UEFA Europa League, UEFA Conference League, international matches) will not count for this market.

If multiple players tie for the most assists, this market will resolve to the tied player who completed more passes during the 2026-27 Serie A season. If a tie still persists, this market will resolve in favor of the tied player whose last name comes first alphabetically.

If the 2026-27 Serie A season is canceled or not completed by June 14, 2027, the market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source will be official statistics from Serie A. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
