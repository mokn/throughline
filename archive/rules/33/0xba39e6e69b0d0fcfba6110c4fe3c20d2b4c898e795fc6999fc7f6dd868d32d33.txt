This market will resolve to "Yes" if Cristiano Ronaldo scores his 1,000th career goal by the listed date, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Career goals will be counted according to ESPN's Cristiano Ronaldo goal tracker, which covers goals for his clubs and the Portugal senior men's national team in official competitive matches. Only goals the tracker credits to Cristiano Ronaldo will count toward his career total.

The 1,000th career goal will be considered scored on the date (ET) of the match in which it is scored, regardless of when the tracker is updated.

The primary resolution source for this market will be ESPN's Cristiano Ronaldo goal tracker (https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/46751020/cristiano-ronaldo-goal-tracker-road-1000-career-goals); however, if the tracker is unavailable or has not been updated, a consensus of credible reporting may be used.
