This market will resolve to "Yes" if City Protocol officially launches a token by 11:59 PM ET on the date specified in the title. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Only an official token launched by City Protocol will qualify. Stablecoins, memecoins, LSTs and synthetic tokens will not count.

The token must be actively and publicly tradable. Announcements alone do not qualify.

The primary resolution source for this market will be information from City Protocol (https://x.com/cityprotocolhq), however a consensus of credible reporting will also be used.
