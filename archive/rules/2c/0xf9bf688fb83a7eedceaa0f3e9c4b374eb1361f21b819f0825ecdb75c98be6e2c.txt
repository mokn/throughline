This market will resolve to “Yes” if a Real Madrid CF men’s first-team match is broadcast live on an official China Central Television (CCTV) channel between market creation and December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to “No”.

For the purposes of this market, a qualifying broadcast must air live, in full or in part, on an official CCTV television channel (e.g., CCTV-5 or CCTV-5+). Highlights, replays, delayed broadcasts, and streams available only on online platforms, including CCTV’s own online and mobile platforms such as Yangshipin (央视频), will not qualify.

Any match involving Real Madrid CF’s men’s first team, including league, cup, continental, and friendly matches, will qualify. Kylian Mbappé does not need to play in or appear on screen during the broadcast.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official CCTV broadcasting; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
