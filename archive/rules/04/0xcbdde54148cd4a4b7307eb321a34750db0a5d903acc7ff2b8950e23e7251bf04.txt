This market will resolve to “Yes” if the Vancouver Whitecaps of the MLS announce a relocation of the team’s home games to a permanent location outside of the Vancouver metropolitan area by December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to “No”.

A qualifying announcement will immediately result in a “Yes” resolution for this market, regardless of when the relocation is set to take place.

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the MLS and/or the Vancouver Whitecaps; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
