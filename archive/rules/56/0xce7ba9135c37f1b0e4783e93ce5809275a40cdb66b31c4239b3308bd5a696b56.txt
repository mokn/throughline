The Senate Democratic Caucus is expected to hold its leadership election for the 120th United States Congress on December 1, 2026, including the election of the Senate Democratic Leader.

This market will resolve according to the number of votes cast for Chris Van Hollen for Senate Democratic Leader in the Senate Democratic Caucus leadership election for the 120th Congress.

Only votes cast for Chris Van Hollen on the first ballot for Senate Democratic Leader will count, regardless of whether he is elected on that ballot. Votes on procedural questions, such as motions to change the date or method of the election, are not ballots. If Chris Van Hollen receives no votes on the first ballot, including because he is not a candidate, this market will resolve to the lowest bracket.

If Chris Van Hollen is elected Senate Democratic Leader without a tally of votes cast for him being announced or reported (e.g., by acclamation or unanimous consent), this market will resolve to the highest bracket. If a person other than Chris Van Hollen is elected without such a tally being announced or reported, this market will resolve to the lowest bracket. If no ballot to elect the Senate Democratic Leader for the 120th Congress has taken place by January 3, 2029, 11:59 PM ET, or if it is otherwise not possible to resolve this market by such date due to ambiguity, this market will resolve to the lowest bracket.

The resolution sources for this market will be, in the following order of priority: (1) official announcements from the Senate Democratic Caucus; (2) Punchbowl News; (3) Politico; (4) The New York Times; (5) The Wall Street Journal; (6) The Washington Post; (7) Bloomberg; and (8) a consensus of credible reporting. If sources conflict, the highest-priority source that reports the number of votes cast for Chris Van Hollen will be used.
