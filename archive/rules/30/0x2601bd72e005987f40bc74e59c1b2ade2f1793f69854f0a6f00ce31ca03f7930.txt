This market will resolve to “Yes” if the season-to-date snowfall for New York City exceeds the season-to-date normal as of April 30, 2027. Otherwise, this market will resolve to “No”.

The qualifying data is the “SINCE JUL 1” line under “SNOWFALL (IN)” in the National Weather Service Daily Climate Report (CLI) for Central Park (KNYC; CLINYC, issued by NWS New York/Upton) covering April 30, 2027. This market resolves “Yes” if the OBSERVED value on that line is greater than the NORMAL value on that line. A tie, or a trace (“T”) observed value, resolves “No”. Normals are the 1991 to 2020 normals printed in the report, used exactly as printed. The observed value is also used exactly as printed, even if snowfall on a day the report shows as missing (“MM”) is not included in it; daily values will not be recounted.

The governing report is the first published CLI covering the full climate day of April 30, 2027, normally issued early on May 1, 2027. Reports issued on April 30, 2027 that cover only part of that day (marked “VALID TODAY AS OF” or “VALID AS OF” a local time) will not be considered.

This market will resolve once the governing report is published. If no governing report is published by 11:59 PM ET on May 3, 2027, this market will resolve 50-50.

The first published governing report is final, and its publication is the revision cutoff; later corrected or updated reports will not be considered, except a correction of a clearly erroneous value under the following paragraph.

If the Resolution Source publishes a clearly erroneous value because of a technical or operational error or unauthorized interference, this market may remain open for up to seven (7) calendar days ET awaiting a correction. A correction published in that window is eligible. Otherwise, Polymarket will issue a Clarification.

Revisions remain subject to the cutoff above. The only exception is a correction of a clearly erroneous value under the preceding paragraph, which will be used even if published after the cutoff.

Resolution Source: National Weather Service Daily Climate Report (CLI) for New York City (CLINYC), published at forecast.weather.gov: https://forecast.weather.gov/product.php?site=NWS&issuedby=NYC&product=CLI&format=CI&version=1&glossary=0. Archive of past reports: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/wx/afos/list.phtml?by=pil&pil=CLINYC
