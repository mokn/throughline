This market will resolve according to the date on which "Avengers: Doomsday" first reaches a cumulative domestic box office gross of at least $1 billion.

The date will be the first date for which the "Total Gross" figure in the "Daily Box Office Performance" table on the "Box Office" tab of this movie's The Numbers (https://www.the-numbers.com/) page is $1,000,000,000 or greater. If The Numbers does not have final figures by March 2, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve according to the first date for which the "To Date" figure on the "Domestic Daily" tab of this movie's Box Office Mojo (https://www.boxofficemojo.com/) release page is $1,000,000,000 or greater.

If "Avengers: Doomsday" does not reach a cumulative domestic gross of $1 billion on or before January 31, 2027, this market will resolve to "After January".

This market will resolve as soon as final (i.e., not studio estimates) figures establish which bracket the date falls in, and no later than March 2, 2027, 11:59 PM ET. If neither source has final figures by that time, this market will resolve according to the figures The Numbers reports at that time, or, if The Numbers reports none, the figures Box Office Mojo reports at that time.

Dates refer to the calendar day of box office grosses as listed by the resolution sources. Cumulative domestic gross is counted from the movie's opening (December 18, 2026), including any previews counted toward the opening day, regardless of whether domestic refers to only the USA, or to USA and Canada, etc.
