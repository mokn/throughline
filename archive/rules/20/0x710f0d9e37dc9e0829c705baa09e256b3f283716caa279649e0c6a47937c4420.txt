This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team finishes 15th in the 2026-27 Primeira Liga. Otherwise, the associated market will resolve to "No".

If at any point it becomes impossible for the listed team to finish 15th in the 2026-27 Primeira Liga, the associated market will resolve to "No".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from Primeira Liga; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
