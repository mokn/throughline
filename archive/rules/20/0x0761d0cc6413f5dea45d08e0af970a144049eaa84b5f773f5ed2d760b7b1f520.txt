A transaction counts when it becomes official, as confirmed by an official announcement from the relevant team or MLB. A transaction that remains subject to a physical or medical examination, league review, or any other contingency capable of voiding it is not official until that contingency is satisfied or can no longer void the transaction. If a trade or signing is voided or falls through before completion, it will not qualify for resolution. This market will resolve as soon as a qualifying transaction becomes official.

If the first team Teruaki Sato officially joins before March 25, 2027, 12:00 AM ET is not an MLB team, or if he does not officially join any team before that time (including if he remains with the Hanshin Tigers or retires), this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from MLB and/or the relevant team; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
