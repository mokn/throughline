This market will resolve to the club that finishes the league phase of the 2026-27 UEFA Champions League with the best goal differential.

Only matches played in the league phase of the 2026-27 UEFA Champions League will be considered. Qualifying rounds, the play-off round, the knockout phase play-offs, and all knockout rounds will not be considered.

In the event of a tie, this market will resolve according to the club that scored the most total goals in the league phase. If a tie still persists, this market will resolve to the club that is listed first alphabetically.

If the 2026-27 UEFA Champions League league phase is shortened, truncated, or ends early for any reason, the outcome shall be determined using available official data for completed matches.

If the 2026-27 UEFA Champions League league phase is cancelled, postponed after February 28, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there is otherwise no official leader declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from UEFA; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
