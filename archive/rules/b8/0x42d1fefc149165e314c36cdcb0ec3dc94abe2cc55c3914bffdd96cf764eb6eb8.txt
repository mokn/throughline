This market will resolve to "Yes" if Collapse plays in an official match of a qualifying Dota 2 tournament between market creation and December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

For the purposes of this market, a "qualifying tournament" is a Dota 2 tournament listed as Tier 1, Tier 2, or Tier 3 on Liquipedia. Playing as part of any team's lineup counts, including as a stand-in. Show matches, streamer or charity tournaments, and other tournaments not listed as Tier 1, Tier 2, or Tier 3 on Liquipedia will not count. An announcement of a return, or a move from inactive to active status without playing an official match, will not count on its own.

As of market creation, Collapse is on Team Spirit's inactive roster (since September 21, 2026).

The primary resolution source for this market will be Liquipedia (https://liquipedia.net/dota2/Collapse); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
