This market will resolve according to the listed actress who is officially announced as the next Bond girl by December 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET.

For the purposes of this market, the "next Bond girl" refers to the actress cast in the principal female lead role opposite James Bond in the next official James Bond film from Amazon MGM Studios. The role does not need to be described as a "Bond girl", and it is not defined by any specific character name; qualification is based on the actress being officially announced in the principal female co-lead role, such as Bond's primary love interest or female counterpart. Recurring supporting roles (e.g. M or Moneypenny) and minor or cameo roles will not qualify.

Examples from previous Bond films, for illustration of which roles would and would not qualify:
- Qualifying (principal female lead): Ursula Andress as Honey Ryder (Dr. No), Michelle Yeoh as Wai Lin (Tomorrow Never Dies), Halle Berry as Jinx (Die Another Day), Eva Green as Vesper Lynd (Casino Royale), Olga Kurylenko as Camille Montes (Quantum of Solace), and Léa Seydoux as Madeleine Swann (Spectre).
- Not qualifying: Judi Dench as M and Naomie Harris as Eve Moneypenny (recurring supporting roles); Caterina Murino as Solange (Casino Royale), Gemma Arterton as Strawberry Fields (Quantum of Solace), Monica Bellucci as Lucia Sciarra (Spectre), and Ana de Armas as Paloma (No Time to Die) (secondary female roles); Rosamund Pike as Miranda Frost (Die Another Day) (secondary villain); and Lashana Lynch as Nomi (No Time to Die) (secondary role alongside the principal female lead, Léa Seydoux).

Examples of announcements for the next film:
- Qualifying: Amazon MGM Studios announces that an actress will star opposite James Bond as the film's female lead, even if her character's name or role is not disclosed; or an actress is officially announced as playing Bond's love interest or principal female counterpart.
- Not qualifying: reports that an actress is in talks, has auditioned, screen-tested, or is on a shortlist; an actress announced to the cast in an unspecified or supporting role without being identified as the female lead; an actress cast as M, Moneypenny, or another recurring supporting character; an actress cast as a villain or henchwoman who is not the principal female lead; or an actress cast as James Bond himself.

If more than one actress is announced in principal female roles at the same time, this market will resolve to the actress identified as the female lead in the official announcement or, if this is unclear, by a consensus of credible reporting. If it remains unclear, this market will remain open until the earlier of (i) a consensus of credible reporting confirms one female lead; or (ii) December 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, when it will resolve to "No Bond girl chosen".

Transgender women will qualify as female.

This market will resolve based on the first official announcement of who will be the next Bond girl, regardless of any changes made thereafter.

If no actress is announced as the next Bond girl within the timeframe, this market will resolve to "No Bond girl chosen".

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from Amazon MGM Studios. However, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
