This market will resolve to "Yes" if the Fully Diluted Valuation of Beezie's token is greater than the value specified in the title 1 day after launch. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Only an official token launched by Beezie after the creation of this market will qualify. Stablecoins, memecoins, LSTs and synthetic tokens will not count.

The token must be actively and publicly tradable to be considered a launch.

The FDV will be determined using the total token supply multiplied by the token price.

"1 day after launch" is defined as 4:00 PM ET on the calendar day following launch. The resolution source for this market is the most liquid price source available. If Beezie (@Beezie) doesn't launch a token by December 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "No".
