This market will resolve to "Yes" if a Serbian presidential election is called by the specified date, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

An election is called when it has been declared under the relevant Serbian electoral, legal, or constitutional procedures, to take place on a specific date, and the date of the election has been publicly announced.

A statement of intention to call the election will not qualify.

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the government of Serbia; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
