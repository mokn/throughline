This market will resolve to "Yes" if, at the start of Paper Rex's first official match of the 2027 VALORANT Champions Tour (VCT) season, all five players on Paper Rex's active starting VALORANT roster as of market creation (d4v41, f0rsakeN, something, Jinggg, invy) are on Paper Rex's active roster and none of them has been officially announced as a substitute or reserve for the 2027 season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

An official match is a match in a VCT competition, such as VCT Kickoff, a VCT international league, Masters, or Champions. Showmatches and third-party tournaments do not count.

A listed player is considered to have left if they are officially removed from Paper Rex's active roster before that match, including through a transfer to another team, release, benching to inactive/reserve, the end of a loan, retirement, or any other departure from the active lineup, as reflected in the roster table on Paper Rex's Liquipedia page (https://liquipedia.net/valorant/Paper_Rex). If Paper Rex officially announces before that match that any listed player will be a substitute or reserve rather than part of its starting lineup for the 2027 season, this market will resolve to "No". A departure or substitute announcement officially made by Paper Rex or the player before that match counts even if the resolution source is updated after the match.

The addition of new players to the active roster does not by itself affect this market, as long as all five listed players remain on the active roster. A listed player missing a match while remaining on the active roster (for example, due to illness or visa issues, with a temporary stand-in) does not count as leaving. Name changes or aliases referring to the same player, and coach or staff changes, do not count.

If Paper Rex has not played an official 2027 VCT match by March 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve based on whether all five listed players are on Paper Rex's active roster on Liquipedia at that time. If Paper Rex disbands or no longer fields a VALORANT roster before then, this market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source for this market will be Liquipedia (https://liquipedia.net/valorant/Paper_Rex); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
