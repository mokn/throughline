This market will resolve to "Yes" if, between market creation and December 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, the listed company and/or an acquiring entity officially announce that they have entered into a definitive agreement for that entity to acquire the listed company. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No". 

The announcement must be made by the listed company, the acquiring entity, or both, for example through a press release, regulatory filing, or official company statement. Media reports of talks, negotiations, offers, bids, term sheets, letters of intent, or any other non-binding or unconfirmed arrangement will not qualify. 

A definitive agreement for an acquiring entity to obtain a majority of the listed company's equity or voting control, or to purchase substantially all of its assets, will qualify. 

A group of buyers counts as one acquiring entity only if they are officially announced as jointly acquiring the listed company. Equity sold to multiple investors in a financing round will not qualify, even if those investors together hold a majority. 

A merger will qualify if, under its announced terms, the listed company's existing shareholders will own less than half of the combined company. 

Minority investments, strategic partnerships, licensing agreements, internal reorganizations, and the hiring of the listed company's founders or employees will not alone qualify, whether or not the listed company continues to operate afterward. A license to the listed company's technology or intellectual property, however broad, exclusive, or perpetual, does not count as a purchase of its assets. 

An officially announced definitive agreement will qualify for a "Yes" resolution regardless of whether the acquisition is ultimately completed.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from the listed company and/or the acquiring entity.
