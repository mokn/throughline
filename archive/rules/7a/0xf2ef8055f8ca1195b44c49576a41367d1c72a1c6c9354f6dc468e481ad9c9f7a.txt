This market will resolve to "Yes" if Ange Postecoglou is the manager of Al Nassr on the final day of the 2026-27 Saudi Pro League season, scheduled for May 29, 2027. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Postecoglou was appointed Al Nassr head coach in July 2026 on a reported two-year contract. If he is sacked, resigns, departs by mutual agreement, or leaves for another role at any point before the final day of the season, this market will resolve to "No".

A temporary absence for illness, compassionate leave or suspension does not count as departure while Postecoglou remains under contract as manager.

If the 2026-27 Saudi Pro League season is cancelled or is not completed by June 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from Al Nassr and the Saudi Pro League. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
