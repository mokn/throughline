This market will resolve to “Yes” if the USS George Washington (CVN-73) arrives in Yokosuka, Japan by the listed date, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

The Washington will be considered to have arrived in Yokosuka upon confirmation by the U.S. Navy, the U.S. Department of Defense, U.S. 7th Fleet, or U.S. Indo-Pacific Command that the ship has arrived in Yokosuka, or upon a consensus of credible reporting confirming that the ship has physically arrived in Yokosuka.

The arrival must occur by the listed deadline. In the event of ambiguity as to whether the USS George Washington arrived in Yokosuka by the specified time, this market will remain open until the earlier of: (i) confirmation that the USS George Washington arrived in Yokosuka by the listed deadline, or (ii) the end of the seventh calendar day (ET) following the listed deadline. The market will resolve based on the totality of information available at that time. If there is material ambiguity as to whether the USS George Washington arrived in Yokosuka by that time, this market will resolve to “No.”

Port calls, exercises, or other operations outside Yokosuka, including at other ports in Japan, will not qualify. Announcements, orders, schedules, or reports that the ship will arrive, is en route, or is heading to Yokosuka will not qualify absent confirmation that the qualifying arrival has occurred. A qualifying arrival will count regardless of the ship's subsequent movements or tasking.

The resolution sources for this market will be official information from the United States government and military, including the U.S. Navy, the Department of Defense, U.S. 7th Fleet, and U.S. Indo-Pacific Command, and a consensus of credible reporting.
