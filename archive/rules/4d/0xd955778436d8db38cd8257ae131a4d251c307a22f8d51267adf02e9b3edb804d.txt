The Kansas Citizenship Voting Requirement Amendment (HCR 5004) is a legislatively referred measure that is scheduled to appear as a ballot measure in Kansas in the statewide general election currently scheduled for November 3, 2026, which would amend the Kansas Constitution to provide that only a citizen of the United States is eligible to vote in elections in Kansas.

This market will resolve to “Yes” if this ballot measure is approved as a result of the Kansas statewide general election currently scheduled for November 3, 2026. Otherwise, this market will resolve to “No.”

If voting on the specified ballot measure does not occur, or the results thereof are not known definitively, by March 31, 2027, this market will resolve to “No”.

Subsequent litigation or any failure to implement the measure will have no impact on the resolution of this market. 

This market will resolve based on a consensus of credible reporting. If there is ambiguity, this market will resolve solely based on official information from the State of Kansas, including the Kansas Secretary of State (https://sos.ks.gov/).
