This market will resolve to "Yes" if the Dallas Cowboys or the Houston Texans win the 2027 NFL Super Bowl (the championship game of the 2026-27 NFL season, scheduled for February 14, 2027). Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

If at any point it becomes impossible for both the Dallas Cowboys and the Houston Texans to win the 2027 NFL Super Bowl (e.g., both teams have been eliminated), this market will resolve to "No".

If the 2027 NFL championship game is cancelled, postponed after March 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or no champion has been declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "No".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the NFL (https://www.nfl.com/); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
