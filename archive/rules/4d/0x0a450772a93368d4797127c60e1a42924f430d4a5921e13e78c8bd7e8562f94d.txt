This market will resolve according to how much "You Can See Everything" box office will gross domestically from its opening (October 16, 2026) through November 30, 2026.

This market will resolve as soon as a final (i.e., not studio estimates) cumulative figure through November 30, 2026 is available, and no later than December 30, 2026, 11:59 PM ET. The primary resolution source is the "Total Gross" figure for November 30, 2026 in the "Daily Box Office Performance" table on the "Box Office" tab of this movie's The Numbers (https://www.the-numbers.com/) page. If The Numbers does not have a final figure by December 30, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve according to the "To Date" figure for November 30, 2026 on the "Domestic Daily" tab of this movie's Box Office Mojo (https://www.boxofficemojo.com/) release page. If a source lists no figure for November 30, 2026, its figure for the most recent listed date before November 30, 2026 will be used.

If neither source has a final figure by December 30, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve according to the figure The Numbers reports at that time, or, if The Numbers reports none, the figure Box Office Mojo reports at that time. If neither source reports any figure by December 30, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to the lowest bracket.

If the reported value falls exactly between two brackets, then this market will resolve to the higher range bracket.

This market will resolve according to the cumulative domestic gross until and including November 30, 2026, regardless of whether domestic refers to only the USA, or to USA and Canada, etc.
