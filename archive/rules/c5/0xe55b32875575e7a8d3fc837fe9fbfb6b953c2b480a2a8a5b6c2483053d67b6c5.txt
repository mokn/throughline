After earlier delays, the Palestinian presidential and legislative elections are currently scheduled to be held on September 11, 2027.

This market will resolve to "Yes" if voting for the presidency or the Palestinian Legislative Council is rescheduled to take place after September 11, 2027, or otherwise does not take place by September 11, 2027, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

The primary source of resolution for this market will be Official information from the Palestinian government; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
