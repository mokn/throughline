The Grand Final of the Eurovision Song Contest 2027 is scheduled for May 15, 2027.

This market will resolve to the country ranked first by points received from the national juries in the Grand Final of the Eurovision Song Contest 2027, according to the ranking below.

Countries will be ranked according to the total number of points received from the national juries in the Grand Final. If two or more countries are tied, ties will be broken by the total number of points received from the public vote. If still tied, ties will be broken alphabetically by English country name. This market will resolve according to the country ranked first under this ranking.

If the results of the Grand Final of the Eurovision Song Contest 2027 are not announced by July 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from Eurovision (https://www.eurovision.com/), including live footage of Eurovision Song Contest 2027, however a consensus of credible reporting will suffice.
