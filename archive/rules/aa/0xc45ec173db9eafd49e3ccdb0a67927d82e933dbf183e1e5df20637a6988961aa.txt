The Critics Choice Awards are presented annually by the Critics Choice Association to honor outstanding artistic and technical achievements in film. For the 32nd Critics Choice Awards in 2027, nominations are scheduled to be announced on December 4, 2026, followed by the ceremony on January 3, 2027.

This market will resolve according to the listed director who wins the 32nd Critics Choice Award for Best Director. 

In case of a tie for the winner, this market will resolve in favor of the nominated director whose listed last name comes first in alphabetical order. Where an option lists more than one director, if one of the listed directors wins individually, or both listed options win jointly, that option will resolve to "Yes". If only one of the jointly listed directors is a joint winner with another individual (e.g., tied with a director from another option), only the winning listed director's last names will be considered for the purposes of alphabetical ordering. If both jointly listed directors win alongside one or more directors not listed in that option, all winning listed directors last names will be considered for the purposes of alphabetical ordering.

If, for any reason, no winner is declared by April 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from the Critics Choice Awards and the Critics Choice Association, including data from their website (e.g., https://www.criticschoice.com/) and the live broadcast of the award ceremony; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
