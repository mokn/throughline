This market will resolve according to the team that wins the Atlantic Division in the 2026-27 NHL regular season, as determined by the final official NHL standings, including any official NHL tiebreakers.

If at any point it becomes impossible for a listed team to win the Atlantic Division (e.g. mathematical elimination before the end of the regular season), the associated market will resolve to "No".

If the 2026-27 NHL regular season is cancelled, or has not been completed by June 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the NHL; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
