This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team is relegated from Slovak Niké Liga at the conclusion of the 2026-27 Slovak Niké Liga season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Official relegation is defined as a team losing its place in Slovak Niké Liga for the following season through their final standing in the 2026-27 Slovak Niké Liga, via relegation playoff defeat, or by any other official league decision recognized by Slovak Niké Liga.

If the 2026-27 Slovak Niké Liga season is cancelled, postponed after June 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there are no teams confirmed for relegation within that timeframe, the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from Slovak Niké Liga. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
