The 2027 Pro Football Championship is scheduled to take place on February 14, 2027.

This market will resolve to "Yes" if the NFL officially announces a woman as the headliner of the 2027 Pro Football Championship halftime show. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No."

For the purpose of this market, "headliner" refers to the artist officially announced as the lead performer of the halftime show by the NFL. If multiple artists are billed equally as co-headliners, this market will resolve to "Yes" if at least one of them is a woman.

If a headliner has not been officially announced or if 2027 Pro Football Championship is postponed beyond February 15, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "No."

The primary resolution source will be official information from the NFL (https://www.nfl.com).
