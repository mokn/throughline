The Critics Choice Awards are presented annually by the Critics Choice Association. For the 32nd Annual Critics Choice Awards, nominations are scheduled for December 4, 2026, and the ceremony for January 3, 2027.

This market will resolve according to the listed person who wins Best Actor at the 32nd Annual Critics Choice Awards.

In case of a tie for the winner, this market will resolve in favor of the nominated person whose listed last name comes first in alphabetical order.

If, for any reason, no winner is declared by April 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source will be the television broadcast of the Critics Choice Awards and the official Critics Choice Association website (https://www.criticschoice.com/); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
