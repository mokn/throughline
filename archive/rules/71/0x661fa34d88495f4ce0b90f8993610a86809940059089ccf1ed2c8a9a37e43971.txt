This market will resolve to “Yes” if the “Total RWA Value” shown on rwa.xyz is equal to or greater than $50B on any day by December 31, 2026. Otherwise, it will resolve to “No.”

The resolution source is the “Total RWA Value” chart, available at:
https://app.rwa.xyz/

A data point on the rwa.xyz chart is considered finalized once the data point for the following day is published.

If rwa.xyz becomes permanently unavailable, the market will resolve based on other credible and publicly verifiable sources.
