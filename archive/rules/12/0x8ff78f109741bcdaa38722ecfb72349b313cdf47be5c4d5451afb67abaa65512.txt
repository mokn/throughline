This market will resolve according to the NHL team that wins the 2026-27 Presidents' Trophy (best regular season record by points).

If two or more teams finish the 2026-27 NHL regular season tied on points, this market will resolve according to the official NHL tiebreaker. If a tie still persists, this market will resolve in favor of the team with the most wins during the 2026-27 NHL regular season. If a tie still persists, this market will resolve in favor of the team with the most goals scored during the 2026-27 NHL regular season.

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the NHL; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
