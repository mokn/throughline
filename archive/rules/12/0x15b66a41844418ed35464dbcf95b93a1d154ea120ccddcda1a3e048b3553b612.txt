This market will resolve according to the person appointed as the next permanent general manager of the Indianapolis Colts.

If no permanent general manager is appointed by March 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, the market will resolve to "Other."

Appointments of 'interim,' 'acting,' or other non-permanent general managers will not impact this market's resolution. A permanent appointment that includes the title of general manager, including combined titles such as "executive vice president and general manager," will qualify.

An announcement of a new permanent general manager's appointment before this market's close date will immediately resolve this market to the corresponding option, regardless of when the announced appointment goes into effect.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from the NFL and/or the Indianapolis Colts; however, a consensus of credible reporting will also be used.
