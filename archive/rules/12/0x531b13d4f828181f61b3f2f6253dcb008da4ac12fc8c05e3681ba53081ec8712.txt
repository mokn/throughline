This market will resolve to “Yes” if LeBron James reaches 45,000 or more career NBA regular season points during the 2026-27 NBA regular season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to “No”.

Only points scored in NBA regular season games count toward this total, regardless of the team LeBron James plays for. Points scored in the preseason, the NBA Play-In Tournament, the NBA Playoffs, the NBA Cup Championship game, or All-Star events will not count. LeBron James finished the 2025-26 NBA regular season with 43,440 career regular season points.

If at any point it becomes impossible for LeBron James to reach 45,000 career regular season points during the 2026-27 NBA regular season, including if he retires, this market will resolve to “No”.

If the 2026-27 NBA regular season is cancelled, or postponed after April 25, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, before LeBron James reaches 45,000 career regular season points, this market will resolve to “No”.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from the NBA (https://www.nba.com/); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
