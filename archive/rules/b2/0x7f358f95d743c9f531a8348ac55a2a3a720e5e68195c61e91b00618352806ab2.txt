This market will immediately resolve to “Yes” if any Gate 1-minute candle for LAB (LAB/USDT) between the creation of this market and December 31, 2026, 23:59 ET has a final "High" or "Low" price equal to or beyond (above for ↑ High Prices, below for ↓ Low Prices) the listed price. Otherwise, the market will resolve to “No”.

The resolution source for this market is Gate, specifically the LAB/USDT “High” and "Low" prices available at:
https://www.gate.com/trade/LAB_USDT
with the chart set to “1m” (one-minute candles) on the top bar.

Only the Gate LAB/USDT price feed will be used.
Prices from other exchanges, trading pairs, or spot markets will not be considered.
