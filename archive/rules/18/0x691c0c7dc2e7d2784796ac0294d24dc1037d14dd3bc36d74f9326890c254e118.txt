Kai Cenat and IShowSpeed are scheduled to begin a Grand Theft Auto VI challenge on November 24, 2026, with the stated goal of seeing which of them can make $1,000,000 in the game first.

This market will resolve to "Kai" if Kai Cenat reaches $1,000,000 in Grand Theft Auto VI live on stream before IShowSpeed does, as accepted by them as a successful completion of the challenge, between market creation and December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET. This market will resolve to "Speed" if IShowSpeed reaches $1,000,000 in Grand Theft Auto VI live on stream before Kai Cenat does, as accepted by them as a successful completion of the challenge, within the same timeframe.

If both reach $1,000,000 at the same time, or if neither reaches $1,000,000 within this timeframe, this market will resolve to 50-50.

Playing or completing any individual game, pauses in the livestream, or temporary breaks from gameplay will have no bearing on this market. If Kai Cenat and IShowSpeed permanently move on from the challenge to other activities, the challenge will be considered over at that time, even if their livestreams continue uninterrupted.

Only events occurring while Kai Cenat or IShowSpeed is broadcasting the challenge live will count. Footage of off-stream or previously recorded attempts will not qualify.

If the challenge is cancelled, abandoned before completion, or delayed to a point after the specified date, this market will resolve to 50-50.

The resolution source for this market will be footage from Kai Cenat's and IShowSpeed's official broadcasts on YouTube and Twitch.
