This market will resolve according to the place in which the United States finishes in the World Happiness Report 2027 ranking of countries by life evaluations (in the World Happiness Report 2026, this ranking was published as "Figure 2.1: Country rankings by life evaluations. Gallup World Poll (2023–2025)").

The World Happiness Report 2027 is expected to be published in March 2027.

Countries will be ranked according to their published rank in the World Happiness Report 2027. If two or more countries share the same published rank, they will be ranked by their life evaluation score (the average Cantril ladder score) as published in the report, with higher scores ranking higher. If they remain tied, they will be ranked in alphabetical order of their English names. This market will resolve to the bracket that contains the place occupied by the United States after applying this ranking.

If no World Happiness Report 2027 ranking is published by June 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or the United States is not ranked, this market will resolve to "28th or lower".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the World Happiness Report (https://worldhappiness.report/); a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
