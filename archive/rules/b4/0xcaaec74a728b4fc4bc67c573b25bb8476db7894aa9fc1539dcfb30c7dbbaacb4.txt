This market will resolve according to the next NFL head coach fired from market creation to the conclusion of the 2026-27 NFL season.

A head coach will be considered fired if their team officially announces that they have been fired, demoted, dismissed, or relieved of their duties, including a mutual parting of ways announced by the team. Voluntary resignations and retirements will not count.

If two or more head coaches are fired on the same day, this market will resolve to the coach whose firing was officially announced first.

If the next head coach fired is not listed, or if no NFL head coach is fired by February 15, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from the NFL and/or its teams; however, a consensus of credible reporting will also be used.
