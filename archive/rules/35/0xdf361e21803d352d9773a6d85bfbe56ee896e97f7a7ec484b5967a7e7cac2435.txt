This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed player records 4 or more shutouts during the 2026-27 NHL regular season, as reported by the NHL. Otherwise, the associated market will resolve to "No".

If at any point it becomes impossible for the listed player to record 4 or more shutouts during the 2026-27 NHL regular season, the associated market will resolve to "No".

If the 2026-27 NHL regular season is cancelled, postponed after April 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or it cannot be determined whether the listed player has recorded 4 or more shutouts within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official information from the NHL; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
