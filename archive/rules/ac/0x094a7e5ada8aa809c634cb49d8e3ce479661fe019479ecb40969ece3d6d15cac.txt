This market will resolve to "Yes" if any match of the 2027 League of Legends Pro League (LPL) season is broadcast live with an official English-language broadcast between market creation and December 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

An "official English-language broadcast" is a live broadcast with live, human English-language commentary that is produced by, or produced on behalf of, Riot Games, Tencent, TJ Sports, or the LPL, and is streamed on a channel owned and operated by the LPL or LoL Esports (e.g., the LPL English or LoL Esports channels on Twitch or YouTube).

Co-streams, watch parties, or re-broadcasts streamed on channels not owned and operated by the LPL or LoL Esports will not qualify, even if the streamer is officially permitted, partnered, or paid under a co-streaming program. Machine-translated or AI-generated commentary will not qualify.

For the purposes of this market, the "2027 LPL season" refers to any official LPL domestic competition held in 2027, including its splits, playoffs, and regional finals. Broadcasts of international events that include LPL teams (e.g., MSI, Worlds, First Stand) will not qualify.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from Riot Games, the LPL, or LoL Esports; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
