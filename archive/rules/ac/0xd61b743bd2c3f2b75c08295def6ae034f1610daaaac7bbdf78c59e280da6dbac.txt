This market will resolve to "Yes" if Donald Trump signs any bill or joint resolution passed by the 120th United States Congress into United States federal law between market creation and the specified date, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Legislation enacted over veto or without Trump's signature will not qualify, nor will legislation that received final congressional passage before the 120th Congress convened.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from the White House and Congress.gov; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
