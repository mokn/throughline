On November 23, 2026, NVIDIA announced that its Board of Directors had reinstated Jensen Huang as President, Chief Executive Officer, and a member of the Board, and that Harvey C. Jones will remain as Chair of the Board.

This market will resolve to “Yes” if Harvey C. Jones’s departure through resignation or removal as Chair of NVIDIA’s Board of Directors is officially announced between market creation and the specified date, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to “No”.

The departure will be considered to be announced when Harvey C. Jones, NVIDIA, or their authorized representatives publicly and definitively announce that Jones has resigned or been removed, or will resign or be removed, as Chair of NVIDIA’s Board of Directors. Such an announcement will qualify regardless of when the specified person formally leaves the position; resignation includes retiring or stepping down as Chair, including to remain on the Board of Directors in another role, and removal includes removal as Chair by NVIDIA’s Board of Directors or removal from the Board of Directors by NVIDIA’s stockholders. A resignation or removal from NVIDIA’s Board of Directors will also be considered a departure as Chair. A change in the title of the position (e.g., to Executive Chair or Non-Executive Chair) will not be considered a departure if Jones continues to chair NVIDIA’s Board of Directors.

Only announcements which are framed as announcing a departure through resignation or removal intended to be effective within 18 months of the date of the announcement will qualify (e.g., neither “I will leave office in 2 years,” nor “I will not be in office forever,” would qualify).

Announcements of temporary leaves of absence, dated suspensions, indefinite suspensions, administrative leaves, or other temporary changes to the specified person’s duties in the specified position do not qualify.

The following do not qualify as public and definitive announcements: statements that the specified person is considering or open to departing; conditional statements that the specified person will depart only if certain conditions are met; the announcement of an offer of resignation that requires acceptance and remains pending or has been refused; media reports that the specified person plans to depart; statements made in sarcasm or jest; and statements by persons other than the specified person, the relevant authority governing the specified position, or their respective authorized representatives.

Jones’s annual term as a director, and any term as Chair that ends at an annual meeting of NVIDIA’s stockholders, will not be considered a predefined term with a scheduled end date. An announcement that Jones will retire from or not stand for re-election to NVIDIA’s Board of Directors, or will step down as Chair, at an annual meeting will qualify if it otherwise meets the requirements above. If Jones is not re-elected to NVIDIA’s Board of Directors by NVIDIA’s stockholders, it will be considered a removal.

If the specified person formally ceases to hold the specified position through such a departure without a qualifying announcement, the market will resolve to “Yes” if a qualifying resignation or removal occurs by the specified date, 11:59 PM ET. If the specified person ceases to hold the specified position by any other means, this market will resolve to “No”.

The primary resolution sources for this market will be official information from Harvey C. Jones and NVIDIA; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
