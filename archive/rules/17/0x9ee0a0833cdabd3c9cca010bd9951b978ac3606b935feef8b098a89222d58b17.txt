This is a market on the result of the 2027 Ryder Cup between Team USA and Team Europe, scheduled for September 2027 at Adare Manor, Ireland.

The "Team USA" market resolves "Yes" if Team USA finishes with more total points than Team Europe; the "Team Europe" market resolves "Yes" if Team Europe finishes with more total points than Team USA; the "Tie" market resolves "Yes" if the competition ends level on points (e.g., 14–14).

If the Ryder Cup is postponed, these markets remain open until the competition has been completed. If the Ryder Cup is cancelled entirely with no make-up competition, the "Tie" market resolves "Yes" and the "Team USA" and "Team Europe" markets resolve "No".

The primary resolution source will be official information from Ryder Cup Europe / the PGA of America (https://www.rydercup.com); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
