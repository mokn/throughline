This market will resolve to "Yes" if the President of Taiwan, Lai Ching-te, is formally impeached by the Legislative Yuan by December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No."

The primary resolution source for this market is official information from the Taiwanese government, however a consensus of credible reporting may be used.
