In sports, a Scorigami is a scoring combination that has never before occurred in a sport or league's history.

This market will resolve to "Yes" if the number of NFL Scorigamis that occur during the 2026-27 NFL regular season equals or exceeds the listed number. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

The 2026-27 NFL regular season covers all games from Week 1 through Week 18. Each game whose final score is a Scorigami counts as one Scorigami toward the total, including games played before this market opened. If two or more games in the same week produce Scorigamis, each counts separately toward the total. Preseason and postseason games, including Super Bowl LXI, will not count toward the total.

This market may resolve to "Yes" as soon as the listed number of Scorigamis has occurred.

If any 2026-27 NFL regular season game is canceled, or is postponed beyond February 14, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve based on the final scores of all completed regular season games.

The primary resolution source for this market is official information from the NFL (https://www.nfl.com/scores/), however other sites tracking Scorigami may be used (nflscorigami.com, twitter.com/NFL_Scorigami).
