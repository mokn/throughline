This market will resolve to "Yes" if any Premier League title won by Manchester City FC is officially stripped or revoked as a result of the Premier League's proceedings against Manchester City FC concerning alleged breaches of the Premier League's financial rules, which were announced on February 6, 2023 (commonly referred to as the "115 charges" case) by June 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

A title will count as stripped once the Premier League, the independent Regulatory Commission, or the Appeal Board officially announces that it has been removed from Manchester City FC, regardless of whether the decision is later appealed or overturned, or whether the title is reassigned to another club. Calls, recommendations, or reports that a title could be stripped will not count.

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the Premier League, the independent Regulatory Commission, the Appeal Board, or Manchester City FC; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
