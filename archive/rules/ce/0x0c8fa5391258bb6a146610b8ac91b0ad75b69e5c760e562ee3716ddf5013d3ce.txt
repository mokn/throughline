This market will resolve to “Heads” if the opening coin toss lands on heads at Super Bowl LXI.

This market will resolve to “Tails” if the opening coin toss lands on Tails.

If Super Bowl LXI is cancelled, postponed after February 28, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there is no coin toss outcome determined within that timeframe, this market will resolve to 50-50.

In the event of a re-flip, this market will resolve based on the coin toss result that determines which team chooses to defer or receive possession or to choose a goal to defend.

The primary resolution source for this market will be the official broadcast of Super Bowl LXI; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
