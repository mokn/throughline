This market will resolve to "Yes" if the combined domestic Opening Weekend Box Office gross of "Dune: Part Three" and "Avengers: Doomsday" (both releasing December 18, 2026) is greater than the equivalent combined figure for "Barbenheimer" — "Barbie" and "Oppenheimer" — of $244,477,464. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

This market will resolve as soon as final (i.e., not studio estimates) figures for both films' 3-day opening weekend (December 18 - December 20) are available, and no later than January 20, 2027, 11:59 PM ET. For each movie, the primary resolution source is the opening weekend figure in the "Weekend Box Office Performance" table on the "Box Office" tab of that movie's The Numbers (https://www.the-numbers.com/) page. If The Numbers does not have a final figure for a movie by January 20, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, that movie's "Weekend" figure for its first weekend on the "Domestic Weekend" tab of its Box Office Mojo (https://www.boxofficemojo.com/) release page will be used.

If neither source has a final figure for a film by January 20, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, that film's figure will be the one The Numbers reports at that time, or, if The Numbers reports none, the one Box Office Mojo reports at that time. If neither source reports any figure for a film by January 20, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "No".

This market will resolve according to the figures for the 3-day weekend (which typically includes Thursday's previews), regardless of whether domestic refers to only the USA, or to USA and Canada, etc.

If both "Dune: Part Three" and "Avengers: Doomsday" do not both release on the weekend of December 18 - December 20, 2026, this market will resolve to "No".
