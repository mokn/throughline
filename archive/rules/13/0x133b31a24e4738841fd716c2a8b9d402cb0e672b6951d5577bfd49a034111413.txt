This market will resolve “Yes” if the listed team reaches the 2026 Big 12 Championship Game. 

If at any point it becomes impossible for the listed team to advance to the 2026 Big 12 Championship Game (e.g. they are mathematically eliminated), the associated market will resolve to "No".

If the 2026 Big 12 Championship Game is cancelled, postponed after January 15, 2027, 11:59 PM ET or the 2026 Big 12 Championship Game matchup has not been declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to “No”.

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the Big 12; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
