This market will resolve according to Tucker Carlson's path in the 2028 U.S. presidential election. Exactly one of the following outcomes will resolve to "Yes"; all others will resolve to "No":

- "Doesn't announce GOP run before 2028": Carlson does not make a qualifying announcement that he is running for the Republican nomination for President of the United States by December 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET. If this occurs, this market will resolve to "Doesn't announce GOP run before 2028" regardless of any later events.
- "Runs and loses GOP nomination": Carlson makes a qualifying announcement by that deadline but does not win the 2028 Republican presidential nomination. If this occurs, this market will resolve to "Runs and loses GOP nomination" regardless of any later events.
- "Wins GOP nomination and loses general": Carlson makes a qualifying announcement by that deadline, wins the 2028 Republican presidential nomination, and does not win the 2028 presidential election.
- "Wins presidency": Carlson makes a qualifying announcement by that deadline, wins the 2028 Republican presidential nomination, and wins the 2028 presidential election.

A qualifying announcement is an official announcement by Carlson that he is running for the Republican nomination for President of the United States in the 2028 presidential election. An announcement will qualify regardless of whether Carlson has filed, or later files, any paperwork to run. The announcement must be explicit and unambiguous; conditional statements, or statements that are clearly sarcastic, made in jest, or immediately clarified as not being genuine, will not count. Announcements that Carlson is exploring or considering a run, including the formation of an exploratory committee, will not count. An announcement of a run as an independent or as the candidate of any party other than the Republican Party will not count.

Carlson will be considered to have won the 2028 Republican presidential nomination if he wins and accepts the 2028 nomination of the Republican Party for President of the United States. Any replacement of the Republican nominee after Carlson has won and accepted the nomination will not change this. If another individual wins and accepts the 2028 Republican nomination, Carlson will be considered not to have won it, even if he later replaces that individual as the nominee.

Carlson will be considered to have won the 2028 presidential election once the Associated Press, Fox News, and NBC have all called the race for him. If all three sources have not called the race for the same candidate by the inauguration date (January 20, 2029), the winner will be determined by who is inaugurated.

The resolution source for whether Carlson makes a qualifying announcement will be official statements by Carlson (e.g., via speech or social media); however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used. The resolution source for the Republican nomination will be a consensus of official Republican Party sources. The resolution source for the presidential election will be the Associated Press, Fox News, and NBC, as described above.
