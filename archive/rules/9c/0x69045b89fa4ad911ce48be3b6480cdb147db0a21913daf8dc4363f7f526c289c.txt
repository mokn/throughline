This market will resolve to "Yes" if Logan Paul and Tom Brady are officially announced to face each other in a sanctioned fight. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Resolution for this market will be based on an official announcement of a bout between Logan Paul and Tom Brady, regardless of whether the fight ends up taking place.

For the purposes of this market, a "sanctioned fight" means a professional or exhibition combat sports bout (including but not limited to boxing, MMA, or kickboxing) between the two, sanctioned or overseen by a recognized athletic commission or combat sports governing body, under any promotion. Scripted professional wrestling matches, including WWE matches, do not count.

Only official announcements which include a scheduled date for the bout will count. Qualifying announcements must come from the promotion or event organizer staging the bout, or jointly from both fighters or their official representatives. Announcements with no date or which do not confirm that the fight is official, speculation, reports of negotiations, or other unofficial announcements will not count.

If no qualifying announcement is made by December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from the promotion staging the bout and from the fighters' official channels. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
