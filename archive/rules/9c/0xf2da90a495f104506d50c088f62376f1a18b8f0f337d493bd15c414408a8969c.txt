This market will resolve to "Yes" if LeBron James records 5 or more triple-doubles during the 2026-27 NBA regular season. Otherwise, it will resolve to "No".

A triple-double is a game in which a player records 10 or more in three of the five statistical categories of points, rebounds, assists, steals and blocks, per the official NBA box score, including any statistical corrections the NBA subsequently issues. Only 2026-27 NBA regular-season games count. NBA Cup group-play and knockout games other than the NBA Cup Championship game are regular-season games and count. Statistics from the preseason, the NBA Cup Championship game, the Play-In Tournament, the playoffs and the All-Star Game are excluded. If a player plays for more than one team during the season, his statistics from all teams are combined.

If LeBron James does not play in the 2026-27 NBA regular season, including because of injury or retirement, this market will resolve according to the number of triple-doubles he recorded, which may be zero.

If the 2026-27 NBA regular season is cancelled, shortened, or postponed after April 25, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve according to the number of triple-doubles LeBron James recorded in regular-season games completed by that time.

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the NBA, including NBA.com/stats; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
