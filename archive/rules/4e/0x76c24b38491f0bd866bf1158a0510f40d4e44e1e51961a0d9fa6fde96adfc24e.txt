This market will resolve to the club that records the most total goals in the league phase of the 2026-27 UEFA Europa League.

Only matches played in the league phase of the 2026-27 UEFA Europa League will be considered. Qualifying rounds, the play-off round, the knockout phase play-offs, and all knockout rounds will not be considered.

In the event of a tie, this market will resolve according to the official leader as determined by UEFA. If multiple leaders are announced, this market will resolve to the club with the higher final position in the league phase standings. If a tie still persists, this market will resolve to the club that is listed first alphabetically.

If the 2026-27 UEFA Europa League league phase is cancelled, postponed after February 28, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there is otherwise no official leader declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from UEFA (https://www.uefa.com/uefaeuropaleague/statistics/clubs/goals/).
