This market will resolve according to the winner of the Republican nomination to contest the 2027 special election for United States Senator from Minnesota, held to fill the remainder of Amy Klobuchar's term (Class I). The Republican primary is scheduled to take place on August 10, 2027.

If no nominee is announced by November 2, 2027, 11:59PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source for this market will be the first official announcement of the results from the Republican Party of Minnesota; however, an overwhelming consensus of credible reporting may suffice.
