This market will resolve according to which English Premier League manager is fired first during the 2026-27 season.

If the listed manager is the first manager to be officially fired, dismissed, or sacked by their respective Premier League club during the 2026-27 season, this market will resolve to "Yes". Otherwise, it will resolve to "No".

If multiple managers are fired on the same calendar day, resolution will be based on the earliest official announcement time.

If a manager resigns, mutually agrees to part ways, or departs for another job, this will not count as being fired, and that will not qualify as "Yes" for this market.

If no Premier League managers are fired by May 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source will be official club announcements or official Premier League communications. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
