This market will resolve to "Yes" if legislation implementing the core provisions of the Multilateral Alignment of Technology Controls on Hardware (MATCH) Act is enacted into law between market creation and the specified date, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

For the purposes of this market, legislation will qualify if it restricts, or requires the executive branch to restrict, the sale of semiconductor manufacturing equipment to China as a whole, rather than only to specific Chinese companies, facilities, or end uses. Exceptions for facilities controlled by United States or allied companies, national security waivers, or a deadline for allied countries to adopt aligned controls will not prevent legislation from qualifying.

Qualifying legislation may be enacted under any name, either as standalone legislation or as part of broader legislation. Legislation that only requires studies or reports, or that only authorizes but does not require such restrictions, will not qualify. Executive actions alone will not count. Only enactment is required; the restrictions do not need to have taken effect, and any subsequent repeal, amendment, or judicial invalidation will not affect resolution.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from the US federal government, including Congress.gov; however, a consensus of credible reporting will also be used.
