This market will resolve to "Yes" if TownSquare officially launches a token by the specified date, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Only an official token launched by TownSquare after the creation of this market will qualify. Stablecoins, memecoins, LSTs and synthetic tokens will not count.

The token must be actively and publicly tradable. Announcements alone do not qualify.

The primary resolution source for this market will be information from TownSquare (https://x.com/TownSquarexyz), however a consensus of credible reporting will also be used.
