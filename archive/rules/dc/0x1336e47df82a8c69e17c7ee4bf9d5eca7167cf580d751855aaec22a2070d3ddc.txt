This market will resolve to "Yes" if players on the listed team record a combined total of 3 or more aces across all maps the listed team plays at the PGL Major Singapore 2026 (Stage 1, Stage 2, Stage 3, and the Playoffs), currently scheduled for November 24 to December 13, 2026. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

An ace is a round in which a single player kills five opponents, as recorded by HLTV as a 5-kill round in the listed team's map statistics. HLTV lists the PGL Major Singapore 2026 as three event pages: Stage 1 (https://www.hltv.org/events/9303/pgl-major-singapore-2026-stage-1), Stage 2 (https://www.hltv.org/events/9304/pgl-major-singapore-2026-stage-2), and the main event covering Stage 3 and the Playoffs (https://www.hltv.org/events/8302/pgl-major-singapore-2026). Aces from all three events count, as shown on HLTV at 11:59 PM ET on the day after the Grand Final is played. Statistics revisions made after that time will not be considered.

Only aces in rounds that begin after this market was created count. Round timing will be determined from HLTV's match pages.

Teams are identified by organization. A team competing at this tournament under an alternative name required by local regulations (e.g. GOSH for BC.Game, THE ONE for 1win, BB Team for BetBoom, PVISION for PARIVISION) counts as the listed team. Aces by any player count while that player plays for the listed team, regardless of roster changes.

If the PGL Major Singapore 2026 is canceled, postponed after January 12, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or HLTV's statistics for the event are not available by January 12, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "50-50".

The resolution source for this market will be HLTV (https://www.hltv.org/).
