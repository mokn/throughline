This market will resolve to the player who records the most assists during all phases of the 2026-27 Liga MX season (excluding friendlies and exhibitions).

If multiple players tie for the most assists, this market will resolve to the tied player who completed more passes during all phases of the 2026-27 Liga MX season. If a tie still persists, this market will resolve in favor of the tied player whose last name comes first alphabetically.

If the 2026-27 Liga MX season is canceled or not completed by June 27, 2027, the market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source will be official statistics from Liga MX. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
