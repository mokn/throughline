This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team finishes 3rd in the final standings of the 2026-27 Eredivisie season. Otherwise, it will resolve to "No".

In the event of a tie, this market will resolve to the team officially recognized by Eredivisie as finishing in third place. If multiple teams are officially awarded third place, the market will resolve to the team whose listed name comes first alphabetically.

If at any point it becomes impossible for the listed club to finish 3rd in the 2026-27 Eredivisie season, the market will resolve to "No".

If the 2026-27 Eredivisie season is cancelled, postponed after June 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there is otherwise no 3rd place team declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source will be official information from Eredivisie. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
