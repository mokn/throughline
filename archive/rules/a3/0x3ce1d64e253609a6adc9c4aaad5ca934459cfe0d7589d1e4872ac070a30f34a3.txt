This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team is relegated from Colombia Primera A at the conclusion of the 2026 Colombia Primera A season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Official relegation is defined as a team losing its place in Colombia Primera A for the following season through their final standing in the 2026 Colombia Primera A, via relegation playoff defeat, or by any other official league decision recognized by Colombia Primera A.

If the 2026 Colombia Primera A season is cancelled, postponed after December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or there are no teams confirmed for relegation within that timeframe, the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from Colombia Primera A. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
