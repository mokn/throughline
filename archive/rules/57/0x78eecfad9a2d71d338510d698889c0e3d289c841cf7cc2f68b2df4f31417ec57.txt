This market will resolve according to the region of the team that wins the PGL Major Singapore 2026, currently scheduled for November 24 to December 13, 2026. A team's region is its Valve Regional Standings (VRS) region as listed for this tournament's participants, grouped as follows:

Europe: MOUZ, Vitality, Aurora, Spirit, BB Team (BetBoom), Falcons, FUT, Nemiga, G2, THE ONE (1win), Natus Vincere, Astralis, Ninjas in Pyjamas, PVISION (PARIVISION), HEROIC, FaZe, Eternal Fire, 100 Thieves
Americas: FURIA, Legacy, MIBR, Liquid, 9z, M80, DENDELE, Imperial, Wildcard
Asia: The MongolZ, GOSH (BC.Game), FlyQuest, Chinggis Warriors, Lynn Vision

Teams are identified by organization. A team competing at this tournament under an alternative name required by local regulations (e.g. GOSH for BC.Game, THE ONE for 1win, BB Team for BetBoom, PVISION for PARIVISION) counts as the listed team.

If the winner of the PGL Major Singapore 2026 is not determined by January 12, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or the tournament is canceled or postponed past that date, this market will resolve to "Other".

If multiple teams are officially declared co-winners, this market will resolve in favor of the region of the team whose listed team name comes first alphabetically.

The resolution source for this market will be HLTV (https://www.hltv.org/events/8302/pgl-major-singapore-2026); however, a consensus of credible reporting (e.g., Liquipedia at https://liquipedia.net/counterstrike/PGL/2026/Singapore) may also be used.
