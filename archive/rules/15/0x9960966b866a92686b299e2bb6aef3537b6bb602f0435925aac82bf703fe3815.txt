This market will resolve according to the champion selected for Xun's (Peng Li-Xun) commemorative skin for winning the 2026 League of Legends World Championship (Worlds 2026) with Bilibili Gaming.

This market will resolve as soon as the champion for Xun's Worlds 2026 skin is officially announced by Riot Games (including official League of Legends and LoL Esports channels) or Bilibili Gaming. An official announcement of the champion selection is sufficient; the skin does not need to be revealed or released. Leaks, datamines, and unofficial reports will not be considered.

If official announcements name more than one champion for Xun's Worlds 2026 skins, this market will resolve to the first champion named in the earliest such announcement.

If the selected champion is not listed in this market, this market will resolve to "Other".

If Riot Games officially announces that no Worlds 2026 skin will be made for Xun, or if the champion for Xun's Worlds 2026 skin is not officially announced by June 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

Xun's team affiliation at the time of the announcement does not affect this market.

The resolution source for this market will be official announcements from Riot Games and Bilibili Gaming.
