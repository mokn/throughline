This market will resolve to "Yes" if the Detroit Pistons reach the Championship game of the 2026 NBA Cup. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Two teams reach the Championship game: the winners of the two Knockout Rounds semifinals. A team that reaches the Championship game counts regardless of the result of that game.

If at any point it becomes impossible for the Detroit Pistons to reach the Championship game of the 2026 NBA Cup (e.g. they are mathematically eliminated), this market will resolve to "No".

If the 2026 NBA Cup is cancelled, postponed after December 23, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or the teams reaching the Championship game have not been determined within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "No".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the NBA; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
