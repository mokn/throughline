This market will resolve to the player who wins the NBA Cup Most Valuable Player award for the 2026 NBA Cup.

If the NBA names more than one winner, this market will resolve to "Other".

If no winner is announced by December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, for any reason, including cancellation, this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the NBA; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
