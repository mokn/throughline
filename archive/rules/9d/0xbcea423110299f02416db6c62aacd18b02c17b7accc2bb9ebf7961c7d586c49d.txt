This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team finishes 16th in the 2026 Brazil Série A. Otherwise, the associated market will resolve to "No".

If at any point it becomes impossible for the listed team to finish 16th in the 2026 Brazil Série A, the associated market will resolve to "No".

If the 2026 Brazil Série A season is cancelled, postponed after December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or there is otherwise no 16th place team declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from Brazil Série A; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
