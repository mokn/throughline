This market will resolve according to the team that wins the 2027 CONCACAF Champions Cup.

If at any point it becomes impossible for a listed team to win the 2027 CONCACAF Champions Cup per the rules of CONCACAF (e.g., they are eliminated), the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

If the 2027 CONCACAF Champions Cup is cancelled, postponed after June 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there is otherwise no winner declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source will be official information from CONCACAF; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
