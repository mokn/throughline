This market will resolve to "Fury" if Tyson Fury is officially declared the winner of the fight against Anthony Joshua at Principality Stadium in Cardiff, Wales, scheduled for December 11, 2026. It will resolve to "Joshua" if Anthony Joshua is officially declared the winner.

If the fight is declared a draw or technical draw, ruled a No Contest, not scored, canceled, or postponed beyond December 25, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve "50-50."

This market will resolve based on the official result announced at the conclusion of the event. Any later change to that result, including an overturn to a No Contest, will not be considered.

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from Zuffa Boxing; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
