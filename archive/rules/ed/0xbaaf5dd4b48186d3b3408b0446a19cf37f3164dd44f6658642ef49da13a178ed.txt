This market will resolve according to the team that finishes last in the league phase of the 2026-27 UEFA Champions League.

If at any point it becomes impossible for the listed club to finish last in the league phase, the associated market will resolve to "No".

If two or more clubs are level on points, the ranking will be determined according to UEFA's official tie-breaker criteria as published for the competition.

If the 2026-27 UEFA Champions League league phase is canceled or not completed by February 28, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source will be official information from UEFA. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
