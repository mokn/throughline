This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team is relegated from Allsvenskan (Sweden) at the conclusion of the 2026 Allsvenskan (Sweden) season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Official relegation is defined as a team losing its place in Allsvenskan (Sweden) for the following season through their final standing in the 2026 Allsvenskan (Sweden), via relegation playoff defeat, or by any other official league decision recognized by Allsvenskan (Sweden).

If the 2026 Allsvenskan (Sweden) season is cancelled, postponed after December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or there are no teams confirmed for relegation within that timeframe, the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from Allsvenskan (Sweden). A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
