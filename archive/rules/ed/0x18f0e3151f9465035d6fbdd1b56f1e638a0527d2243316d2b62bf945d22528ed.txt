This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team is relegated from Liga Profesional (Argentina) at the conclusion of the 2026 Liga Profesional (Argentina) season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Official relegation is defined as a team losing its place in Liga Profesional (Argentina) for the following season through their final standing in the 2026 Liga Profesional (Argentina), via relegation playoff defeat, or by any other official league decision recognized by Liga Profesional (Argentina).

If the 2026 Liga Profesional (Argentina) season is cancelled, postponed after December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or there are no teams confirmed for relegation within that timeframe, the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from Liga Profesional (Argentina). A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
