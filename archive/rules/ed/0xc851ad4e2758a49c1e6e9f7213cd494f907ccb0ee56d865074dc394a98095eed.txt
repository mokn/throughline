This market will resolve according to the team that wins the 2026-27 AFC Champions League Elite.

If at any point it becomes impossible for a listed team to win the 2026-27 AFC Champions League Elite per the rules of the AFC (e.g., they are eliminated), the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

If the 2026-27 AFC Champions League Elite is cancelled, postponed after May 15, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there is otherwise no winner declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source will be official information from the AFC; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
