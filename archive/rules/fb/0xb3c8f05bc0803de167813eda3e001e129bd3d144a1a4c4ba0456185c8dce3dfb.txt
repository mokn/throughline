This market will resolve to “Yes” if the percentage change from Anthropic's IPO price to the official closing price of its Class A common stock on its first day of trading is greater than the percentage specified in the title. Otherwise, this market will resolve to “No”.

The percentage change is calculated as (closing price minus IPO price) divided by IPO price, multiplied by 100, with no rounding. A percentage change exactly equal to the percentage specified in the title will resolve this market to “No”.

The IPO price will be the final offering price to the public per share of Class A common stock, as stated in the first final prospectus filed under Rule 424(b) filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, even if that prospectus is filed after trading begins. The official closing price as listed by the primary exchange will be considered the closing share price.

Anthropic’s first day of trading refers to the first day Anthropic’s Class A common stock trades on the secondary market on its primary exchange. The sale of shares to investors at the IPO price, and any trading before the exchange listing (such as on private-share platforms), will not be considered.

For this market, an IPO means an underwritten, SEC-registered offering of Anthropic’s Class A common stock, listed on a U.S. national securities exchange and priced as shown by the first final prospectus filed with the SEC under Rule 424(b). Direct listings, SPAC mergers, reverse mergers, private placements and non-U.S. listings will not count. An IPO counts as completed by December 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET if the pricing date on that prospectus is on or before that time; if the prospectus has not been filed by then, this market will wait until 11:59 PM ET on the second business day after December 31, 2027 for it. If Anthropic does not complete an IPO by December 31, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or announces a definitive agreement to be acquired or merged before completing one, this market will resolve to “No”. If the IPO is priced but the shares never begin trading on the listing exchange, this market will resolve to “No”.

Resolution will be based on the first final prospectus filed under Rule 424(b) for the IPO price and the primary exchange's official listing page for the closing price. In the event that the relevant figure is not displayed, another reliable source will be used.

In the event of an interruption in the course of the normal trading session on Anthropic's first day of trading (e.g., a circuit breaker or half-day), the market will resolve according to the official closing price of the abbreviated session. If no such official closing price is published, the market will resolve according to the next trading day on which an official closing price is published, treating that as the first day of trading.
