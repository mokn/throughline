This market will resolve to "Yes" if Robinhood officially launches a native or governance token for Robinhood Chain by 11:59 PM ET on the date specified in the title. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Only an official token launched by Robinhood (or a wholly-owned subsidiary) for Robinhood Chain after the creation of this market will qualify. Stablecoins, memecoins, LSTs, synthetic tokens, and Stock Tokens will not count.

The token must be actively and publicly tradable. Announcements alone do not qualify.

The primary resolution source for this market will be information from Robinhood (https://x.com/RobinhoodApp, https://x.com/RobinhoodCrypto), however a consensus of credible reporting will also be used.
