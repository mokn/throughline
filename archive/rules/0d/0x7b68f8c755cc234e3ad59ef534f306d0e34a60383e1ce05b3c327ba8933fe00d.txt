The winner of Festivali i Këngës 65, RTSH's national selection for the Eurovision Song Contest 2027, is scheduled to be announced on December 20, 2026.

This market will resolve to the artist ranked first in Festivali i Këngës 65, according to the ranking below.

Artists will be ranked according to their official ranking in Festivali i Këngës 65. If two or more artists share the same ranking, ties will be broken according to the official tie-breaking procedures of Festivali i Këngës 65. If still tied, ties will be broken alphabetically by the artist's name as listed in this market. This market will resolve according to the artist ranked first under this ranking.

If at any point it is impossible for the listed artist to win Festivali i Këngës 65 based on the rules of the competition (i.e. they are eliminated), this market may immediately resolve to "No".

If the winner of Festivali i Këngës 65 is not announced by December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to the artist officially announced as Albania's representative at the Eurovision Song Contest 2027, if that announcement is made by May 10, 2027, 11:59 PM ET. If no such announcement is made by May 10, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from RTSH (https://www.rtsh.al/) and Eurovision (https://www.eurovision.com/), however a consensus of credible reporting will suffice.
