This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team finishes 3rd in the final standings of the 2026 Premium Liiga (Estonia) season. Otherwise, it will resolve to "No".

In the event of a tie, this market will resolve to the team officially recognized by Premium Liiga (Estonia) as finishing in third place. If multiple teams are officially awarded third place, the market will resolve to the team whose listed name comes first alphabetically.

If at any point it becomes impossible for the listed club to finish 3rd in the 2026 Premium Liiga (Estonia) season, the market will resolve to "No".

If the 2026 Premium Liiga (Estonia) season is cancelled, postponed after December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or there is otherwise no 3rd place team declared within that timeframe, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source will be official information from Premium Liiga (Estonia). A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
