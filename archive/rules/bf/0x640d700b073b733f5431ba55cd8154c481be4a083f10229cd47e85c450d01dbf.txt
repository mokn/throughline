The winner of Benidorm Fest 2027, RTVE's annual song festival, is scheduled to be announced on January 30, 2027.

This market will resolve to the artist ranked first in Benidorm Fest 2027, according to the ranking below.

Artists will be ranked according to their official ranking in Benidorm Fest 2027. If two or more artists share the same ranking, ties will be broken according to the official tie-breaking procedures of Benidorm Fest 2027. If still tied, ties will be broken alphabetically by the artist's name as listed in this market. This market will resolve according to the artist ranked first under this ranking.

If at any point it is impossible for the listed artist to win Benidorm Fest 2027 based on the rules of the competition (i.e. they are eliminated), this market may immediately resolve to "No".

If the winner of Benidorm Fest 2027 is not announced by February 28, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to the artist officially announced as Spain's representative at the Eurovision Song Contest 2027, if that announcement is made by May 10, 2027, 11:59 PM ET. If no such announcement is made by May 10, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, this market will resolve to "Other".

The primary resolution source for this market will be official information from RTVE (https://www.rtve.es/) and Eurovision (https://www.eurovision.com/), however a consensus of credible reporting will suffice.
