This market will resolve according to Japan's real gross domestic product growth rate (Quarter-over-Quarter Annualized, %, seasonally adjusted) in the fourth quarter of 2026, as reported in the Japan Cabinet Office's (ESRI) Quarterly Estimates of GDP (First Preliminary Estimates) release for Oct.-Dec. 2026, scheduled for release on February 15, 2027.

The relevant figure is the "GDP (Expenditure Approach)" value for the "2026/10-12." row in the table titled "Real, Seasonally Adjusted Series (Quarter-to-Quarter, Annualized)", found under the heading "I. GDP (Expenditure Approach) and Its Components" on the release page for Oct.-Dec. 2026 (The 1st preliminary). This figure expresses the quarterly growth rate compounded over four quarters to produce an annualized equivalent; the non-annualized "Quarter-to-Quarter" figure will not be used.

To find the release page: go to https://www.esri.cao.go.jp/en/sna/sokuhou/sokuhou_top.html and click "Time series table" (which links to the latest release), or go to the Release Archive (https://www.esri.cao.go.jp/en/sna/data/sokuhou/files/toukei_top.html), select 2027, and click "Quarterly Estimates of GDP for Oct.-Dec.2026 (The First preliminary Estimates)". The same figure published in the release's summary PDF ("1-1. GDP (Expenditure Approach) and Its Components") may also be used. Changes in the Japan Cabinet Office's GDP reporting format will not disqualify a published figure from counting.

If the reported value falls exactly between two brackets, this market will resolve to the higher range bracket.

If no data for the specified quarter is released by the date the next quarter's data is scheduled to be released, this market will resolve based on data from the last available quarter.

Note: the resolution source for this market reports Quarter-over-Quarter Annualized GDP growth rates to only one decimal point (e.g. 2.1%). Thus, this is the level of precision that will be used when resolving the market.

Note: data from the initial release of the referenced GDP report is what will be used to resolve this market. Data may be revised during the following quarter or as a part of the next estimate's publication, however any revisions to GDP report data made after the initial release will not be considered for this market's resolution.
