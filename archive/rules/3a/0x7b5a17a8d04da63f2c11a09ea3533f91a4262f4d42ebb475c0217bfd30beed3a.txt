This market will resolve to "Yes" if the results of the October 27 Israeli Knesset election are annulled by December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No". 

The results of this election will be considered annulled if they are formally declared invalid by the Israel Central Elections Committee, the Supreme Court of Israel, or another official body of the government of Israel with the legal jurisdiction to do so.

If an official body of the government of Israel with legal jurisdiction to rule on this matter has formally accepted a case seeking annulment of this election and no ruling has been issued by December 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, this market may remain open until the earlier of (i) the public announcement of a ruling or (ii) June 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET.

This market will resolve based on the first qualifying ruling issued that annuls this election. Subsequent appeals or challenges will not be considered.

The resolution sources will be official information from the government of Israel and a consensus of credible reporting.
