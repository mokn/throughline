This market will resolve to "Yes" if the International Energy Agency (IEA) requests that Germany release emergency oil stocks between market creation and the specified date, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

A qualifying request may take either of the following forms:

(i) the IEA Member countries formally agree to a new coordinated emergency release of oil stocks (an IEA collective action) within the market's timeframe, under which Germany is requested, allocated, or assigned a contribution of emergency oil stocks; or

(ii) the IEA, its Executive Director, or an authorized representative thereof officially and explicitly calls on, requests, or asks Germany or the German government to release, draw down, or make available its emergency oil stocks, including any previously committed but not yet released volumes. An explicit request addressed to a group of countries also qualifies if Germany is expressly named as one of the countries being asked to release stocks.

For the purposes of this market, "emergency oil stocks" means strategic or emergency stocks of crude oil and/or petroleum products (including diesel), such as those held by Germany's Erdölbevorratungsverband (EBV).

General calls or requests addressed to IEA members, EU member states, or European countries as a group that do not specifically name Germany will not qualify, unless Germany is assigned a contribution as described in (i). General statements supporting or suggesting possible stock releases, without an explicit request applicable to Germany, will not qualify. Discussions, consultations, or proposals for a collective action will not qualify unless they independently satisfy (ii). Requests made prior to market creation, including Germany's allocation under the IEA collective action agreed in March 2026, will not qualify. However, a new request made within the market's timeframe to implement or accelerate a previous commitment will qualify. Merely describing or reaffirming an existing commitment, without a renewed request to release stocks, will not qualify.

A qualifying request will resolve this market to "Yes" regardless of whether Germany agrees to it or actually releases any stocks. A request concerning crude oil or any qualifying petroleum product counts, regardless of whether it relates to the diesel dispute described above.

The following will not qualify: statements by persons not authorized to speak for the IEA; anonymous, unattributed, or leaked claims not confirmed by the IEA or the German government; and third-party speculation, analysis, or predictions.

Resolution will be based on official information from the IEA or the German government; however, a consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
