This market will resolve to "Yes" if at least one Western team wins at least one game in a Knockout Stage match of the 2026 League of Legends World Championship (Worlds 2026), currently scheduled for October 15 to November 14, 2026, with the Knockout Stage scheduled for November 3 to November 14, 2026. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

A Western team is a team that qualified for the tournament through the LEC (Europe / EMEA), the LCS (North America), or the CBLOL (Brazil). Teams that qualified through the LCK (South Korea), the LPL (China), or the LCP (Asia-Pacific) are not Western teams. A team that enters the Swiss Stage through the Play-In keeps the region it qualified through. A game counts if it is a game of a Knockout Stage match (quarterfinals, semifinals, or Grand Final) that is officially recorded as won by a Western team, per official tournament results. Games from the Play-In or the Swiss Stage do not count.

If at any point it becomes impossible for a Western team to win a Knockout Stage game (e.g. every Western team in the Knockout Stage is eliminated without winning a game), this market will resolve to "No".

If the Knockout Stage is not completed by December 14, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, or the tournament is canceled or postponed past that date, this market will resolve to "No", unless a Western team has already won a Knockout Stage game, in which case it will resolve to "Yes".

The resolution source for this market will be official information from the tournament organizer, Riot Games, via the LoL Esports website (https://lolesports.com). However, a consensus of credible reporting (e.g., Liquipedia at https://liquipedia.net/leagueoflegends/World_Championship/2026) may also be used.
