This market will resolve to "Yes" if the listed team achieves promotion from the LaLiga 2 to the LaLiga for the 2027-28 season at the conclusion of the current league season. Otherwise, this market will resolve to "No".

Official promotion is defined as a team clinching a place in the LaLiga for the 2027-28 season through their final standing in the LaLiga 2, via playoff victory, or by any other official league decision recognized by the LaLiga 2 and LaLiga.

If the 2026-27 LaLiga 2 season is cancelled, postponed after June 30, 2027, 11:59 PM ET, or there are no teams confirmed for promotion within that timeframe, the corresponding market will resolve to "No".

The primary resolution source will be official announcements from the LaLiga 2 and LaLiga. A consensus of credible reporting may also be used.
